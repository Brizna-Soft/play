De qué trata

"Shadow Mist Idle" is a placeable pixel-style dark loot-grinding game. Build your skills through selectable talents and a vast array of unique equipment, and vanquish the hordes of enemies that come your way.

Easily place the treasure hunter, or manually challenge and clear the level

Multiple characters bring different experiences
Fire Mage with powerful skills

Elven Archer skilled in shooting and summoning

Paladin who excels in both output and survival

Combining talents, equipment, and skills to create gameplay builds for different schools
A pure treasure-hunting experience, where you gradually become stronger through construction
