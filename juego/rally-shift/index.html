De qué trata

Rally Shift is a first person arcade rally game. Where you are tasked to escort a bomb to safety across the world while dodging a big bad alien that's trying to eliminate you.

You get to control an unruly Rally spec car as you progress through the levels you build your understanding of the car’s handling with every turn. The closer you get towards your goal the more defenses you unlock against the alien in your fight to escort the bomb to safety.

Features
• Rally across various gloomy biomes spanning 12 levels.

• Level & stage completion ranks.

• Local & Global Leaderboards

• Custom In-House Post Punk Music for every single level
