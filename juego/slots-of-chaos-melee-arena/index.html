De qué trata

A slot machine just fell into the sword fight. Pull the lever.

Slots of Chaos: Melee Arena is a physics sword brawler for up to six players. Every swing is real, every limb can come off, and every pull of the lever makes the fight worse.  Each game is packed with SLOTS OF CHAOS.

A slot machine crashes into the middle of the fight. Get to the lever, pull it, and the reels pick a new chaos rule for the whole arena. Usually a bad one.

Over 20 different ridiculous chaos modes, with multiple active at a time to make every game different, hilarious, and fun.
• EVERYTHING EXPLODES: Any spare limb you find on the ground is now a live grenade.

• STICKY ROCKETS: Throw one at someone. Enjoy the flight.

• FLATTENED: Everyone is paper-thin. Nobody is any less dangerous.

• THE PIT: Don't fall in, but feel free to shove others.

• THE GLITCH: Something is wrong with reality. Stay away from the sphere.

• BOULDER RUN: Get in the way and you're pancaked onto the stone.

• LOW GRAVITY: Jump higher, fall slower. Loose limbs drift away.

• BOOM: A ten-foot bomb just landed. The fuse is lit. Five seconds.

...and many more.

Your mouse or a controller thumb stick swings the sword.  Is it awkward?  Yes.  Is it funny? Yes.  Use the tutorial to get the mechanics down.

You've just received your DISMEMBERship card and almost anything can be cut off.  Decapitations? Check. Limb chopping? Of course.  Blood spurts? Absolutely. Can you throw corpses in the lava?  Yes, and they even explode!

4 Fighters to choose from:  KNIGHT, GOREHORN, WREAKER, SKELETON

Host an online lobby for up to six players, with no extra accounts to make, or fight up to three bots offline.

Requisitos

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10 64-bit

• Procesador: Quad-core 2.5 GHz (Intel Core i5-4590 / AMD Ryzen 3 1200)

• Memoria: 8 MB de RAM

• Gráficos: DirectX 12 or Vulkan 1.1 GPU with 2 GB VRAM (GeForce GTX 960 / Radeon RX 470 or better))

• DirectX: Versión 12

• Almacenamiento: 1 GB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: Windows-compatible audio device

• Compatibilidad con RV: OpenXR, requires two motion controllers. Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S or Quest Pro (via Quest Link, Air Link, Virtual Desktop or Steam Link). Other OpenXR PC VR headsets (Valve Index, HTC Vive) should work but are untested.

• Notas adicionales: Internet connection required for online multiplayer only.
