Escape From Streamer House

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Simuladores · Acción, Aventura, Simulación

Trapped inside a dangerous streamer's house, you must find his computer, delete a compromising private video, and escape before he catches you. Hide, solve puzzles, explore dark rooms, and survive a terrifying game of cat and mouse.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

It's 23:30. ZAPPO, a loud, sporty streamer, has a video of you, and the premiere starts at midnight.
You're trapped inside his house. Get to his PC, delete the video, and find a way out before the clock hits twelve. ZAPPO is live the whole time, and so is his chat.
A HOUSE THAT WATCHES BACK
Webcams in the studio, the kitchen and in his hand. Thousands of viewers type what they see, and ZAPPO listens. Stay out of frame, keep quiet, and use the moments when he's busy streaming.
HE STREAMS. YOU SNEAK.
ZAPPO games at his desk, cooks for chat, walks the garden live and runs errands. Every activity opens a new window. Every noise you make can close it.
THE HUNT STREAM
Make too much noise and ZAPPO finds out someone is in the house. He grabs his phone, turns on the flashlight and goes live to hunt you, room by room, with chat calling out hiding spots.
MEET BYTE
His husky waits on a chain in the garden. Once ZAPPO knows about you, he lets BYTE loose. Dog food, a ball or a treat buys you a few seconds. Closed doors stop him. Open ones don't.
EVERY NIGHT IS DIFFERENT
Items are hidden in different spots every run, and one way out might be blocked tonight. Charge the spare garage remote, unscrew the breaker panel, find the gate key, or send ZAPPO on errands with donations from his old phone.
