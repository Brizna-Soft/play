Chill Summoners

Juego de PC · Casual

Chill Summoners is an idle game celebrating Grand Summoners' 10th anniversary. It features nine relaxing arrangements of Grand Summoners music plus a Pomodoro timer to assist your productivity while working or studying. Customize the settings for your own personal stroll through Raktehelm.

De qué trata

Chill Summoners is an idle game celebrating Grand Summoners' 10th anniversary. It features nine relaxing arrangements of Grand Summoners music plus a Pomodoro timer to assist your productivity while working or studying. Customize the settings for your own personal stroll through Raktehelm.
