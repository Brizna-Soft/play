◆About NEKOPARA Sekai Connect (Neko Connect)
- Planning, main story, character design, and illustrations all handled by original creator Sayori herself!
- Nekos from all around the world appear, each with their own character story!
- High-quality 2D animation bringing these uniquely charming Nekos to life!
- Easy-to-use semi-auto battles, plus sandbox-style gameplay where you can build your own shop!
- Enjoy a rhythm game featuring original songs and music videos!
◆Introduction
It's the near future where people live alongside AI-created Nekos—beings that look human, but are just a little different...
One day, while running the patisserie La Soleil with your adorable Nekos, you receive an invitation to the Neko Festival, a global competition where Nekos and their owners vie for popularity!
Rumor has it the winner will be granted any wish by the Neko-creating AI...
Laugh, cry and grow closer to the Nekos as you uncover the mysteries of the world.
