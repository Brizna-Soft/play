De qué trata

How Deep Can You Go?
Neverending Descent is an endless dungeon crawler where every floor brings new dangers, stronger enemies, and better loot. Fight your way through increasingly challenging dungeons, defeat powerful bosses, collect rare equipment, and push deeper into the unknown.
Your journey begins in a safe zone, where you can manage your equipment, prepare for battle, and choose when to enter the dungeon.
Once inside, there's only one way forward: DOWN.
⚔️ Fight, Level Up, and Grow Stronger
Battle hordes of demons and monsters as you descend through an endless series of dungeon floors.
Earn experience by defeating enemies and level up your character. Each level allows you to improve one of three attributes:
• Attack Speed – Fire your weapon faster.

• Damage – Deal more damage to enemies.

• Health – Increase your maximum HP.

Every level requires more experience, but enemies become more rewarding as you descend.
💀 Powerful Boss Battles
Every 10 floors, a powerful boss awaits.
Defeat the boss to unlock the next section of the dungeon and earn a chance to return safely to your base.
But there's a choice to make...
Will you return with your loot, or risk everything to descend even deeper?
🎒 Collect Powerful Equipment
Discover weapons, armor, and rare equipment throughout the dungeon.
The deeper you travel, the stronger the equipment becomes.
Find increasingly powerful gear, improve your character, and prepare yourself for the challenges ahead.
🏠 Return to Safety
After defeating a boss, you can return to your safe zone with the equipment you've collected.
Here you can:
• Equip newly discovered gear.

• Manage your inventory.

• Prepare for your next dungeon run.

• Continue from your previously reached floor.
