Fished Out

Juego de PC · Aventura, Casual, Indie · Aventura, Deportes, Indie

Fish, flee, and gamble your way to quota in this 1-4 player roguelike. Fight every catch, haul it aboard, avoid demonic beasts, and make it back to the ship with just enough time to bet it all at the casino. Miss quota and the sea takes every coin. One wrong cast could cost everything.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

Sail onward. Gather your crew of up to four souls doomed to (probably) die. Race against each day's quota, catch an infinite variety of procedurally generated fish.

There are many terrors that await idle hands. Make expedient use of daylight, as all sailors know that horrible things happen after dark.

Between each island, the crew is given a chance to rest and resupply. They wont. There is a casino.

Every island is procedurally generated, so no two voyages match. Every level scales up the quota.

Set sail alone, invite up to three friends, or browse the public crews and jump aboard with strangers. Prox chat makes every call for help sound as close or as distant as it really is.

• 1-4 player online co-op with proximity voice chat

• Procedurally generated fish species, including trophy giants that take two to carry

• 4 island types and 4 kinds of weather, different every voyage

• A casino with 6 kinds of game, played by hand

• Monsters that hunt by sight and sound, from pests to things you can only run from

• Dress your pirate from a kit of heads, tops, hooks and peg legs
