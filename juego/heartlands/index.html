De qué trata

Heartlands is a multiplayer pixel sandbox where players create EVERYTHING.
Draw a sword, block, skin, instrument, NPC, or mount inside Heartlands! Press "SAVE", and your creation becomes part of a live multiplayer world where other players can immediately discover and use it. Build realms, explore community creations, and help create a world where everything comes from the players!
🎨 Built-In Pixel Editor
Design everything right inside the game! Blocks, skins, wallpapers, sound effects, ladders, liquids, emojis & more! Create animations frame by frame, paint pixels freely, and edit your creations at any time. Place creations into the world to design the game world with other players!

💬 Make Your Own Skins!
Make and wear your own skins. Show off your style and creativity — or your sense of humor — with several skin states to choose from: Stand, Run, Jump, Double Jump, Crouch, Dash, Wall Slide & More!

💬 Online Multiplayer
Chat, build, and explore together in a shared online world. All creations are live — what one player builds, others can instantly see.

🗺 Exploration & Warps
Set personal warps to quickly return to your favorite builds or hidden corners of the world.

🪦Adventure Awaits
Explore a world that is as safe or deadly as you want! Every realm is a world created by the player and could be filled with unexpected surprises!

💡Lighting Mechanics
Light up your world with Light Creations! Players create the graphic, light colors and intensity!

🎸 Instruments
Create your own instruments with sound effects you design for other players to use and enjoy!

🔉 Sound Editor
Import and manage your own sound effects! Change your skins jump and walk sounds to make it truly unique.
