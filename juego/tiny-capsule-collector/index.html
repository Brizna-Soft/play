Tiny Capsule Collector

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

Run a cozy capsule shop at the bottom of your screen. Attract adorable animal customers, collect charming capsule toys, create and decorate a relaxing, ambient world. This desktop companion brings you small moments of joy making your desktop a place you’d rather be. All hand-crafted, no AI used.

De qué trata

Run a cozy capsule shop at the bottom of your screen
Why wait until after work to enjoy some cozy gaming? Tiny Capsule Collector is a relaxing idle game, where you manage your own tiny Gachapon shop at the bottom of your screen. It plays itself while you work, and quietly lives alongside your day, while you work, study, just chat or simply unwind. It is your ambient desktop companion making your desktop a place you'd rather be.

Welcome adorable animal customers
Your shop attracts a steady flow of cute animal visitors, each bringing life and charm to your world creating a relaxing ambient work environment.
• Discover a growing cast of endearing creatures

• Turn your screen into a Tokyo daydream

• Enjoy a lively, ever-changing ambient scenery

Collect, complete, and display your collectable toys
Every visit adds to your collection. Fill your collector’s journal and enjoy your favorite finds directly on your screen.
• Unlock a wide variety of cute collectibles and adorable animal customers

• Complete sets in a satisfying collecting experience

• Display your collection as part of your world

Build and decorate your cozy shop
Create your own relaxing space with vending machines, decorations, and layouts that reflect your style.
• Place and manage Gachapon machines

• Customize your shop with a colorful, stylized 2D aesthetic

• Expand your tiny business at your own pace

A relaxing, low-focus high-reward idle game
Built for fans of desktop companions, casual job simulations, and cozy management, Tiny Capsule Collector is designed to be enjoyed without pressure.
