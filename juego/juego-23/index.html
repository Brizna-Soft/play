De qué trata

Just one more swirl.
Move the cone and build your soft serve higher and higher. Start with a wide base, or go thin to chase a taller tower. The more ambitious you get, the more it wobbles—and it might all come tumbling down.

You have 90 seconds. If it looks too risky, you can finish early. The height of a successfully finished soft serve becomes your record.Keep your creations
Customize your cream colors, cone, sauces, and decorations. Meet the unlock conditions to discover new looks. Your gallery keeps your 10 most recent creations, and you can save up to 10 favorites.Controls
Move the cone with your mouse. Hold the left mouse button to swirl, and press Space to finish. Start with a solo game and challenge your own best record.

The game interface is available in Japanese only.
