My Little Cafe

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

Build your own cozy café in this pixel-art management game. Create drinks and discover hidden recipes, hire staff and assign their roles, and balance customer satisfaction with running costs. Decorate with furniture and plants, then expand your little shop with an outdoor terrace.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

A little shop. A café of your own.

A few tables, a coffee machine, and a space waiting for your ideas. What kind of café will you create?
My Little Cafe is a cozy, single-player management game with charming pixel art. Make the space your own, develop drinks, and welcome customers as you build the everyday life of your little shop.
Create a place worth staying for

Wooden tables, comfortable chairs, warm lighting, and a little greenery. Mix furniture, wallpaper, and flooring to give your café its own personality.
Think about the paths your customers and staff take, too. Finding a layout that looks good and works well is part of the fun.
Find your café’s signature flavor

Combine coffee beans, milk, fruit, and flavorings to develop new drinks. Follow hints from customers and discover hidden recipes along the way.
Not every combination will succeed. Explore which ingredients work together, put your creations on the menu, and find the flavors that make your café special.
Build a team. Welcome your regulars.

Hire staff, assign counter and serving roles, and help your café owner and team improve through their daily work. As business picks up, your staffing and equipment choices matter more.
Learn what different customers enjoy, keep an eye on satisfaction, and give them a reason to return.
Grow into a neighborhood favorite
Review the day’s finances, plan your next investment, and earn rewards through requests and goals. Expand your shop, decorate an outdoor terrace, and compete in café contests for taste and atmosphere.
You can keep running your café after the ending. Create more drinks, try a new look, and keep making the space your own.
