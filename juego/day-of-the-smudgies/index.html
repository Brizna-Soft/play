Day of the Smudgies

Juego de PC · Aventura, Casual, Indie · Aventura, Indie

A sponge drank the wrong puddle. Now he's the Blot, and he's eating your verbs. Bounce through 1826, 2226 and yesterday at 12:46 AM to un-ink him in this free, fully voiced point-and-click comedy.

Tráiler

De qué trata

A sponge drank the wrong puddle.
It's 12:47 AM behind Tumble's Comics & Copy. Sludgie spots a shiny puddle of Overprint Ink, the stuff that leaks out of the Professor's basement. Smudge says do NOT touch it. Sludgie has a tiny little soak.

Now he's the Blot. He's hungry for stories, he's eating the verbs right off your screen, and he's talking to you. Yes, you. The one holding the mouse.

One copier. Three centuries.
Willis is jammed in the present. The Dude got printed 200 years ago. Verny got printed 200 years ahead. Same building, three eras: 1826, where Cornelius Tumble is busy inventing everything, 2226, where the Big Blot rules from Inkwell Tower, and yesterday at 12:46 AM, one minute before the first sip.

Swap items across time through the copier and fix it before every page goes black.

Features
• Classic 9-verb point-and-click. Give, Pick up, Use, Open, Look at, Push, Close, Talk to, Pull. Until the Blot eats them.

• Fully voiced, with subtitles

• Three playable heroes across 1826, 2226 and yesterday

• Four acts of comic-book chaos, about an hour long

• Smudge builds hero costumes out of household junk. Colander included.

• A secret arcade lounge with two playable arcade games. Find the tokens.

• Theater mode to rewatch every cutscene you've unlocked

• End credits with Verny's music video, Verny's Cut

• Free. No ads, no in-app purchases.

A Saturday-morning cartoon crossed with a 90s adventure game. Big heart, dumb jokes, and characters who know they're in a comic.
