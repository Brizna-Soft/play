Shattered Void Online

Juego de PC · Acción, Simuladores, Free to Play · Acción, Simulación

One war, three ways to fight it. Take the bridge of a warship in 5v5 fleet battles, board an enemy outpost and fight for it on foot, or command a whole fleet across six star systems. Vanguard against Ironsworn: pick your side and get on the line.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

One war. Three ways in. Shattered Void is Vanguard against Ironsworn, fought from three places at once: the bridge of a warship, the deck of a boarded outpost, and the command table. Pick your side on the main menu and queue. Same war, same servers, every match moves the front.CAPTAIN — Take the bridge
One warship, and she's yours. Five a side in Zone Capture: hold the sectors while your shields peel away, charge the railgun and wait for the shot, then jump across the map and drop out of warp on top of the fight the moment it turns. First to eight hundred.

• Six warships, six ways to fight. Hold the line in a Dreadnought, cloak behind it in an Interceptor, keep everyone alive in a Frigate, or sow the lanes with mines and leave them a turret. Every ship is yours from your first match.

• Sectors are territory. A sector pays only while it is linked home. Cut the supply line, shoot down the convoys, and their score stops.

• A captain in your ear. Pick a captain and the fight comes in their voice: the sector you just lost, the ally going down, the drive coming online.

• The Arena. Ten pilots, no allies, one life each, in a ring of corrosive fog that closes to nothing.

ASSAULT — No station falls from the outside
So you go in. Your pod latches onto the outpost's hull, the hatch blows, and five marines fight five on foot for the flags inside. Kick down doors up close as a Breacher, bend the station's power into the fight as a Fieldwright, or build machines that fight beside you as a Fabricator. Take it room by room.

Builds for each class, the kit you grab first and how you look all live in the Armoury. Fight as a class and it remembers: VETERAN, then MASTER, and looks you can't buy anywhere.COMMAND — Move the whole war
Five commanders a side across six star systems.
