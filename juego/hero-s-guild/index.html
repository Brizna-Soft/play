Hero's Guild

Juego de PC · Indie, Rol, Simuladores · Indie, Rol (RPG), Simulación

Your heroes are people. They form bonds, take grudges, fall in love, and die permanently. Run a guild of brilliant, emotionally unstable adventurers — and watch their story write itself.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Your heroes are not expendable. They have names. They have friends. They have lovers. They have opinions about their equipment. And when they die—permanently—your guild will never quite be the same.

Hero's Guild is a guild management roguelike. Recruit heroes from the tavern, train them, argue with them about party composition, send them into dungeons that are actively trying to kill them, and watch as their lives become a story you didn't write and can't entirely control. The heroes have their own feelings about everything. They are not always helpful feelings.

HEROES WHO ARE, UNFORTUNATELY, PEOPLE

Every hero arrives with a personality. Not a stat page pretending to be a personality—an actual disposition that determines whether they'll make friends, start fights, fall in love with the worst possible candidate, or quietly hold a grudge for forty in-game days before exploding at a dinner party.

Relationships develop across many tiers, from outright hostility to the kind of devotion that produces either legendary teamwork or catastrophic grief when something goes wrong. Heroes argue. They reconcile. They fall in love, get married at your chapel, and name their swords after each other. Or they develop a blood feud that makes party composition a diplomatic negotiation.

When mood breaks, so do heroes. Push them too hard and they'll desert, go berserk, confess secrets that unravel friendships, or simply take a personal day at the worst possible time. The tavern offers drinks, feasts, gambling nights, and a bard—all valid therapeutic approaches. None of them guaranteed.THEY WANT THINGS, AND THEY DON'T TELL YOU

Every hero is quietly saving for something. A better cloak. A small house. A name that outlasts them.
