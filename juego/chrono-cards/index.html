Chrono Cards

Juego de PC · Estrategia · Estrategia

Chrono Cards is a fast-paced, multiplayer competitive card game where you have the power to bend time itself! Build history-spanning decks and construct explosive, timeline-shattering combos. Exploit endless synergies to shape the timeline in your favor and send your opponent back to the stone age.

De qué trata

Chrono Cards adds a whole new dimension to competitive card gaming...literally. Maneuver your cards around the ever-shifting timeline to outwit and outflank your opponent, assemble unique decks from the hundreds of cards at your disposal, and enjoy it all from the very beginning: All cards are available from the moment you launch. It's quick enough to pick up in an instant, and deep enough to keep you coming back for all eternity.

Prove your mastery as the ultimate time traveler against your friends, or chase even higher glory in online ranked play. Stay a step ahead of your opponent by playing cards to the future to set up later turns, all while countering their best-laid plans with perfect positioning. Or, forge your own destiny and manipulate time itself to your advantage: Like your board state from 3 turns ago? Rewind time and force your opponent to relive that pain.

Summon cards from past and future alike to annihilate any rival time traveler that dares challenge you. With hundreds of cards available to you, the strategies are limitless. Overwhelm your opponent with an endless army of Medieval peasants. Amass a fortune of cards and mana with an unstoppable engine of assembly lines and steam trains. Sacrifice your weak, tasty minions to summon increasingly powerful (and hungry) dinosaurs. Or discover entirely new synergies, never before seen in any timeline, that will reduce your enemy to a footnote in the history books.

Chrono Cards gives you all the cards right away, forever. No need to slog through endless grinding, pay-to-win schemes and premium currencies: matches are a test of your skill, not your bank account. Spend your time cooking up the strategy that will define the meta, not unlocking last year's mythic rare.
