PRINCESS VANGUARD

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Rol · Acción, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Control five elemental princesses, unleash spectacular Special Skills, and battle massive enemy hordes in this fast-paced 15-minute 2D top-down action RPG. Upgrade your weapons, defeat powerful bosses, and save the world!

De qué trata

Save the World with Five Elemental Princesses
Take control of five elemental princesses, cut through massive hordes of enemies, and challenge the Demon Army!
Rescue the princesses imprisoned by the Demon Army and embark on an epic adventure to save the world alongside the five elemental heroines.
Princess Vanguard is a 15-minute 2D top-down action RPG featuring fast-paced combat, intense boss battles, and satisfying character progression.
Strengthen your weapons and equipment as you battle overwhelming enemy hordes!

Rescue the Captured Princesses and Reclaim the Power of the Five Elements!
Unique Combat Styles for Every Element
Each of the five princesses possesses a unique elemental affinity and fighting style.
Master their exclusive weapons, normal attacks, weapon skills, and devastating Special Skills to crush the Demon Army!
🔥 Fire – A high-damage melee attacker that overwhelms enemies.
❄️ Water – A mid-range fighter who controls the battlefield with ice magic.
🪨 Earth – A durable powerhouse with exceptional defense and strength.
⚡ Lightning – A lightning-fast attacker who outmaneuvers enemies.
🌪️ Wind – A long-range archer who dominates the battlefield from afar.
Experiment with different weapons and abilities to discover your own unique playstyle.

Epic Boss Battles
A powerful boss awaits at the end of every stage.
Learn each boss's attack patterns, dodge deadly assaults, counterattack, and unleash devastating Special Skills to claim victory!

Buy and Equip Weapons and Armor
Starting in Chapter 2, visit the Shopping District in the Royal Capital to spend the gold you earn in battle. Buy weapons at the Blacksmith and armor at the Tailor, then equip your purchases via Change Equipment in the Central Plaza. Shop stock expands as you progress through the story.
