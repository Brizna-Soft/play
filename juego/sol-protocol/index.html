Sol Protocol

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Simuladores · Acción, Indie, Simulación

Sol Protocol is a dynamic VR Co-Op roguelike where you team up and physically pilot a spaceship through dangerous, procedurally generated stellar systems. Manage your spaceship, man the turrets, explore abandoned stations on foot, and make strategic decisions as you push deeper into the deep space.

De qué trata

GATHER YOUR TEAM
Whether solo, duo, or with a full crew of three, you'll have to adapt dynamically to survive the galaxy of Sol Protocol. Want to navigate through an asteroid field? Become a Pilot. Ever dreamt of shooting down foes with your turrets? Gunner is perfect. Or would you rather strategically plan your next move and manage ship’s shielding system? Welcome aboard, Captain. Each role has a distinct set of tasks to perform and handy tools to utilize throughout the journey.
PLAN YOUR BATTLES
Enemy spaceships are roaming the deep space, looking to destroy and loot your ship. You'll need to use different weapons scavenged across space and strategize based on your available resources to deal with intense randomized encounters and collect your loot afterward. Choose your battles wisely, warping away is sometimes the only way to avoid total annihilation.
MAINTAIN YOUR SHIP
Without your ship, your crew is lost! Do everything you can to protect it! Manually repair damaged components by replacing them with parts looted from abandoned bases and vessels. Put out fires (literal and figurative) to take care of what you can't replace. The ship's various features can be individually upgraded to become even sturdier or to gain new and powerful traits to ensure you survive.

GATHER YOUR RESOURCES
Resources are sparse, but vital both for the maintenance of your ship as well as future upgrades. Explore the deepest corners of the cosmos to find abandoned space stations and lost vessels to gather the necessary materials. Be careful, though - loot is rarely gathered easily. Traps and puzzles will hide the best loot, enemies will patrol key locations, and you'll be working against the ticking clock of your own oxygen supplies...
