Rice & Ink: Family Chronicle

Juego de PC · Aventura, Rol, Simuladores · Aventura, Estrategia, Rol (RPG), Simulación

A family simulation set in China, 1500–1850, in the canal and rice country around Suzhou: land, exams, shops, courts, lineage feuds, combat. Your family may live through the late Ming, the Manchu conquest and the Opium War — or it may not. It does not have to be honest.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

China, 1500, the Ming dynasty. Your family lives outside Suzhou on the lower Yangtze, near today's Shanghai: canals, rice paddies, silk, the richest region in China at the time. The family has an estate, land and an ancestral hall. The game runs to 1850, through the Manchu conquest and the Opium War. You can also start later: the world lives through the years before your start without you.
The family
The head of the family and his wife, sons and daughters-in-law, servants, stewards, retainers. People age and die, and the heir becomes the head. Thousands of households live across the county around you: they grow rice, sit the exams, go bankrupt and go to court — sometimes against you.
Rice
Ploughing, transplanting, watering with a treadle pump, reaping, threshing — month by month. Harvests fail. Grain spoils in the granary. Taxes are paid in grain and silver, and the county office counts the surcharge its own way.
The exams
A son is prepared for the exams for years. In the exam hall itself, you answer the questions: interpreting the theme, the policy essay, the eight-legged essay, the poem, the fair copy. A degree brings the family respect and a path to office. Office means evaluations, lawsuits, disasters and letters from the county office.
Money
Shops, mills, weaving workshops, brokers. Stewards skim the takings. Clerks and officials at the county office take bribes, and you will have to pay them.
The lineage
An ancestral hall, a lineage fund, children betrothed young, funerals by Zhu Xi's Family Rituals. Servants are bought. A feud with the neighbouring lineage can end in a brawl at the graves, and a captive will stand in your courtyard until he is ransomed.
