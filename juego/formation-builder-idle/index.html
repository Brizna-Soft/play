Formation Builder Idle

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Rol · Estrategia, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Every hero changes the heroes beside it. Arrange 46+ heroes on a grid, chain their abilities, and find the layout that multiplies your damage. Then prestige across 21 worlds, fight with mounts, and run missions, mines and a workshop while you're away.

De qué trata

Every hero changes the heroes beside it
Formation Builder Idle is an idle RPG about placement. Your heroes fight on their own, but where you put them decides how hard they hit. Move one hero by a single slot and your damage can change by orders of magnitude.

A puzzle made of 46 heroes
Every ability reads the grid. Some heroes buff their neighbors, some buff a whole column, and some care about traits or the entire formation.
• Lorn buffs every neighbor, and gets stronger for each neighbor and each different trait among them.

• Perrin copies the buffs aimed at him and passes them to the column in front.

• Nerissa gets stronger the more positional buffs point at her.

• Falgrim counts the traits he shares with his neighbors, and his bonus grows with the square of that count.

With 17 traits, gear on every hero and a scarf stat that strengthens that hero's ability, the best layout keeps changing as your roster grows. Save several formations per world and switch between them.

Left-click levels a hero. Right-click buys max.

Prestige across 21 worlds
Push until the waves stop you, then prestige. Your gold becomes talent points to spend across 99+ talents, and each world pays out its own World Essence. There are 125 stages to clear, and World Mastery rewards you for going back to earlier worlds with a stronger team.

Mounts and the Tower of Domination
There's a second combat game inside the first. Collect 12 mounts, level them up, give them hero riders and build teams around 6 terrain elements for a 100-stage mount campaign. Then take them into the Tower of Domination, a weekly 1v1 climb where leaders don't count and only mount stats, abilities and elements decide the fight.

Heroes who aren't fighting still work
• Missions. Send spare heroes on timed missions.
