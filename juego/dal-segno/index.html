De qué trata

In this romantic, slice-of-life visual novel, you take the role of a transfer student newly admitted to Kazana Academy, a school set on an island created to be an everlasting summer paradise. Yet something seems off with the Paradise System run by the AI girl you meet on your first day.

As you attend classes and grow closer to your classmates, you'll learn more about their personal stories, find love and friendships, and uncover what each girl really needs to find true happiness.

This story takes place on Kazana Island, an island created to be an everlasting summer paradise, with sparkling beaches, perfect weather, and everything the residents could need.

Your choices will lead you down many paths, including each heroine's story and one unlockable ending.
