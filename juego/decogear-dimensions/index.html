De qué trata

A single player beat 'em up heavily inspired by tightly designed arcade classics such as Final Fight. With a dash of extra sauce like destructible terrain & a gallery shooter inspired gun character. Designed primarily for beat 'em up veterans but everyone who can tolerate a steep learning curve & a bit of frustration is welcome!
FEATURES
• Designed to kill you! The game will not let you rest or relax, if you don't aggressively manage enemy crowds you will be overwhelmed in no time.

• Short length & brisk pace! Runs take ~30 minutes and are densely packed by unique encounters with no redundant fights or gimmicks.

• Enemy expression! 25 nasty, playful enemy types. All with their own unique behavioral quirks & sets of attacks.

• Flatter difficulty curve! No need to wait until the halfway point for the game to start heating up, you'll be forced to lock in right away.

• 3 completely unique characters! Prepare to radically re-learn the game when switching characters.

• Ultra mode! Crazy difficulty mode meant to challenge your beat 'em up skills & your luck to the max. Not even remotely fair.
