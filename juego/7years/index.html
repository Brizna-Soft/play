7Years

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Estrategia, Indie, Terror

Offering a single-player story mode, three unique multiplayer game modes, and even the ability to play as the demons, 7Years is a very different and challenging horror game. With random spawns of demonic enemies, mini games, items, puzzles, and a feline protector, each round is its own story.

De qué trata

7Years is a unique & challenging non-linear, horror adventure game made by a first-time developer and his son. 7Years offers a single-player story mode and THREE unique multiplayer game modes that offer both PvE and PvP experiences, with tons of additional content already in production.
SINGLE-PLAYER STORY MODE
• Traverse your now-possessed mountainside neighborhood that contains 4 homes, an elementary school, a playground and a power station

• Find and beat demon challenges

• Befriend the neighborhood feline guardian

• Find a way to expel the demons back to Hell

MULTIPLAYER COOP
• Up to 4 players

• A player can now fight on the side of evil, transforming into the alpha demon, CLOAK, and using his powerful abilities to stalk the human players, force hunts from subordinate demons and even call on the mysterious Wraith to continue his chase inside of structures!

PORTAL HUNT
• Up to 4 players can choose to work together or against each other

• Locate the cursed items needed to open hidden portals scattered randomly around the neighborhood

• Evade the gradually growing army of demon enemies

• Portal Hunt also includes playable Cloak!

SURVIVE THE HORDE
• Up to 4 players are trapped inside of the elementary school as Hell unleashes hundreds of its demon foot soldiers

• Fend off 5 increasingly dangerous waves of enemies, with each of 5 waves ending in a climatic hunt
