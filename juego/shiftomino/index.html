Shiftomino

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie

A puzzle platformer where you push your block body against other blocks to shift into them. Use your "SHIFT" ability to make your way through each stage! The protagonist has fallen from their home in the sky onto this planet. Keep moving forward, and maybe you'll find a way back to the moon someday.

De qué trata

Shift your way forward!
Shiftomino is a puzzle platformer where you push your block body against other blocks to shift your soul into them and make your way through each stage.

Update (Nov. 2): This is a compact game that takes about 1 hour to complete if everything goes smoothly, or 2–3 hours for most players.
Shift Your Way Onward

Use your special ability, "Shift," to push through each stage. Put your brain to work, and someday you'll find your way back to your home floating in the sky!
Simple Controls

Simple controls you can play with just the D-pad. Enjoy one-of-a-kind puzzles built entirely around "Shift."
Retry as Often as You Like
Fall into lava as many times as you want. There's no penalty, and no time limit either, so take it at your own pace.

Collect the small gems scattered across the stages. Each gem lets you undo one move.
