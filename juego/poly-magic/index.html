De qué trata

POLY MAGIC
Poly Magic Is a fast-paced action roguelike about powerful mages who must stand against endless waves of monsters.

Each run lasts 12 minutes. The enemies grow thicker, the danger rises, and the upgrades you choose turn a novice sorcerer into a devastating magical force. Gather experience, unlock new spells, strengthen them, and build your own combination of abilities.

Choose your mage. Six heroes offer different ways to play. Build a unique magical loadout. Pick new spells when you level up, then upgrade them. Combine abilities of different types — from volleys and beams to auras and a destructive rain of magic.

Fight across six dangerous biomes. At the end of each trial, a boss with unique attacks is waiting.
Grow stronger after every run. Collect gold and buy permanent upgrades for all heroes.

Play together
Team up with a friend in online co-op for two. Pick different heroes, combine their strengths, and survive the onslaught side by side.

Features
• Intense 12-minute runs.

• 6 heroes with unique stats and schools of magic.

• 6 biomes with special events and dangerous zones.

• A wide range of spells, upgrades, and combinations.

• Bosses with unique mechanics.

• Permanent meta-progression bought with gold.

• Single player and online co-op for two.

A small team with a big dream
Lucky Compass is a team of just two people, and POLY MAGIC is our first game. The game is in Early Access. We plan to keep improving it with feedback from the community. Join our Discord channel.

If you like the idea of POLY MAGIC, please add the game to your Steam wishlist. For a small team, that support means a great deal: it helps us keep developing the game and make it even better.

Thank you for setting out on this adventure with us.
