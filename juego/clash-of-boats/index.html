De qué trata

The sea is only half the arena.

In Clash of Boats: Action your speedboat races, jumps and fights on the water, then opens its wings and takes the battle into the sky. Up to four players, real physics and a crown that everyone wants.

• FLY: take off from a ramp or a wave, roll, loop and dive. Your boat is a plane whenever you want it to be.

• FIGHT: homing missiles, mines, triple shots, traps, shields and nitro, on the water and in the air.

• PICK YOUR BOAT: seven boats, each with its own ability: a teleport dash, a kamikaze dive, a ground slam and more.

• PLAY TOGETHER: party up with friends and play free-for-all, 2v2 or missiles only.

• FLY THE MUSIC: BEAT FLIGHT turns the soundtrack into the enemy. Its drums fire the missiles.

• STYLE IT: the Boathouse lets you paint, finish and decorate your boat, down to its afterburner's flame.

• TRAIN: a shelf of drills with medals takes you from your first take-off to your first dogfight win.

Easy to start, hard to put down. Watch your back if you are the king.
