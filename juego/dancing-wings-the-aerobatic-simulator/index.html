Dancing Wings - The Aerobatic Simulator

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Simuladores · Acción, Indie, Simulación

Team aerobatics flight simulator - do it solo. Fly, save the replay, fly in overlay mode, save the new replay, and repeat it up to six aircrafts. Realistic aerodynamics and landscape. Step-by-step training. Share your replay online.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Flight simulator dedicated to team aerobatics -- no gun, no missile.
YOU CAN DO IT SOLO
You don't need anyone else. You can do it solo with the replay overlay function. First, you fly as Lead, and save your replay data. Second, you enter the replay overlay mode, fly as Wingman together with Lead that you previously played as, and save the new replay -- now you have a replay of two aircrafts. You can overlay up to 6 ships.
TRAINING FROM BASIC TO ADVANCED
This game provides various trainings from basic to advanced. The basic level includes level flight, ascend/descend, turn, takeoff and landing. The advanced level teaches you how to fly in a formation.
DOWNLOAD/UPLOAD REPLAY OVER INTERNET
Replay data can be downloaded from or uploaded to the server. You can join (overlay) the team performance done by someone else in the world, or vice versa.
REALISTIC AERODYNAMICS
The game employs a realistic aerodynamic simulation of lift, gravity, thrust and drag.
REALISTIC LANDSCAPE
You'll also enjoy the landscape modeled from actual height data of the northeast region in Japan, around Higashi-matsushima city.
Please Read Before Purchasing
• The only aircraft featured is the T-4 Blue Impulse.

• The only map is the Matsushima Air Base area.

• This is not an officially licensed product of the Japan Air Self-Defense Force (JASDF).

Demo Limitations
• Formations are limited to 3 aircraft.

• Each flight is limited to 5 minutes.

• #DemoVer is automatically added to the beginning of every replay description.

• Only part of CURRICULUM mode is available.

• Replay uploads are limited to 3 per day.
