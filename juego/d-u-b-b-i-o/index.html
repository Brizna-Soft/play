D.U.B.B.I.O

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie, Terror

Become D.U.B.B.I.O, a counter-terror AI deployed to the city of Sowrat. Juggle simultaneous hacking tasks to interfere with terrorists, gather the clues they leave behind, and deduce where they will strike and how they are armed. But this mission is only the surface of the operation.

De qué trata

Sowrat — a city where terror strikes every single day.
With every counter-terror team having failed, an artificial intelligence is brought in: D.U.B.B.I.O.

You are D.U.B.B.I.O — the Digital Unit for Behavioral Breach Inquiry & Observation — operating under Security Bureau operator Shiro.
Your job: interfere with terrorists, track them down, and identify where the attack will happen and how they are armed before it is too late.
But this mission is only the surface. The deeper you go, the more you learn about another way to stop the terror at its source.

Interference
Hacking tasks open across multiple screens at once. Recover emails, sniff packets, crack PIN codes, match fingerprints, capture CCTV footage, take over elevators — more than 20 kinds of tasks pile up against the clock. Every successful interference delays the attack and earns you chances to track.

Tracking
Spend your tracking chances to collect clues about the target region and the terrorist's weapon. Cross-check your clue log, eliminate suspects one by one, and submit your final judgment.

Inspection
When the day's operation ends, take direct control of a maintenance robot and explore the facility to carry out its tasks. Along the way, the hidden story of D.U.B.B.I.O, the Security Bureau, and Sowrat slowly comes to light.
