SICRUN

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Casual · Acción, Aventura, Terror

A first-person arcade horror game about greed. Every level's exit costs SicCoins, and the goal is to buy your way out as fast as possible. Collect coins in the dark while something hunts you, gamble to multiply them, and spend them on upgrades and companions. High risk, high reward.

De qué trata

SICRUN
• SICRUN is a first-person arcade horror game about greed, bad luck, and something in the dark that already knows where you are.

• Five levels stand between you and the way out, and every exit costs SicCoins. Grab coins as you explore, and stay out of reach of whatever is hunting you. Gamble them for a chance to multiply your stack, spend them on upgrades and companions, and pay your way to the next level. The goal is to get through all five as fast as you can.
SICCOINS

• SicCoins are scattered through every level, and the maze keeps refilling them. Earn your way out the slow way, running the halls and filling your pockets while the thing behind you loses your scent. Or put everything on a single roll and find out in two seconds whether this run was worth anything.

• Spend your SicCoins on upgrades. Move faster. Drag coins toward you from across the room. Double what every pickup is worth. Make the maze refill quicker so there's always something worth chasing.

GAMBLING
• Every level has its own gambling game. Walk up to the table, name your stake and make your call. Win and your stake multiplies. Lose and it's gone. There is no safe bet. There's only the bet you can afford and the one you make anyway.

ENEMIES
• Every level has at least one thing hunting you, and it doesn't walk a fixed route hoping to bump into you. It comes. It remembers where it last saw you, and it checks. Learn the shortcuts, the dead ends and the sightlines and you can shake it off fast. Get cornered holding a full run's worth of coins and you lose every one of them. You keep your upgrades and start again from the furthest level you've reached.
