De qué trata

“Lestvitsa” is an isometric point-and-click detective game that continues the tradition of Disco-like games, in which dialogue is the main gameplay element.
This is the beginning of a saga about Alexei and the town of Sinitsk in the Russian North, where the past is inseparable from the present. You will play through the introductory part of the investigation and get acquainted with the world, some of the characters, and the mechanics. The next part of the saga about a the town that remembers more than its inhabitants do is coming in 2027.
• INVESTIGATE. Look for clues, talk to townspeople, make difficult decisions, and deal with their consequences. Uncover ancient secrets and find out whose interests lie behind the theft of the city relic.
• STEP THROUGH TIME. Use your gift and step through time. Take part in the events of the distant past and influence the present.
• FIND YOUR OWN WAY TO ACT. Make your playthrough unique with a flexible skill system. Notice important details, choose the right words, or simply solve the problem with brute force.
• ENGAGE IN INNER STRUGGLE. Let Virtues and Passions intervene in your conversations, argue with each other, and comment on your thoughts. Which side will you choose?
• GROW THE TREE OF THE SOUL. Breathe life into the Tree of the Soul by filling it with thoughts. Cultivate virtues, nourish your passions, or maintain balance—it’s all in your hands.
