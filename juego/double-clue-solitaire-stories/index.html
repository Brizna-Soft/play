De qué trata

The business tycoon Nicholas McCullen is dead. It looks like suicide but the Mayor wants no loose ends.

Work along with the crack team headed by Det. Carrie Tucker to investigate this case.. Uncover clues, interrogate the suspects and discuss the case with the team to reveal what really happened. Play the solitaire card mystery in three different challenging game play modes as you solve the crime and nab the culprit.
