Bumper Bout

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual, Indie · Acción, Indie

A PvP pinball hero fighter! Battle friends or the CPU, local or online across a mix of quirky and colorful arenas each bursting with hazards and surprises. Master every character’s unique ultimate move to turn the tides at the last moment!

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

A world first competitive pinball hero battler where two players directly compete on a shared playfield with shared pinballs. Rack up points to earn Attack Balls that can only damage your opponent, or save them up to launch a powerful Super Ball which triggers your character's ultimate.
Bumper Bout is easy to understand at a glance, with plenty of depth to sink your teeth in to for more competitive players.

Featuring 20+ stages at launch, each offering a unique mechanic to shake up the formula and give players something else to discover on top of the core mechanics.

Bumper Bout features a cast of colorful characters, each with their own dedicated ultimate power. Pick your favorite and experiment with how these powers match up against each other or combine with different stage mechanics.

• Single player modes including Arcade and Survival

• Local multiplayer (2 player)

• Online multiplayer

• Training mode
