Card Heroes: Rune Deck

Juego de PC · Indie, Rol, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Fantasy turn-based card battler. Time your charges, chain five-element counters and rage-fueled ultimates. Conquer 15 dungeons across three difficulty tiers, brave the trial tower, duel a ranked arena, collect 541 cards and become the Rune Master.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Card Heroes: Rune Deck is a fantasy turn-based card battler. If you enjoy card battle games, 541 cards and a five-element battlefield await.
Lead your heroes into battle: an 8-card deck, charge timers, and rage-fueled ultimates — the cards play themselves out, victory belongs to your math.

Core Gameplay
• 8-card cycling deck: 4 cards in hand + 4 in the cycle pool. Casting a card starts its charge countdown — at zero it auto-fires and a fresh card is drawn from the pool

• Charge mind games: every card charges at a different speed. Read the enemy's rhythm and seize the tempo

• Rage ultimates: rage builds dynamically from the damage you actually deal — multi-hits, AoE, crits and elemental counters all count. Harder hits, faster meter; fire it manually when full to turn the tide

• Card progression: every card grows on two tracks — levels boost damage, shields and HP; stars raise crit rate and effect trigger chances. Heroes level up too, with HP and rage capacity scaling along

• Five-element counter loop: Fire → Earth → Wind → Arcane → Water → Fire. Hit a weakness for +50% damage; matching elements grant immunity to status effects

• Row splash: the main target takes 100% damage while adjacent enemies in the same row take 50% each — counters and crits are rolled independently on every hit

• Deck building: up to 2 copies of the same card. Mix and match 541 cards into your build

• Dungeon conquest: 15 dungeons (11 themed + 4 trials) across Normal / Elite / Epic difficulties, 10 stages each — 320 stages in total, from the storms of Verdant Forest to the endgame of the Lost Ruins

• Trial Tower (Gauntlet): 24 grueling stages across 6 distinct floors. Your heroes' HP and death states persist between stages—push on wounded or fall for good.
