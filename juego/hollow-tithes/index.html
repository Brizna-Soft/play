De qué trata

Hollow Tithes is a dark fantasy, party-based RPG that blends first-person dungeon exploration with turn-based combat. Assemble four adventurers, descend beneath Cinderwatch, and fight your way through six dungeons filled with secrets, treasure, and corrupted keepers. Defeat the dungeon’s main boss to open the way home—and bring back the spoils that will rebuild your refuge.
Build your company
Unlock 13 character classes and choose the four heroes who will face the depths together. Develop their skills, improve their equipment, and discover combinations that turn a desperate encounter into a hard-earned victory.
Make every turn count
Read enemy intentions, disrupt dangerous abilities, and decide when to attack, defend, heal, or spend your dwindling supplies. Equipment and character stats influence your chances of landing a blow. Prepare for poison, bleeding, and fallen allies before a promising expedition becomes a disaster.
Explore beyond the obvious
Navigate changing dungeon layouts, solve puzzles, open hidden compartments, and uncover rare encounters with special rewards. Watch your torchlight and the rising alarm as you search. Every detour offers an opportunity—and another demand on your resources.
Earn your way home
The way out opens when the main boss falls. Push toward the keeper, weigh the risks of further exploration, and return with treasure, crafting materials, and equipment to strengthen your company.
Restore Cinderwatch
Rebuild the town’s services with the materials you recover. Train your heroes, purchase and forge equipment, identify mysterious finds, enchant trusted gear, and prepare supplies for your next descent. Complete contracts and follow a story that leads through all six dungeons toward the final encounter.
