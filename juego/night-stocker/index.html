Night Stocker

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

Immerse yourself in the neon nostalgia of the 90s! Nightstoker is a cozy organization game where you task yourself with bringing order to a chaotic video and music rental store. Sort through thousands of tapes and listen to retro hits. The pure joy of a perfectly organized shelf.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

DESCRIPTION
Night Stocker is a fun organization game where you calmly tidy up a huge, cluttered retro media store. Arrange tapes, books, and board games on shelves with different themes. Experience the satisfaction of transforming chaos into a perfectly organized collection.

COFFEE MUSIC BREAK
Take a break from sorting and listen to music in the store. Grab a cup of coffee and enjoy the atmosphere, while the cute cat Monyo keeps you company from time to time. You can even try picking him up.

THE MOMENT WHEN CHAOS BECOMES ORDER
Each completed section brings visible changes to the store. Watch the clutter disappear and enjoy the satisfaction as the last tape falls into place and the last banana peel is tossed into the trash.

KEY FEATURES
• Item Organization: Organize over 2,500 items throughout the store.

• Store Decluttering: In addition to tapes, books, and board games, clear away piles of clutter and trash scattered around the store.

• Music & Radio: Relax and enjoy a variety of tracks while you organize the store.

• Visual Evolution: Watch the store transform from a chaotic mess into a perfectly organized retro paradise.

• Coffee Break: Take a few minutes to brew yourself a cup of coffee and enjoy the view by the window overlooking the city.

• Customizable Lighting: Choose the brightness and color of the lighting to match your mood and create the atmosphere you want.

• Cat Monyo: His Majesty, the owner of the store, will keep you company so you never feel alone.

• Easter Eggs: Some tapes, books, and board games may contain references or hidden easter eggs for you to discover.

Are you ready to restore order and balance to the store?
