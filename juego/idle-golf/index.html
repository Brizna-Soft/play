De qué trata

Idle Golf is a chill-yet-addictive idle game where you tee off automatically, rake in cash, and build the ultimate golf empire one shot at a time.
Unlock a growing roster of balls and golfers, each with their own twist on how the money flows. Chase flags for big bonuses, smash destructibles, and watch your earnings compound with every upgrade.

As your game grows, pour your earnings into upgrades that change how every shot plays out:
• Faster fire rate and longer drives
• Money multipliers that stack with every run
• Specialty balls with unique mechanics

When you've squeezed every dollar out of a run, retire to earn Medals and spend them on permanent unlocks that carry over forever. Each run starts fresh — but your collection of balls, golfers, and courses stays with you.
Idle Golf is a satisfying loop of swinging, earning, and upgrading — whether you're watching every shot or just checking back in to count the zeroes.
