De qué trata

Feed your fellow Shape Buddies to a bizarre cast of Glutton Monsters before they put you on the menu, in this chaotic physics-based party game. Solo or with a friend, catch, balance, and fling food with a collection of absurd, anything-but-proper plates. Everything obeys physics; nothing obeys you.

Serve the monsters using an increasingly absurd collection of plates and makeshift serving tools, from humble ladles and buttery croissants to enormous legendary swords. Every plate has its own shape, friction, weight, and bounce, turning even a familiar level into a fresh disaster.

Play through the campaign solo, or play with a friend in local co-op or versus modes, with support for Steam Remote Play Together.
Customer satisfaction is mandatory. Employee survival is considered a bonus!

Catch. Balance. Feed.
Read each monster’s order, catch the correct food, and keep it stable long enough to make the throw. Serve too many wrong orders, and your shift—and possibly your life—is over.
Every Plate Is a New Physics Problem
Bowls, platters, ladles, croissants, swords, and other objects that probably should not qualify as dinnerware all behave differently. Learn their weight, friction, and bounce—or watch dinner roll straight off the edge.

A Whole Planet of Hungry Customers
Serve increasingly strange Glutton Monsters across colourful kitchens, ancient jungles, travelling circuses, and other suspiciously cheerful locations. Each level introduces new hazards, movement patterns, and ways for your carefully prepared meal to escape.

Serve Alone or Make a Mess Together
Take on the campaign solo, cooperate with a friend, or compete over the same food in versus mode.
