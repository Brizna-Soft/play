Clearance Defence

Juego de PC · Casual, Estrategia · Estrategia

Defend the clearance shelf. When the lights go out, the seasonal war begins. Lead your unsold Easter stock against waves of full-price enemies, before morning reveals the secret.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Every night, when the lights go out and the staff go home, the shelves come alive, and the clearance items are fighting for their lives.
Clearance Defence is a strategic tower defence set inside a retail shop after closing time. You are the tactical commander of Easter's last holdouts: unsold chocolate eggs and seasonal stock. Against you: full-price Summer Garden Centre stock, marching down the aisles in cardboard armour to clear the shelves before morning.
Build your defences and hold out until morning, and don't let the staff find out.
Your towers are built from whatever store supplies you can scavenge. Stack the boxes into a maze and the enemy has to pick its way through it, under fire from your towers all the way.
Your Safe Zone is the stockroom that keeps the fight going. The Easter stock you save joins your workforce, and you decide who makes the foam darts, who makes Construction Material and who carries it all out to your towers. Let the supply run dry and your towers fall silent. On Hard, your engineers carry every load through the aisles on foot, where the gnomes can catch them. Buy the Delivery Drones upgrade and they fly straight over your walls instead, for faster delivery.
The enemy grows stronger with each passing night, and the gnomes come in larger and larger waves.
Each night ends in a Boss Wave, and no two bosses fight the same way. One heals the gnomes around it, one rolls through your maze with your darts glancing off it, and one turns your walls into a barbecue.
Play It Your Way
Made for keyboard and mouse, and just as happy on a pad: Xbox controller and Steam Deck players get the whole game, from the radial build wheel to ordering defenders about with a flick of the stick. Every button on screen works from the pad.
