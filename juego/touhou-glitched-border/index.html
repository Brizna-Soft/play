東方異線境 ~ Touhou: Glitched Border

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie · Acción, Indie

Stop to shoot. Draw to cast! Restore an overlapping Gensokyo in this Touhou action roguelite. Choose from six heroines, draw Spell Cards with your mouse, and face bosses from other timelines.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Stop to shoot. Draw to cast.
Stand still and your heroine fires on her own. Drag the mouse and you draw a Spell Card: lines become barriers, circles bloom into fields of ice and fire. Time slows while you draw, so every cast is a decision made in the middle of the danmaku.
A Gensokyo stitched together from timelines that never were
An endless winter. Danmaku with no rules. An afternoon frozen in time. A crack in the Hakurei Barrier has pulled other timelines over Gensokyo, and at the end of every chapter waits a familiar face from the wrong one, declaring Spell Cards you have never seen.
Three rifts, three eras
Restore present-day Gensokyo and the next rift opens: a rain-soaked neon city of the far future, then the ancient land from before the Barrier was ever tied. Each world brings six chapters, new enemies and bosses, its own arrangement of the soundtrack, and a new outfit for every heroine.
Six heroines, a different run every time
Start with Reimu and Marisa, then free Cirno, Youmu, Sakuya and Remilia from their timelines to make them playable. Each has her own shot and signature Spell Card. Level-up cards reshape every run, and time shards buy permanent upgrades at the shrine between runs.
Features
• Six mouse-drawn Spell Cards, plus a signature spell for each heroine

• Six playable heroines, each with outfits from three worlds

• Three worlds and 18 chapters, with mid-bosses and Spell Card boss battles

• Dodge, graze, hide behind cover and ricochet your shots

• Voxel scenery, holographic UI and dimensional-rift effects

• Fully playable in 7 languages: English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese

This is an unofficial Touhou Project fan work, unaffiliated with the original creator.
