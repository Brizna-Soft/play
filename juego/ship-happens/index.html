De qué trata

Ship Happens is a cooperative multiplayer (2-4 player online co-op) space game where you and your mates have found yourselves on a spectacularly reckless journey. Each mission, your crew will warp onto a ship in need, take the controls, and escape before a black hole swallows you and your chances of a payout.

Each mission gives you and your friends a different, nonsensical spacecraft to figure out. Explore the ship, learn what keeps it running, and work together to get out alive - with a hefty payday if you pull it off.

The work can be dangerous, but it doesn't have to be with the proper coordination. Collaborate with your teammates as you shoot down asteroids, transport giant pieces of coal, and experiment with gravity.

Your crew will fail as much as you succeed. Dust yourself off and go again, or try your luck on a different mission.
