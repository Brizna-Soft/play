De qué trata

A curious little girl sees herself lost in an unfamiliar place, struggling to understand what's happening both around her and within her own mind. Lyara starts her journey in a new world, making friends along the way and facing all sorts of challenges that will help her grow and make players reflect.
Join this adventure to overcome challenges and learn alongside Lyara.

Lyara is a challenging 2D platformer with unique mechanics for each world, demanding agility and precision from players, as well as puzzle-solving skills.
Get ready for a challenge! Inspired by classic platform games and mixing a wide range of obstacles, Lyara provides a rich gameplay experience and a meaningful, enchanting story.

Every new world has a distinct look and specific mechanics, compelling players to adapt their gameplay accordingly. As you move forward and help Lyara overcome different obstacles in her journey, you will discover a moving narrative that raises as many answers as new questions to unravel.
Become entranced in this ever-changing universe until you reach the conclusion of this alluring tale!

Help Lyara balance her inner chakras with each challenge and explore unique worlds that will shape the course of her story.

Get to know more about Lyara's life during her journey, look for answers, and find new characters that will help you immerse yourself in this introspective story.
Each new friend has a different role in Lyara's life, but they're all essential to her growth.

And more!
