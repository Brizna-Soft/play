Data Center Simulator

Juego de PC · Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

Night shift at a data center, first person. Tickets come in from the NOC: run the diagnostic, strap in, pull the right part, swap it, close it by the book, and get graded. 45 real procedures, a sandbox where you can break the facility, and interview prep for data center tech jobs.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

You just clocked in for your first shift at DataCenter One.

Data Center Simulator puts you on the floor of a realistic data center facility where every rack, cable, and system is your responsibility. This isn't a clicker or an idle game — it's a hands-on training tool wrapped in a first-person experience that teaches the same procedures used in real facilities worldwide to prepare you for real world DC Tech Interviews.ARRIVE. BADGE IN. GET TO WORK.
Start each shift by clearing the security mantrap with your badge, checking in with the guard, and reviewing your task board. Read your shift handover emails. Check the NOC console for facility status. Then head to the floor.45+ TRAINING SCENARIOS ACROSS 9+ CATEGORIES
• Hardware Operations — PSU swaps, fan tray replacements, drive carrier hot-swaps

• Structured Cabling — Copper uplinks, fiber patching, TIA-606 labeling audits

• Network Troubleshooting — CRC errors, VLAN misconfigurations, duplex mismatches, STP loops, BGP flaps

• Cooling Operations — CRAH failures, hot aisle breaches, chilled water valve adjustments

• Power Operations — Utility transfers, UPS battery tests, generator load banks, full failover drills

• Rack Deployment — Single rack commissions to full row deployments

• Security Operations — Badge reader repairs, tailgating response, fire suppression drills, incident forensics

• Capacity Planning — Power audits, cooling analysis, growth forecasts, PUE optimization

• Production Floor — Thermal drift response, shift handovers, maintenance windows, emergency response

Each category has 5 levels that unlock progressively as you prove your skills.REAL PROCEDURES. REAL TOOLS.
Every scenario follows real-world procedures. Equip your ESD wrist strap before touching hardware.
