Neurospasm

Juego de PC · Acción · Acción

A Weird First Person Roguelike Horde Shooter. Hook your brain into your computer, dive into a game inside a game, unbox and stack unique items, use up to 6 weapons at once, kill weird fleshy enemies, and uncover why nothing here makes any sense

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

A First Person Roguelike Horde Shooter
Kill hordes of weird fleshy enemies, using lots of weapons, and active abilities that you power up by getting kills

Collect and Stack 100+ Unique items
Each item can change your weapons and bullets, change your stats, add passive abilities, or spawn buddies that help you in some way. And the best part, every item can stack, making them more and more powerful

Ascend the Pyramid
Travel through 5 layers, all with their own styles, enemies, and unique mechanics

Fully Procedular Maps
The maps, and their layout are randomised each time you play

Upgrade Yourself
Earn money based on how well you do, then spend it between runs, upgrading yourself

Unique Events
To make runs even more interesting, random events can happen which alter how entire layers play

Beat Your Friends
Earn score based on your kills in the endless mode that remixes the base game, then show your score off on a global leaderboard

Hidden Secrets
Discover secrets hidden on the maps, that allow you to unlock modifiers, that change how the game plays

Key Features:
• 100+ Unique Items to Unbox and Stack

• Use Up to 6 Weapons at once

• 5 Unique Stages, that are procedurally generated

• An Endless Mode, with a worldwide leaderboard

• Lots of fleshy enemies, and Bosses to fight

• Permanent Upgrades, and Unlocks

• Randomised Events, that can shake up runs

• Unlockable Modifiers, that change how the game plays
