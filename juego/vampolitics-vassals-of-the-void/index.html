De qué trata

Vampolitics is a narrative resource management RPG set in a dark and silly world of The SPACE VAMPIRES.

Take on the challenging role of a Space Sovereign and rule your own Space Vampire Space kingdom during the twilight years of a once powerful coven... but be warned your resources are almost gone, the nobles want you dead and your closes family members have their own ambitions.

...and worst of all your own Space Janitor is viewed more favorably by your most trusted advisers than you are.
Rule from the Throne Room
Each week, you’ll decide how best to spend your resources. Will you use your gold to upgrade the old ships from your last battle, or patron a visiting artist from another corner of the galaxy? The choice is yours as you sit upon your throne as the oddballs of the galaxy come to ask for your aid.

Family Matters in a manner
Your family bloodline also plays a vital role in establishing your claim throughout the Space Vampire realm. Choose your starting family wisely, as some offer unique events and resources that could make or break your attempt to balance the covens books. Will you marry off a relative to the cultists to keep the peace? Trade a spouse for gold? Or perhaps attend a grand ball for the other prominent Space Vampires? The choice is yours.
Face the Strange and Unusual
Build your reputation by navigating bizarre events and difficult dilemmas. A prisoner of war might be sold, drained for blood, put to work, or kept in a cage in your throne room for your own amusement. A strange merchant might try to sell you a painted rock... or the very secrets of the cosmos. From astro-artists and doctors with brains in bowls are there to explore.

Fighters of the Void
Rebuild your coven’s fleet and notoriety.
