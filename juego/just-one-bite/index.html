Just One Bite

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Acceso anticipado · Acción, Indie, Terror

A 1~4 player co-op horror game. Your mission is to gather as much food as possible for the humans. But not all food is normal. Can you complete this mission to the very end?

Tráiler

De qué trata

Background
One day, monsters began to appear inside food all around the world.
Your mission is to become a wooden puppet crafted by humans and gather as much food as possible.

Food? Monster?
Is that really food in front of you?
You won’t know until it’s already in your mouth.

Floating Weapon
You wield a powerful weapon you can control freely—
it will act as your very hands and feet. Find the weapons that suit you best.

Control your weapon to grab food and items, then use them in combat. Sometimes, even your teammates can become great weapons.

Floating Truck
A modified truck with various functions will help you. Ride the truck to a safe zone and prepare for your next adventure.
but don’t expect it to be very sturdy.

Turn Food Into Money!
In this world, food is money.
Use the food you collect to upgrade your fork and your body.
Will you be able to complete every mission? What reward awaits you at the end?
