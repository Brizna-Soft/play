De qué trata

Banana Life Sim is a game in which you can become a banana.
This game offers meditative gameplay. You can stand and contemplate the beauty of the forest around you, living your life as a banana. You can also perform the actions that bananas usually do (mostly standing in the same place).

Features
• You're a banana

• You're in the forest

• There are other bananas around you

• And not only bananas
