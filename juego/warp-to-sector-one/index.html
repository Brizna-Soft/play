Warp to Sector One

Juego de PC · Indie, Simuladores, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie, Simulación

Trade, fight, and explore a procedurally generated galaxy in this deep space trading game inspired by 80's sci-fi computer screens and early text based space games. Command fleets, negotiate with alien factions, colonize planets, and forge your legacy across five unique career paths.

De qué trata

This game is my homage to Trade Wars 2002, a text/ANSI graphic based multiplayer space-trading game from the '80s/90s that I've tried to recreate the core concept and look and feel faithfully without being an exact clone, and also inspired by the computer UI's from science-fiction films/TV shows of the '70s and '80s with wireframe graphics and all caps text.  I hope for those of you that play, who know what I'm talking about, it will bring back some nice memories for you, and those of you who don't, I hope you enjoy what the vision of the future in the '80s looked like.

Made with love and care to bring a modern touch to the old style with respect.

WHAT CREATORS ARE SAYING

EXPLORE. TRADE. FIGHT. CONQUER.
Every session begins the same way: you, a Merchant Scout, and a galaxy full of opportunity. What happens next is up to you.

A LIVING GALAXY
▸ 2,000 sectors, procedurally rewired every run. No two galaxies are the same.
▸ A dynamic galaxy where every action that you take or another pilot takes can cascade and affect every corner of the map.  Economic Zones, Hidden Spatial Anomalies, Pirate Hideouts, Unknown Dangers, Trading Ports, Rogue Fighters, Bounty Hunters all interacting with you and each other to make the galaxy an emergent gameplay experience.
▸ Epoch shifts every 2,000, 3000 or 5000 turns that fundamentally shifts the behaviour of the galaxy as a whole: pirate uprisings, trade booms, and faction wars across six eras before the Final Epoch locks in.
▸ Ten alien powers with capitals, patrols, and long memories.
