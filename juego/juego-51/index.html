De qué trata

"I should have bought it back then. I should have sold it back then."
Now you actually can. Go back to 1999 and make every one of those regrets right.
Reborn Investment King runs on real KOSPI and KOSDAQ data from 1999 to 2026 and real Seoul real-estate prices. This is not an invented market — it is the real one, moving day by day exactly as it did.
Live the real incidents, exactly as they happened
The IMF financial crisis, the dot-com bubble, the 2008 crisis, COVID-19 — real events hit the very stocks they hit in real life, on the very days they did, and arrive as news while you are still holding. You are the only person alive who knows what is coming. Will you really have the nerve to hold, to sell, or to buy more?
He lost everything and gave up on life — then woke up in his own body in 1999, with only 1.2 million won and 27 years of memory about to be put to use.
Real data, real consequences
Search, buy, and sell stocks and manage your portfolio using real daily price data for every company listed on the KOSPI and KOSDAQ. Real trading volume limits how much you can actually sell, so you will feel realistic liquidity risk — in a crash, you may not be able to sell as much as you want.
Beyond stocks, real estate — apartments, multi-family housing, and commercial buildings across Seoul’s 25 districts — is also built on real historical sale, jeonse, and monthly-rent price data going back to 1998, giving you a second investment avenue to diversify into.
Story Mode
A 27-year life simulation following a protagonist reborn to get revenge — in cash — on the two people who betrayed him.
