Don’t wait for winter to hit The Slopes! Dodge obstacles, collect coins and dash to the bottom of the mountain in a downhill race for glory any day of the year. Extreme terrain without extreme cold! But be warned--without the risk of frostbite you might never come off the mountain!

De qué trata

Ready for your best day on the mountain? You’ll get it when you hit The Slopes. Experience skiing like never before! No clunky boots, no chairlift lines and no harsh cold! Just you and the mountain--and any number of obstacles. Trees, fences, logs, rocks and poles might slow you down and ruin your chances on the leaderboard but at least you won’t need ski patrol! Tilt your head to steer and use your ski poles for speed. And remember, just because you won’t get a concussion doesn’t mean the bumps don’t hurt!

Built from the ground up for VR, The Slopes is compatible with both the HTC Vive and Oculus Rift + Touch with support for standing AND roomscale spaces.
