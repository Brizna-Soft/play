IMYEON : Yokai Survivor

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie · Acción, Indie

A pixel-art survivors game where you fight swarms of yokai with old Korean weapons and shaman tools like the Horn Bow and Spirit Knives. Pick an upgrade on every level-up and fuse two maxed weapons into one. 6 stages, a 30-minute Endless mode, 20 heroes and 12 fusion weapons.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Fight with old Korean weapons and shaman tools
Yokai pour in from every side and you hold your ground. Move, and your weapons fire on their own. Each level-up offers a choice of weapons such as the Horn Bow, Taegeuk Fan, Spirit Knives and Jakdu Blade, along with rounds and passive upgrades.Fuse two weapons into one
Two weapons that meet the conditions become a single fusion weapon. Fire Aura and Fire rounds become Hellfire, the Horn Bow and Execute become the Death Ledger, and the Jakdu Blade and Lifesteal become the Dragon Pearl. There are 12 fusion weapons in all.Banish the cards you don't want
Don't like your level-up cards? Reroll them or skip. A banished card won't show up again for the rest of the run, and permanent upgrades give you more banishes.20 heroes and permanent upgrades
Every hero has a unique ability or starting weapon. Arzan hits harder while moving, Saddie Backfire rams enemies with a dash, and Gangrim, a reaper of the underworld, starts with the Whip. Clear the Underworld Road to unlock an Underworld outfit for every hero. After each run, spend your gold on permanent upgrades and hero ranks.Stages and Endless
There are 6 stages and 4 difficulties. Beat the boss that appears at 8 minutes to clear a stage, and earn up to 3 stars on each combination. On the last stage, the Underworld Road, the Underworld Judge guards the pass and Yeomra waits at the end. Endless mode is open from the start, and the final boss arrives at 30 minutes.What's inside
• 20 heroes

• 6 stages with 4 difficulties, plus Endless

• 12 fusion weapons

• 46 yokai, including 11 bosses, collected in the Codex

• 27 Steam achievements

• Korean and English, keyboard, mouse and Xbox controller
