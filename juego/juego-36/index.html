忌質物管理局　第七保管室

Juego de PC · Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación, Terror

Welcome to the Ishitsubutsu Management Bureau. Earn ¥10,000 an hour inspecting cursed objects. Compare each item with its inspection record, identify anomalies, and choose to Store, Incinerate, Seal, or Report it. A job-simulation horror game with a seven-day mystery. Japanese only.

De qué trata

Welcome to the Ishitsubutsu Management Bureau.
Hourly pay: ¥10,000.
Your job: Inspect cursed objects.
Please note: This game is available in Japanese only.
You've been assigned to the Seventh Storage Room of the mysterious Ishitsubutsu Management Bureau.
Your job is to conduct secondary inspections of objects that have already undergone their initial inspection during the day.
Carefully examine the objects brought before you and compare them with their inspection records.
Is there anything unusual about their appearance?
Do their properties match what's written in the records?
Is there something strange about the work instructions?
And above all—should this object really be placed in storage?
Identify anomalies and follow the regulations to determine the appropriate course of action.
That's your job.
Inspecting cursed objects is all in a day's work.
『忌質物管理局 第七保管室』 is a job-simulation horror game where you examine anomalous objects, compare them with their records, and decide how to handle them.
Each object comes with an initial inspection record.
Compare the record with the actual object and select the appropriate action:
STORE
INCINERATE
SEAL
REPORT
Make the right decisions and complete your shift.
Your performance will be evaluated at the end of each workday in the form of a pay statement.
But as the days go by, your duties will gradually become more complicated.
What seemed perfectly normal yesterday might not seem so normal today.
Something is happening in the Seventh Storage Room.
This isn't just a job where you sort cursed objects all day.
Unusual work instructions.
Questionable records.
Strange incidents within the Bureau.
