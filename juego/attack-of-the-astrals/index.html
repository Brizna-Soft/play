Attack of the Astrals

Heroes and villains fight the cosmos in this tactical roguelike! Every move unleashes an attack in quick battles that reward creative strategies. Combine necromancy, explosives, mimicry and more to thwart the evolving Astral threat!

De qué trata

Unite legendary heroes and villains in turn-based combat against an Astral army that grows stronger with you.

Champions swap tiles with units in their path and automatically unleash an area attack at their destination. Arrange the battlefield to save allies and doom enemies. Each turn is a fluid 3-move combo, with no menu navigation necessary.

Every region poses a unique Astral threat, with elemental minions spawning each turn to support their masters. Liberating a region grants new abilities, but the Astral army gains the powers of those you’ve defeated.

Add to your champions' skill set with Zodiacs like Honor or Solitude, calling upon them once per battle. Gain experience to level up your team with Upgrades and earn more powerful Scrolls, consumables used to cast map-wide spells.
