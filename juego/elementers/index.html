De qué trata

A multiplayer Action-Adventure, join the mage ranks and master the most powerful abilities the Master Wizard can cook. Try protecting the human race against the random monsters that keep attacking the villages for some unknown reason that you might find out.

PVE Story missions with up to 4 players!
Play the game story through missions, alone or with up to other 3 friends!
More difficulties are unlocked upon completion.

PVP Gamemodes!
You can invite your friend to a duel or have a duel against a random opponent, or take part of a 1v1v1v1 match.

Loadout System!
Customize your loadout, if you don't like an ability, just change it!
You can equip 1 generic dash, 1 generic melee and 4 character specific abilities.
Dashes and melees are usable by all characters.

Boss fights!

Many Abilities to unlock, soon more!
Currently, there are 32 abilities to equip and try out.
