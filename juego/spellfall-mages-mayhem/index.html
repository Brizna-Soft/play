De qué trata

Spellfall: Mages & Mayhem is a chaotic arena brawler where rival mages blast each other with powerful spells as the arena crumbles beneath their feet. Draft new abilities and items each round to create your build, then use it to outplay your opponents across seven rounds of magical mayhem. Land skill shots, evade incoming spells, knock rivals into the void, and survive the chaos.
FAST, ACTION PACKED BRAWLS
Blast your friends and foes in explosive multiplayer arena combat.  Battle it out in Free-for-All brawls with up to 6 players or group up for 2v2v2 and 3v3 team modes.  Find matches quickly with bots stepping in to cover any open player slots.

ZERO CLASSES, TOTAL CHAOS
Draft your power from 40 unique abilities and 30 build defining items. Combine spells and stats to create a build that fits your playstyle.

THE ARENA CRUMBLES
As each round progresses, the arena collapses into the void.  Hold your ground or be claimed by Spellfall. Outplay your opponents to survive the chaos.

MORE MAGES, MORE MAYHEM
Bring your friends and battle in Party Free-For-All or group up for Team Modes.  Looking for more mages to play with?  Join the Spellfall Discord Community to find other players, share builds, and get the latest updates right from the developer.
