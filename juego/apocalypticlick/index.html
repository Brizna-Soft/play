Apocalypticlick

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

An incremental apocalyptic survival builder with research, expeditions, events, raids, and resource balancing. Found a settlement, gather resources, and try to keep the roof on while the wasteland keeps knocking.

De qué trata

A quick incremental settlement builder.

Apocalypticlick is a quick idle settlement builder. Instead of "number go up" this is an experiment in balancing resources, land, and settler hope.

Will you work to optimize life satisfaction, or go full steam ahead with no regard for the people in the way?

Note: Given this is a free and quick game no major updates are anticipated.Define your culture and found your settlement.
Name your settlement and pick from a list of positive and negative traits.Build some, then make room to build more.
Buildings have non-zero size, and land is finite. Keep clearing and building and watch the numbers go up.A complete research tree.
Unlock new techs to keep progressing. Keep progressing until this world feels like the one of old.Expeditions!
Send your soldiers to loot a derelict department store, ancient ruins, a rotting datacenter, or long-dead military bases.Random events.
Will you give the raiders what they want or try to fight them off? Help the destitute or send them away? Crop blight, disease, and strangers with promises of something new.
