Docked Realms

Juego de PC · Casual, Rol, Estrategia · Estrategia, Rol (RPG)

A idle auto-battler drawn from Sumerian myth: bind ancient souls and hold a strip torn from the laws of the gods. Heroes fight every battle on their own and gather rewards even while you are away. You choose the line and the relics; casting spells is optional.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Battles play out on their own, with no input from you. Your souls take hits and can fall, so what you prepare decides the fight: who stands where, what they wear and which skills they carry. Casting spells by hand is optional.

The sacred rules that hold the world together are called the Me. In a serpent's attack a piece broke off from them, fell between the worlds, and became a thin strip: the Docked Realm. Time flows slowly here, and heroes do not age.

The gods could not take the broken piece back, so they bound ancient souls to it. To push back the chaos and mend the Me, they are now yours to command.

Your souls fight on their own
Battles play out by themselves, wave after wave, stage after stage. What you decide is the shape of the fight:
• The Line. Three souls stand in a crescent: Front, Middle and Back. Who stands where is your call.

• A god's book. Worship a god at the Shrine and bind three spells from their book to three keys, for when you want to cast them yourself. More spells are found on the creatures of the strip.

• The moment. Step in if you wish. Time a dodge right and it lands perfect.

The first three
• Lugal, Tank / Frontline. The king of Uruk stands at the very front and, like a wall, does not fall back.

• Gula, Healer / Buffer. She gathered the first tears Anu shed upon the earth. They became the first medicine, and she still closes wounds with them.

• Erra, Melee Burst / Leech. He once scorched the soil of Ki. His fury never cooled; every blow makes him stronger.

Each soul has its own Path tree with forks, skills to remember and rank up with ME Tablets, and relics only it can wear. The Path can be reset for gold.

Relics, the Kiln and the Cube
Relics drop in five rarities, Common through Legendary, each with its own rolled marks.
