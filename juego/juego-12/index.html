Game Overview
After the Demon King is defeated, hero Edmond rescues Princess Ilyana. With his fall, the balance of power unravels and darkness returns. As hidden schemes arise, you become her guardian. Will you guide her through the shadows to a brighter future?

Character Profile

A kind-hearted princess beloved throughout the kingdom.
She shares a deep bond with the hero, and they secretly love each other. Yet they put their feelings aside for the peace of the kingdom and the happiness of its people. Together, they move forward for the future of their kingdom.
