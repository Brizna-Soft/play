De qué trata

Every vehicle showcased in the screenshots is a testament to our community's ingenuity. Homebrew is crafted with passion by a small, dedicated team and while we've poured our heart and soul into this project it has its quirks.

The learning curve is steep, and there may be imperfections, but if you crave a challenge and a platform where creativity knows no bounds, Homebrew awaits.

PLEASE NOTE: while active development is currently paused due to limited resources, every purchase supports the preservation of the game. Despite the lack of recent updates, we are dedicated to keeping Homebrew Vehicle Sandbox accessible and functional.

CURRENT FEATURES
• SANDBOX BUILDING
Take the reins as the master builder. With no loads of parts and customization features, the sky's the limit.

• PHYSICS
In addition to land vehicle physics the game has aerodynamics and water dynamics with waves in place so that you can test your creations to the extreme.

• PARTS
Whether you're into cars, planes, boats, or more – Homebrew has your back. From engines and wheels to wings and weapons, everything's at your disposal.

• LOGIC PARTS
Take building to the next level and combine usual parts with logic components to build autopilot vehicles, stabilizing systems, transforming vehicles, robotic arms and more. Your imagination is the limit.

• WORLD
Explore the world of Homebrew and test your creations on one of the many islands. The world has a dynamic day and night cycle, a weather system and is large enough to test any type of vehicle including boats, supersonic planes, cars and more.

• MULTIPLAYER
Play online and battle with your friends in the multiplayer.

• AN AWESOME COMMUNITY
Whether you seek guidance, camaraderie, or a chat, our doors are open on Steam and our DISCORD CHANNEL.
