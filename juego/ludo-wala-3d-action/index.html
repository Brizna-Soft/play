De qué trata

Ludo Wala 3D & Action brings Ludo, Snakes & Ladders and Stairs to a 3D tabletop for 2 to 4 players

Race four tokens home in Ludo. Climb ladders and dodge snakes on the way to square 100. In Stairs, climb 50 steps through hurdles, ropes and holes, and send rivals back to the floor when you land on them

Take turns with friends on the same screen. Save an offline game and return to it later

Open a private online room and share its code with friends. Players can join across desktop, mobile and browser, so everyone can use their own device

Choose your dice, cups, pawns and board looks. Send reactions and throw toys across the table while you play

Offline play needs no account. Online play requires a separate game account
