Spreadcube

Juego de PC · Utilidades

Spreadcube allows you to view your spreadsheets as 3D objects in virtual reality (or on a standard monitor). Explore example spreadcubes or create your own using the CSV importer. Select your favorite cells and save them to a list for future viewing on any platform.

De qué trata

Spreadcube is an application that lets you view your spreadsheets as 3D objects in virtual reality (or on a standard monitor).

Features:
• Example spreadcubes comparing PC parts, VR headsets, and decisions in strategy games
• Bring-your-own-spreadcube by importing CSVs and selecting which columns to display
• Select favorite cells and generate a list thereof to view cross-platform
• On headsets with eye tracking, use where you look to focus different layers of the spreadcube
• Steam cloud support to easily bring your spreadcubes between devices
