De qué trata

Escape day-to-day drudgery by immersing yourself in this customizable 2D logic puzzle of bathroom etiquette. Make life's big decisions, like which urinal is best. Build your own queue management company. Create new levels. Paint beautiful art on stall walls. Reveal the unspoken culture of public restrooms, and encounter their cultish enforcers.

Features
• Tons of Puzzles

• Queue Management Tycoon

• Head-to-Head Multiplayer

• Custom Level Editor

• Graffiti Painting Editor

• Steam Achievements

Puzzle Mode
Uncover the unwritten rules of public restrooms. Errors lead to encounters with mysterious hooded figures, seemingly bent on enforcing bathroom etiquette. Avoid these cultish foes as you play through dozens of levels, earning stars for speed and accuracy along the way!

Queue Management Mode
Use what you learn from your new robed friends to start a first of its kind queue-management company! Turns out the general public could use some help, and they are willing to pay for your guidance. Put bids in to become the sole manager of each hand-picked restroom location. With the help of some inconspicuous cameras and very, very vague contract guidelines, you can moderate these bathroom lines remotely! Place guests in optimal urinals to earn big tips, and reinvest the money you earn into quality upgrades and new locations.

Multiplayer Mode
Think you have mastered the etiquette? Face off head to head with players from around the world using a full integration of Steam's peer-to-peer multiplayer system. Players may join a lobby together then opt to play with the puzzle ruleset or a full on race. Prove your knowledge of the most esoteric and niche rules of the restroom.
