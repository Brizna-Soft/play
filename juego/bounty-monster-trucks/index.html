De qué trata

A proper Monster Truck game true fans have been asking for is here! Fully customizable trucks, custom track editor, racing and freestyle modes, and many different truck bodies to choose from!Infinite Replayability
With the custom track editor, pick a Race Track Infield, Indoor Arena, Stadium, or Beach environment and design your perfect fantasy track or recreate your favorite one and share it with others!Make it your own
With the wide selection of Monster Truck bodies to choose from ranging from old school, to Golden Era, to Modern Fiberglass bodies, all with customizable extras.Rewarding Learning Curve
The fully physics based model means mastering driving and freestyle will take hours of practice, for a true competitive scene with a high skill ceiling
