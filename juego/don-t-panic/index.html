De qué trata

The galaxy has sides. Pick yours.

Three factions hold the systems between you and the enemy capital. The Reavers are fast, brutal raiders. The Paragon is a machine order of shields and heavy guns. The Gorgons are something alive from the deep systems. Each has its own ships, its own leader and a long memory.
Every faction keeps a standing with you, from War to Allied. Destroy their ships and they hunt you. Do their work, pay tribute or trade in their space and they open their pads and their traders to you. Talk to their leaders face to face: ask for passage, offer tribute, take a contract, propose a pact, or declare war.
Free the three fortresses and break the capital. Or ally with all three factions and sign the peace without a shot. Both end the war. Then start the next one, harder, with everything you earned.

Armor is a puzzle
• Every ship has an armor level. Match your weapon's penetration to the target, or watch your shots ricochet in a bright, useless light show.

• Plasma Blaster, Autocannon, Laser Cutter or Tesla Coils, with ammo you switch on the fly. Smart rockets into crowds, flares against locks, an energy shield for the last moment.

• The fire control system shows you the lead and the armor of every target. Make each shot count.

• Stay in a hot sector and the Threat Level climbs: more enemies, meaner ones, and a soundtrack that rises with the fight (music by Karl Casey @ White Bat Audio).

26 enemy ships, 3 ways to be a boss

• From the Reavers' suicide drones to the Gorgons' G-VI Matriarch mothership, every faction fields its own line of ships, each met for the first time as a stronghold's flagship before it joins the ranks.

• Named warlords hold some systems. Shoot down their aces and they come for you themselves.
