De qué trata

THE 1.0 OVERHAUL: TACTICAL VR SURVIVAL AT UNMATCHED SCALE

Survival Denied has graduated from Early Access, leaving the "casual shooter" days behind. Powered by a completely overhauled, high-performance ECS engine architecture, the game drops you into a brutal, tactical VR action-roguelite designed for maximum scale and player freedom.

There are no floating menus and no textual hand-holding. Everything from weapon selection to level unlocks is handled physically in the world. Read your health on your diegetic forearm displays, check your ammo on your physical weapons, and rely entirely on your own tactical instincts.

250-ZOMBIE SENSORY HORDES

We’ve pushed horde density to the absolute limit. Face down up to 250 undead entities on-screen at once—but they aren’t just running in a straight line.

• Sight & Sound AI: Zombies now patrol in independent roaming packs, hunting by sight and sound.

• Surgical Stealth or Heavy Metal: Creep through the fog with a suppressed weapon to pick off targets silently. Or, fire an unsuppressed rocket launcher and watch the noise pull six different hunting packs down onto your exact coordinates.

• Specialized Threat Matrix: Track Radioactive zones in using the audio clicks of your physical Geiger counter, dodge Crawlers, and eliminate Exploders that deliver powerful blast damaging everyone nearby.

10 MAPS THAT ACTIVELY TRY TO KILL YOU

You aren't just running in circles anymore. The environment is now a core hazard.

• Acid Rain City: When the sky suddenly turns pitch black, you have seconds to get indoors before the rain melts your health, forcing frantic, close quarters room clearing.

• The Toxic Swamp: Navigate narrow wooden walkways over lethal water. One wrong step while running from a horde means death.
