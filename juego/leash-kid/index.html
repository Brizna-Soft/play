De qué trata

Leash Kid is a physics based destruction game where you play a mischievous kid on a leash, dragged through a department store while Mom shops. Grab, throw, and smash everything in reach, rack up points, complete objectives on 6 different grocery routes, and race the clock before Mom finishes her shopping.

Ever wondered what it feels like to be one of those leash kids? Well now you have!!!

FEATURES
• Play as a mischievous kid… on a leash

• An exhausted Mom who’s absolutely done with your shenanigans

• Semi-randomized store layouts for replayability

• Multiple routes and objectives to complete

• Dynamic scoring system to beat your own high scores

• Comedic chaos and physics driven destruction

EVERY RUN IS A DISASTER
Short, chaotic runs packed with physics mayhem and ever higher scores to chase, where
the fun only ends when Mom finishes her shopping trip.

Cause chaos, unleash the tantrum and challenge... everyone!
