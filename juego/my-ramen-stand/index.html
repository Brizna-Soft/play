My Ramen Stand

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

My Ramen Stand is a cosy yet chaotic single player cooking game where you prepare ramen, serve customers, and grow your kitchen as your career progresses. Watch it go from a quiet, humble stall to the busiest most famous Ramen in Tokyo.

De qué trata

A quiet stand, a warm bowl, and a passionate cook.
Welcome to My YOUR Ramen Stand.
My Ramen Stand brings the high-pressure environment of a Tokyo street stall to your desktop. There are no gimmicks or obstacles. Just you, your ingredients, and a growing line of hungry customers.

Career mode
Start small and build. Each day the stand gets a little bigger, the menu a little wider, the lunch rush a little louder. New toppings, extra space, more seats. Unlock the kitchen as you go, until the stall you opened on day one barely looks like the same place.
Challenge mode
Put your ramen stand to the test with challenge mode! A fast-paced 3 minute lunch rush where you serve as many bowls as you can handle. Compare your scores globally or with friends and level up the ranks as you earn more and more money!
