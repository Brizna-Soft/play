Gorilla Tag

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual · Acción

Run, jump, and climb using only your hands. Play tag or infection with people online. Run away from the infected players, or outmaneuver the survivors to catch them. Embrace your inner ape.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Reject Humanity. Become Monke.

Run, climb, and jump in VR using a unique locomotion method that only needs the movement of your hands and arms. No buttons, no sticks, no teleportation. Push off surfaces to jump and squeeze them with both hands to climb.

Outrun and juke your opponents, or outmaneuver them to catch them. Parkour up trees and cliff faces to evade and chase other monke down. Swim, slide, swing on ropes, and hang glide in the clouds. Hang out in a virtual jungle with randos or group up in a private room with friends to hang out and play. The stakes are low, so feel free to just chat or make up your own games. The movement is easy to learn, but hard to master. Crossplay with the PC versions of the game, so play with anyone on any platform.

An ever-growing list of maps includes the OG tag maps (Forest, Canyon, Mountain, Beach, and more) as well as new experiments (Ghost Reactor, Hoverboard Racing, Monke Ball, Prop Hunt) and player-created maps in virtual VR.

Visit our store in the City, with a rotating inventory of items to buy, play with, and wear to express your true monke.
