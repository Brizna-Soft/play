MovingLog

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Casual · Acción, Aventura, Carreras, Indie

A physics-based team party game for up to 12 players in four teams. One player hoists the log overhead while teammates ride on top, and where they stand decides how it tilts. Clear obstacles, ride a rival team's log to throw them off, and get your log and your whole team to the goal first.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

Three of you carry one log to the flag.
Sounds simple.

Steer the Log
The log runs on plain, honest physics. One person walks left, the log tilts left. When three people have three different ideas, the log faithfully reflects all three and goes absolutely nowhere. Start shouting. That's the control scheme.

The Course Is Not on Your Side
Moving platforms. Narrow bridges. None of this terrain was designed with three people steering a log in mind.
The log snags, bounces, and rolls away. Every time, your team has to find its rhythm from scratch.

Neither Are the Other Teams
Up to four teams run the same course at once. You can see exactly where the team ahead of you goes down, and you can hear the footsteps of the one behind.
First team to the flag wins. That's the whole game.
