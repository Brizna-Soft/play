De qué trata

Angler's Lake is a casual, cozy fishing game. Relax as you catch common, rare and epic fish through the stunning views of the lake. The game features short game sessions that reward you with in-game tokens you can use to upgrade your gear and participate in fun, low-stakes seasonal events and competitions.

FEATURES
• Easy mechanics that are fun to master. Start fishing right away! The more you do, the more you will be able to hone your skills and master the art of the hook.

• Upgradeable gear.

• Fishing sessions that respect your time. Short and stress free.

• Calm atmosphere accompanied by stunning visuals. Relax through the lake and take in its beautiful greenery and clean water.

• Online rankings, tournaments and seasonal events. Join in by playing the game! The top 5 players of each event ranking get unique in-game trophies.

• Lots of fish!
