Punch Your Way Out

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

You wake up in a tiny room. Every wall, floor and ceiling is solid cubes. Punch your way out. Smash blocks, vacuum the rubble, sell it for upgrades, find tools buried in the rock and grow some serious arms. Solo or with up to 3 friends, the daylight is out there somewhere.

Tráiler

De qué trata

There is no door. Start punching.
You wake up in a small room made of solid cubes. Walls, floor, ceiling, all of it. The only way out is through, one punch at a time.

Punch, collect, upgrade
Every punch chips the wall and knocks cubes loose. Vacuum up the rubble, sell it, and spend the cash on stronger fists, faster jabs and bigger hauls. The more you punch, the buffer your arms get.

Find what is buried in the rock
The walls are full of surprises. Dig up jackhammers, drills, dynamite and stranger things that help you tunnel faster, until they break or run out. Then it is back to the fists.

Bring your friends
Dig together in online co-op for up to 4 players with proximity voice chat. Split the work, race each other to the loot, and try not to stand next to the dynamite.

Break out
Somewhere out there, few hundred meters of rock away, there is daylight. Punch through the last cube and see what is waiting outside.

Features
• Fully destructible cube world, dig in any direction

• Rubble vacuum, selling and a deep upgrade tree

• Tools and items hidden in the walls

• Arms that grow stronger the more you punch

• Automation: robots that dig and collect for you

• Solo or online co-op for up to 4 players

• A breakout moment worth every punch
