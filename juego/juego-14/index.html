De qué trata

Over 10,000 downloads in its first week! Following the success of Can I Not Fall for Idols?(Aidame), creator duo MIKIMAKI presents their second otome game!
This game’s biggest feature is a unique system that lets you choose whether to make the characters hot… or leave them exactly the way they are.
You can experience each ending with the character design you like best.
The illustrated CGs, full voice acting, and comedy elements loved in the previous game are back and better than ever, now with even more story content and event scenes to enjoy!

You are a 20-year-old girl helping out at the cafe run by your parents.
But on your birthday, something unbelievable happens.
Your pet chinchilla suddenly starts talking.
“The customer who came in today is going to turn hot. Why not trust your gut and start dating him?”
And just like that, you begin a “trial relationship” with a guy you just met that very same day…
Will he actually turn hot? Or…!?

Sasuke Hirokawa
“W-Wow… This really feels like we’re actually dating…”
A kind-hearted college student and incredibly talented artist who often comes to the cafe to work on his illustrations.
Under the online name “Fatigue Fracture,” he creates artwork for the virtual singer fandom community.

Kagetora Inokuma
“I just want to wear the clothes I make. That’s all. There’s no deeper meaning.”
A fashionable gyaru guy attending a design school near the cafe.
He enjoys wearing the clothes he makes himself and identifies as male.

Retsu Anzai
“If only I were ten… or at least five years older…”
A younger boy who visits the cafe to research sweets and desserts in hopes of becoming a pastry chef.
Called "Re-kun"
Note: Features social interaction routes only (no romance paths).
