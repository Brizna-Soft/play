Dusty Relics

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

A cozy first-person archaeology game set in ancient Egypt. Dig through soft sand with your pickaxe, sweep the site with your radar to find buried relic fragments, then carry them to the pedestal and rebuild legendary statues piece by piece.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

Beneath the sands of Egypt, the great statues of the pharaohs lie shattered into thousands of pieces. Grab your pickaxe and radar, step into the tomb, and bring them back to life, one fragment at a time.

Dig, Find, Restore
Swing your pickaxe to carve through the sand, opening pits, tunnels and hidden chambers. Sweep the area with your archaeological radar to reveal fragments buried deep in the earth. Pick them up, carry them to the pedestal and snap each piece into place. Digging is fully free-form: the sand reshapes with every strike, and every path you open is your own discovery.

Build From the Ground Up
Every statue is rebuilt the way it was made: from the base upward. A glowing hologram shows the finished relic. Each piece you place brings the stone closer to its true form, and when the final fragment clicks into place, the statue rises whole again.

3,377 Pieces, Eleven Legendary Relics
Restore the Khepri Scarab, the Pharaoh Bust, the Apis Bull, the Great Sphinx, Ramses II, Horus, Anubis, Thoth, Bastet, Sekhmet and Sobek. In total, 3,377 handcrafted fragments lie buried across the game: Anubis, Bastet, Ramses, the Great Sphinx, Sobek and Thoth each consist of 375 pieces, Horus has 381, Sekhmet 373, Apis 149, the Pharaoh Bust 125 and Khepri 99. Each relic waits in its own hall of the tomb, from torch-lit workshops to columned halls where sunlight filters through.

Uncover Hidden Objects
The tomb hides more than statue fragments. Concealed objects reward those who dig carefully and scan every corner. Search each hall thoroughly; who knows what else lies beneath the sand.

Grow Your Expedition
Every relic you complete earns you gold. Spend it on upgrades across three categories: Digging, Logistics and Restoration.
