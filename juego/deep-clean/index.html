Deep Clean

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie, Terror

A co-op horror comedy for up to 4 players. You're a crew of space janitors: scrub stations after "incidents" and find out what roams the halls and how to survive it. There are no instructions: try everything, log your discoveries in the glossary and piece together the truth.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

There's been an "incident" on the station. The company sent a crew, a van, a mop and a bucket. Whatever caused the incident is still there. The contract doesn't mention that.

Something lives on these stations. How many of them there are, what they want and how to deal with them, the company won't say. You'll have to find out yourselves.

Get closer or run? Make noise or freeze? Mop it or leave it alone? Nobody will give you the right answers: test your guesses, get it wrong, come back and try something else.

Everything you investigate is written down in the glossary. Page by page, you'll piece together the truth about what's really going on aboard these stations.

Scrub stains, bag trash, pack up bodies and hunt for valuables. You get paid for cleanliness, and the station is rebuilt before every landing. Hotel, ice, flood, infection: each site has its own rules and its own surprises.

The quota grows every week. Fines, client reviews and company memos included. Miss the quota and you get a layoff notice, and the run starts over.

Proximity voice chat, ragdolls, wet floors you slip on at a run, a roulette table where you can bet anything on zero, and an LED visor instead of a face: smiling is part of the uniform.
