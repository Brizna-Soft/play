De qué trata

Can a monster learn to love, and be loved in return?

On Love, On Monsters is a Frankenstein-inspired story with an emphasis on love in its many forms. Throughout the game, you play as three characters with alternating POVs; The Doctor, The Priest, and The Exile. Over the course of ten in-game months, foster bonds amongst the three protagonists and a delightful cast of townspeople, some of which seek to ruin your grand, but taboo plans. Through the melding of science, heart, and soul, you must avoid suspicion and bring to life a young girl monster. The key, you'll find, is forged in the name of love.

FEATURES:
• A choice-based narrative with multiple endings

• A varied cast of characters, each of whom possess the opportunity to help or hinder your story

• Enjoy a gothic drama with moments of tension and levity alike

• Support a duo team of queer and trans creators, who are responsible for the music, art, writing, coding, and production of On Love, On Monsters!
