Starwinder

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie · Acción, Indie

A vertical-scrolling shoot-em-up. Each run takes you through 7 of 14 worlds, every stage ends with a mini boss with its own signature move, and a multi-part final boss waits at the end. Local co-op, Remote Play Together, Daily Challenge and Boss Rush.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Starwinder is an original Amiga game that's coming to PC - a vertical-scrolling shoot-em-up. The Amiga version comes out on the same launch date.

Fly. Fight. Survive.

Take command of a lone starship and blast your way through 14 worlds, each with its own terrain, music and enemies: a frozen void, volcanic fortresses, an arctic outpost, coral depths, an orbital station, crystal caverns, an alien hive, desert ruins, a toxic swamp, a storm sky city, asteroid mines, a clockwork foundry, a haunted necropolis and a neon city.

Every campaign draws 7 of the 14 worlds at random, in a new order each time. The game gets harder as the run goes on: the first stage is always the gentlest and the last the toughest, whichever worlds you get.

Every Enemy Fights Differently

Each world has seven kinds of flying enemy and its own turret, and every one of them has its own attack and its own projectile: 112 in all. You'll face braided stingers, homing missiles, lobbed acid, spike mines, lightning strikes, sweeping beams and expanding rings with a gap to slip through. The dangerous attacks show a warning before they hit, so even a busy screen stays readable.

14 Mini Bosses, One Final Boss

Every stage ends with a mini boss, and each has a signature move. The Magma Colossus leaps at you and lands with a shockwave. The Pharaoh Sentinel splits into three mirages, and only the real one casts a shadow. Sentinel Prime reels you in with a tractor beam, and the Megacorp Enforcer sweeps a laser fence down the screen. After the last stage, a multi-limbed alien creature descends: rip off its claws, weapons and engines as it moves through three increasingly aggressive phases.

Score Chasing

Chain kills together to build a score multiplier up to x8.
