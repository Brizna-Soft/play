De qué trata

Obey Me! Sinner’s Choice: Lucifer is a fantasy romance visual novel set in Hell where you make one single choice at the very end of the story.
You are tasked with judging a mysterious man you meet in the underworld. But will your seven days together change the life-altering choice you are forced to make?
Experience a forbidden love in this dark romance story.
Gameplay
A story-driven visual novel, you will progress by reading the narrative and interacting with characters.
Over the course of your seven days in Hell, you will:
• Meet Lucifer and his brothers.

• Observe their behavior and relationships.

• Uncover the truth behind the secrets of the underworld and the brothers’ past.

However, at the end of the seven days, you will be presented with a single, final choice.
Your decision here will decide the outcome of the story.

Key Features
• A new, darker tale of sin and love from NTT Solmare and the team that brought you the popular mobile otome game, Obey Me!

• A massive story with 15 chapters
A deep and complex seven-day story where love meets sin.

• Immersive, fully voiced stories
Emotional, emotive performances that capture even the subtlest breath.

• One single choice that will decide the fate of the demon you love
Step into the role of a human magistrate, selected to judge Lucifer for his sins.

• Beautifully rendered, high-definition visuals
Experience a dark romance that is as cruel as it is beautiful.

Story

Cast into the prison of Cocytus, the fallen Seraph and eldest of seven brothers, Lucifer, faces his reckoning.
Within his soul, a war rages: Is he an angel of light, or a demon of the dark?
The verdict is yours to give.
At the end of all things, you will be faced with a single, irreversible choice.
