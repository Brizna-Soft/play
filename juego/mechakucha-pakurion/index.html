**About This Game**

This is a chaotic multiplayer hide‑and‑seek party game designed for online matches with your friends.
Take turns playing as hiders or seekers, and use unique special abilities to outsmart each other.

**Core Gameplay**
Hiders must stay concealed and avoid being caught before the round timer runs out.
Seekers have to track down and capture every hidden player to win the round.
Every match can be reshaped by player‑driven rules and powerful special skills.

**Key Game Features**
✅ **Democratic in‑match voting system**
All players inside the lobby can cast votes mid‑round. Votes can change game settings, swap team roles or trigger special match events. Majority vote decides what happens next.

✅ **Fully customisable match rules**
Tweak almost every gameplay parameter: round duration, ability cooldowns, hider‑seeker team ratios and win‑loss conditions. Save your favourite rule presets for quick‑start lobbies.

✅ **Shadow‑clone decoy ability**
Spawn clone copies of yourself to confuse seekers, create distractions and improve your survival chance as a hider.

✅ **Optical‑camouflage stealth ability**
Activate optical camouflage to turn yourself hard to spot for a short window. Perfect for hiders to reposition or slip away from incoming pursuers.

✅ **Online private lobbies**
Host private lobbies and invite your friends to play with your custom rules.

**Game Details**
‑ Online multiplayer private lobby support
‑ Unique special abilities for both hider and seeker teams
‑ Highly‑tunable round balance options
‑ Casual, party gameplay for group entertainment
