MyDear.exe

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

Celebrate love with Ellie and Lucien in MyDear.exe. A short and cute game that takes place in the folders of an old PC. In the shoes of Ellie, play the game offered by Lucien after a long life together. Humor, memories and love are on the menu of this short ~2 hours experience.

Tráiler

De qué trata

MyDear.exe is a cute and tiny game about two lovely elders living in Bordeaux, France 🍷🇫🇷
Play as Ellie and spend around 2 hours exploring the game that her husband Lucien developed for her in the old family computer to celebrate their 57 years together! Souvenirs, photographs, sweet notes, narrative mini-games... use everything you find to solve the puzzles he prepared especially for her ❤️💻

57 years together is something! To celebrate it Lucien has transformed an old PC into a game inspired by their life together. Find their memories in the form of photos, drawings, love words... anything!

Everyone loves games, especially Ellie.
In MyDear.exe, Lucien created several mini-games inspired by them and their pets ♡

What would a game be without puzzles?
Find clues in the memories of our cute couple, scattered here and there in the folders of the computer, and solve puzzles created with love.

In MyDear.exe you'll find:
• A cute little game from up to 2 hours, to enjoy with a hot beverage.

• An interactive love letter celebrating a full life spent together.

• Puzzles that will gently put your sense of deduction to the test.

• Narrative mini-games inspired by Ellie and Lucien's favorite games.

• Find out more about Clochette the street cat and Hoshisan the "good boy"!

What you won't find in MyDear.exe:
• AI generated assets. We didn't use any AI tools to create this game.

• A complete computer simulation.

• A demanding game, the experience requires some skill but remains a quiet moment for everyone.

• A long story with lots of characters, Ellie and Lucien are at the center of the adventure.

We're a small studio of two (and a few freelance friends ♡) who wanted to stop working on big and long games for years.
