MEATRASH

Juego de PC · Aventura, Casual, Indie · Aventura, Indie, Simulación

In this city, implant contracts outlive their owners. In an underground processing facility, dissolve the "waste" delivered each morning and recover the implants buried inside. A first-person work sim. Deliver them for pay, or borrow a few for yourself and others?

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

Process the "waste" the city spits out every morning.
Unidentified. Illegally modified. Wrong spec. Some of it still has valuable parts buried inside.
You are a former body-mod doctor who lost your clinic. In a small plant built into the old city's storm drains, you drop the waste into the vat, dissolve it, and pick out the parts that are left.

Organic matter has no value

Take in the waste, drop it into the processing vat, and dissolve the organic matter. Deliver what you recover and get paid for it.

Waste with nothing inside goes to disposal. Processing costs money too, so work efficiently.
Once you know the job, switch to a contractor who brings you more waste.

Deliver it, or borrow it
Your contract says every recovered implant gets delivered. But feed one to the dismantler and you get materials for your own use.
Turn materials into requested items at the fabricator, or use them on your own body at the surgery station.
Weigh your income against investing in yourself.

Upgrade your plant and your body.
Add and upgrade equipment to raise the quality and volume of your work.
You can upgrade your own implants too.

Keep processing waste. Get closer to your real goal.
Your allies send requests to the office terminal.
Complete them alongside your daily work, and claim a small measure of revenge.
