KEYSMASH

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Free to Play · Indie

KEYSMASH is a keyboard fidget toy: mash any key and watch it bloom into color, shape, and sound. Play through 24 hand-crafted worlds, from Bauhaus galleries to cherry blossoms to a crashing stock ticker, and earn 100 honest achievements. No skill, no fail. Just vibes

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Mash anything. Make something beautiful.
KEYSMASH is a keyboard fidget toy. There's no wrong way to play: hammer the home row, drum with ten fingers, or tap one slow key at a time. Every keystroke blooms into color, shape, and sound. Then you look up and twenty minutes are gone.

24 hand-crafted worlds
Each level is its own visual instrument — a different look, a different sound, a different feel under your fingers.
• Studio — Bauhaus gallery, primary colors, big bold shapes.

• Blossom — cherry petals drifting on every tap.

• Mondrian — build the painting one keystroke at a time.

• Ink — sumi-e brushstrokes across the paper.

• Kaleidoscope — symmetry that folds the whole keyboard into a mandala.

• Ticker — a stock market that rallies and crashes as you type.

• ...and 18 more, ending in a hidden finale.

Made for
• Anyone who fidgets, doodles, or needs their hands busy while they think.

• Achievement hunters who want a short, honest, 100% that actually means something.

• Mechanical keyboard lovers who just want to hear it sing.

No timers. No fail states. No pressure. Just you and a keyboard that finally does something beautiful.
