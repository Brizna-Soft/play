De qué trata

Voxel Playground is a fully destructible VR physics sandbox, you can enjoy the game without VR!

Survive the night
Craft a base and hold off zombie waves, or fight guards, drones, and prison squads. Reloading is physical, and bodies break apart.

Sandbox
Skip the scenario and use the sandbox. Spawn physics objects and change the rules when you just want to smash the room.
