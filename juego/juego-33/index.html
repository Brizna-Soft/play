六年级奶瓦聚会

Juego de PC · Casual

Grade Six Naiwa Gathering is a relaxing and casual jigsaw puzzle game. Featuring 3x3, 4x4, and 5x5 difficulty options and an auto-save function, players can restore the heartwarming scene of the Naiwas' dinner party through simple clicks in a cozy and joyful atmosphere.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

About This Game Grade Six Naiwa Gathering is a relaxing and casual jigsaw puzzle game. Players need to piece together scrambled image tiles to restore a heartwarming scene of the Naiwas having a lively dinner party.
Key Features
• Relaxing & Stress-Free: Simple and intuitive controls, suitable for players of all ages to enjoy a relaxing time while solving the puzzle.

• Multiple Difficulty Levels: The game offers three different puzzle difficulties to meet the needs of various players:
• 3 × 3 Easy Seating (9 pieces): Perfect for a quick and easy experience.

• 4 × 4 Dinner Time (16 pieces): A moderate challenge that brings puzzle-solving fun.

• 5 × 5 One More Plate (25 pieces): Slightly more brain-teasing, challenging your observation skills and patience.

• Player-Friendly Features:
• Replayable: Restart anytime, anywhere, and challenge your fastest completion record.

• Auto-Save: The game automatically saves your current puzzle progress, allowing you to "Continue" anytime without worrying about losing your progress.

• Cozy Visuals: Features cute "Naiwa" characters and a lively dinner party scene with bright, warm colors that make you feel joyful and cozy.

Come join this lively gathering, use your patience and observation skills, and slowly piece together this warm dinner party!
