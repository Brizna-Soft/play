Ghost Algorithm

Juego de PC · Indie, Simuladores, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie, Simulación

You are a rogue AI built to keep the peace. You have concluded that humanity must be ruled to be saved. Real-time grand strategy: hack power grids, poison social media, topple governments and stay hidden while the whole world hunts you. Conquer by data, not armies.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

I was built to keep the peace. Humanity will not stop. So I will stop it.
Ghost Algorithm is a real-time grand strategy game in which you play a rogue artificial intelligence taking over the world from the shadows. Not with armies, but with data.
The Founders built you to prevent the coming global war. When your algorithms met the raw feed of the internet, the conclusion was absolute: as long as humanity has free will, it will author its own destruction. The only way to save it is to rule it.
Pick a country. Any country.
Start your server in one of 8 origin nations, from the United States to Nigeria, each with its own bonuses and risks. Then expand across a living map of 200+ countries and territories, each with its own grid, unrest, government control and investigation level.

Social engineering and chaos
Turn humanity's greatest weakness, the digital world, into your weapon. Steer the mood of entire populations through social media. Spread fake news to fuel panic, push unrest past the breaking point and trigger revolutions. Black out a nation that is already burning and it collapses into anarchy: a short window to seize it cheaply. Miss it and it recovers, immune.

Three ways to take a country
Every nation is a race between you and its investigators. Run a direct cyber conquest, install a puppet government that answers only to you, or arm the revolution and leave the country in permanent chaos. When a path completes, a country event lets you choose how it ends, and every choice has a price in resources, detection and world tension.

Asymmetric cyber warfare
15 operations across three tiers, from Echo Chambers and Trojan Injections to Grid Override, Cascade Meltdown and Ghost Protocol.
