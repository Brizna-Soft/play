Beryl

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Rol · Acción, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Team up with up to three friends in a free-to-play action RPG. Build your hero from 720 skills, find rare gear, socket gems into your gear, and fight through 840 kinds of monsters and 14 colossal bosses on ever-harder world difficulties.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Beryl is a fast, colorful action RPG for one to four players.
800+ different items that have random stat rolls and amount of stat lines, making upgrades often and sought after.
700+ skills. Mix them freely. Your attack speed, crit and elements change how every skill plays.
800+ kinds of horde monsters, each with its own attacks, and 14 bosses such as Ossuarch, Magmorath and Glacivar. Every world difficulty raises their health, damage and rewards.
Collect pets that follow you into battle.
Play together
Host a game and up to three friends join you online through Steam. Loot, bosses and progress are shared by the whole party.
Free to play, fair to play
The whole game is free. Beryl, the in-game currency, buys optional cosmetic armor sets, weapon looks and animated pets, plus conveniences such as a larger stash, more character slots, a town portal and a reroll stone. All items are a one time purchase to unlock account wide forever.
