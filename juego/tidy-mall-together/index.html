Tidy Mall Together

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

A cosy co-op tidying game. Sort + 7000 runaway things back onto the shelves of 18 shops with up to 3 friends. Grab, stack and shelve, unlock magnets, scanners and sorting robots, hunt 25 golden items, play mini games and turn the messiest mall in town into the tidiest.

Tráiler

De qué trata

The messiest mall in town needs you.
Pick it up. Sort it. Shelf by shelf.
Somebody let the whole mall fall apart. Sneakers are in the arcade, teddy bears are in the music shop and there's a guitar in the food court. +7000 things are scattered across 18 shops, and every one of them has a home on the right shelf.

Grab them, stack them, carry them back and watch the shelves fill up, one satisfying row at a time. Bring friends and it turns into a happy, chaotic team effort.

Better together
• Online co-op for up to 4 players. Share a room code and tidy the mall together.

• Your own progress. Each player earns their own money and buys their own upgrades.

• Never lose your crew. Friends' names show through walls, so you always know who's where.

• Pick your look. 14 characters, from shoppers and gym fans to a certain man in a red suit.

A whole mall to fix
• 16 shops: Grocery, Arcade, Fashion, Electronics, Music, Sports, Toys & Gifts, Outdoor Gear, Florist, Burger Bar and the Food Court.

• +7000 things to sort, from teddy bears to bowling balls, each with its own spot on the shelf.

• Deliveries: the truck keeps bringing new stock, so there's always more to put away.

• 25 golden items hidden around the mall for sharp-eyed tidiers.

• Sets, goals and a collection book to fill, and a big finale when the last item is home.

Upgrades and helpers
• Bigger Arms and the Shopping Trolley: carry more kinds of items at once.

• Item Magnet: pulls matching items into your stack.

• Item Scanner: reveals nearby items of the kind you're holding.

• Sorting Robots: little helpers that waddle around and shelve things for you.

• Shop Manager: every finished shop earns you money each minute.

• Style Pass: a cap, a top hat, then a golden crown, so everyone knows who the best tidier is.
