An Occult-Horror Adventure Lurking Beyond the Case File
Resequence: The Orchard is a multi-perspective occult-horror adventure game about an otherworldly presence slowly eroding everyday life, featuring unsettling visuals by fracoco and a story by Re,AER.

Players would play the role of Kisuke Saito and uncover the students’ past by hearing their experiences. Reveal the truth of his wife’s missing and eventually bring out what lies beneath the record—what’s haunted “there”.

❖━━━━━━━━━━━❖

Feeling extremely hard to move on after his wife’s missing and drained of the will to live, Kisuke Saito—encouraged by his colleague Aislinn and a high school girl Kaneko Honda—accepted the job as a school counselor. But every student who comes to him is plagued by inexplicable phenomena…

❖━━━━━━━━━━━❖

◇◆Kisuke Saito

A psychiatrist still unable to move on from his wife’s disappearance ten years ago.
Introduced by Aislinn, he serves as her assistant in her work as a school counselor.
Despite being a counselor, he is aloof and cynical.
He may say he wants nothing to do with kids, but in the end, he can’t bring himself to leave them be.

◇◆Honda Kaneko

A languid high school girl who keeps coming up to Kisuke Saito.
For some reason, she had been in contact with him even before he came to her school as a counselor.
She rarely shows much emotion and can come across as cool or even expressionless, but beneath that exterior, she has a rich inner life very much in keeping with her age.
She also has a mischievous side, teasing the people she is close to just to enjoy their reactions, with Kisuke Saito being her favorite target.

◇◆Aislinn

Her full name is Aislinn Conlon.
A brilliant, otaku-minded psychiatrist and a colleague of Kisuke Saito’s.
