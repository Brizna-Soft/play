Alienshire

Juego de PC · Indie, Estrategia, Free to Play · Estrategia, Indie

Command alien fighters in intense real-time battles. Use tactical pause to plan your moves, manage limited resources, and outsmart the enemy in short, replayable RTS scenarios.

De qué trata

Alienshire is a free tactical RTS featuring flying units, real-time combat, and short, replayable scenarios. Developed by a solo creator, it is an experimental take on classic RTS gameplay, focusing on small-scale battles.
Command individual units or entire groups, choose their orders and targets, and outmanoeuvre the enemy. Success depends on how you deploy and control your forces.
Tactical Scenarios
Four distinct tactical challenges, from beginner-friendly battles to extreme tests of strategy.
• Counterattack - EASY: Intercept enemy Fighters and destroy their Mothership.

• Skirmish - MEDIUM: Build your forces and defeat the enemy.

• Clash - HARD: Outsmart a powerful enemy army through positioning and tactics.

• Defence - INSANE: Defend your Mothership against growing waves of enemy reinforcements.

Features
• Produce combat and support units

• Command individual units or large groups

• Use tactical pause to issue orders without time pressure

• Optional automatic unit behaviour reduces the need for micromanagement

• Use the minimap to monitor the battlefield and quickly reposition the camera

• Fight across familiar landscapes occupied by alien forces

Controls
Alienshire's controls are similar to those in MegaGlest and other classic RTS games.
Select units with the left mouse button, issue orders with the right mouse button, and use tactical pause to plan your moves.
The Future of Alienshire
Alienshire has room to grow.
I would love to expand the game with new scenarios, units, and gameplay mechanics, all as free updates, not paid DLCs.
How far development continues will largely depend on player interest, feedback, and reviews.
