De qué trata

In Bloody Lens, you play as Ray, a young content creator who explores abandoned and haunted locations for his social media channel. This time, he ventures into a decaying psychiatric hospital where the line between reality and nightmare begins to fade.

As you explore the dark corridors and underground labyrinth beneath the hospital, you'll solve challenging puzzles, search for hidden clues, unlock sealed doors, and uncover the disturbing secrets buried within its walls. Armed with little more than your camera's light, an axe, and whatever ammo you can scavenge, you must navigate the darkness while the creatures in the shadows hunt you/
Every corner hides a mystery, and every recording could be your last. Sometimes, the decision to keep filming may put your life at risk. Will you survive the horrors of the asylum and reveal the truth before the darkness consumes you?

Long before the asylum was abandoned, it was controlled by the infamous Dr. Felo, a brilliant but ruthless scientist obsessed with pushing the limits of human experimentation. Hidden beneath the hospital, he conducted horrific and unethical experiments on patients, seeking to create the perfect human being.
But his most disturbing subjects were his own two children. Twisted by years of experiments, they became monstrous and diabolical beings that still roam the dark halls of the asylum. While Dr. Felo disappeared under mysterious circumstances, his children remain trapped within the hospital, protecting the secrets of their father's work.
As Ray explores deeper into the underground labyrinth, he gradually uncovers documents, recordings, and hidden laboratories that reveal the truth behind Dr. Felo's experiments.
