De qué trata

From cars to trains and massive ships!

Enjoy the satisfaction of cleaning by hand and the fun of upgrading. 2D Pixel Washing GameEquip yourself with better tools, automate cleaning tasks, and take on bigger targets!

🧽 Rub it gently, and it sparkles!
Spray water, apply foam, and scrub every nook and cranny. You can personally experience the refreshing sensation of dust and mud coming off to reveal the original color.

⚙️ With better tools, more conveniently!
Upgrade your tools and automate cleaning tasks one by one. While the nozzles and brushes are busy at work, get ready to tackle new cleaning targets.

🚗  Beyond cars, even trains and ships!
From long, sprawling trains to ships sailing the seas, the objects to be cleaned are getting bigger and bigger. Remove various types of grime with specialized tools, and take on special requests from occasional visitors!
