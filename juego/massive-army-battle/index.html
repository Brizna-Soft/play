De qué trata

Massive Army Battle (MAB) is a mass-battle simulator where you build two armies and let them clash. Just place the troops, press START, and the fight plays on its own. War horses crash, bombs explode, bodies are launched into the air, and crowds up to a thousand units at once. Release on Windows now and soon to release on Linux and Steam OS.
Play for free: the Campaign
• 15 levels to play for free. Each level gives you a budget. Spend it wisely

• Win a battle, collect money. Use it to boost the budget in harder levels

• Stuck on a level? Visit the FARM MONEY mini game

• Buy a war insurance to raise your war fund

• Play 1 vs 1 Local mode for free (limited budget)

• Play 1 vs 1 LAN (network) mode for free (limited budget)

• Unlock the full version by purchasing "The Sandbox Pack"

"1vs1 LAN" is in BETA
The 1 vs 1 LAN mode is in Beta! We have actually tested fully but please leave a little room for a little error while we gather bug reports and make it better. Give it a try and let us know how it runs!
More different units
There are Warriors, Muay Thai fighters, Karate masters, Sumo Wrestlers, Archers, Zombies, Vegetables that fight back, Bomb-Throwing Cats, Explosive Hyps (from INVASION), Healing Medics, Tuk-Tukanic, Tower, and Crystals that you must destroy to win, and many more.
Each unit fights in its own way: some are brave, some are cowardly, panicking, flanking, protecting, throwing bombs, leaping at the enemy. Learn how they work together.
Simple to learn, hard to master
1. Pick any units and place on the battlefield (Blue on the left, Red on the right)
2. Click the battlefield to place it (press F or click formations to adjust them)
