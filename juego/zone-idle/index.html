De qué trata

Raid, extract, upgrade, and go again, stronger.

Zone Idle is an incremental extraction simulator set in a ruthless world inspired by the extraction genre and book Roadside Picnic. Deploy into the Zone, survive whatever it throws at you, and get out with your haul, or lose everything where you fell. What you bring back funds everything else: better gear, a stronger hideout, deeper automation, and the long road to prestige. Your operative fights and loots on their own, so you run it at your own pace, hands-on or idle.

PREPARATION IS THE COMBAT

Fights resolve on numbers, not twitch. How a firefight goes comes down to your weapon, your armor, your buffs, and the artifacts you carry, every stat you locked in before you deployed. Miss a detail in the loadout and the Zone collects the difference.

So the real combat happens in the menu. Build the kit. Pack the injectors. Read the odds. The winner is not the one who aims better, but the one who prepares their loadout, understands the RNG, and aims for the best statistical advantage.

DEPLOY, SURVIVE, EXTRACT

Every raid is a string of one-off scenarios. Hazardous events surface as you go: firefights, anomalies, wounded stalkers, locked crates, and things worse than any of those. Each one is a decision. Spend the meds or save them. Fight or slip past. Radiation climbs the longer you linger and your health bleeds with it, so every second you spend chasing better loot is a second you might not walk out on.

What you carry out, you keep. Everything rides on a real grid you rotate, stack, and nest into containers, and you will fight for every slot, because the only backpack that counts is the one that makes it home.

HANDS ON, OR HANDS OFF

How much of this you actually do is up to you.
