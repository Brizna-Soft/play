Ann's Parlor

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Rol · Indie, Rol (RPG)

Awakened from years of slumber, the witch Ann returns to her old cabin, where visitors arrive seeking love, truth, or glimpses of fate. Through crystal-ball readings, you interpret their stories and guide their paths—every choice reshaping their destinies and the secrets that await within.

De qué trata

The Witch Awakens
Step into the role of Ann, a witch roused from her long slumber within a forgotten cabin. Through ancient rites of divination, you will guide lost souls who arrive seeking answers. Quietly, you will weave the threads of their fates—each encounter leading to dramatically different outcomes.
Touch the Threads of Destiny
Gently trace the mysterious symbols within the crystal ball and assemble them into unique divination results. As these elements converge beneath your fingertips, fragments of the visitor’s destiny rise to the surface, forming the opening chapter of the guidance you will offer.
Prophecies That Shape Lives
At every crossroads, you choose whether to illuminate their path with hope or temper their desires with caution. Each ripple born from your prophecy may shift the flow of their fate, steering them toward vastly different endings.
Unmask the Heart’s True Self
Every visitor carries a carefully crafted mask, their words hiding unspoken fears and intentions. Only your insight can pierce the veil and uncover the truths buried deep within—and those truths are the key to the final revelation behind the cabin and all who enter it.
Features
• Divination Puzzles – Piece together fragments within the crystal ball to form destiny-shaping readings.

• Interactive Storytelling – Listen to each visitor’s tale and explore multiple narrative outcomes.

• Choice-Driven Paths – Your interpretations and counsel shape not only their futures, but the ultimate fate of the cabin itself.
