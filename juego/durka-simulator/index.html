De qué trata

Story
"Durka Simulator" is a visual novel with humorous elements, set in an alternative reality in the Russian Federation. In this world, there are parallel universes. From one of these dimensions, there are medical professionals who are trying to take over the universe where the game takes place.

The main character, Mickey, finds himself in the middle of these events by chance. Now, he and his friends must save their home universe using a mysterious stone of truth.
