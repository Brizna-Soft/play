About
Enter the dungeon. Fight powerful foes. Die. Upgrade. Repeat. Survidle Knight is an idle RPG dungeon crawler with survivor-like elements, and no input during gameplay. Depending on how frequently you interact with the game, you can expect at least a day of game time, and upwards of a week.
Upgrades
Between runs, spend your earned gems on upgrades to improve every facet of the Survidle Knight, from the basics like health and damage, to increasing the rarity of your in-game level up upgrades and decreasing your maximum ability count to concentrate those upgrades.
Features
• Fight through a never ending dungeon with 8 unique weapons

• 26 Enemies, plus one epic boss fight to culminate the Survidle Knight's adventure

• Choose from 50 upgrades, many with multiple ranks to buff and specialize your fighting style

• Go from struggling to defeat rats and spiders to slaying dragons in the blink of an eye

• Steam achievements and in-game stats
