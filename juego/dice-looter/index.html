Dice Looter

Juego de PC · Estrategia · Estrategia

Roll dice to get items, combine equipment, and fight enemies. Defeated a boss? Now your hero will take its place. Dice Looter is a roguelike auto-battler with dice-rolling mechanics.

De qué trata

Roll the dice to collect loot, combine your gear, and discover powerful builds in this roguelike auto-battler.
You are a brave warrior who challenges enemies. You have it all: courage, valor, unmatched strength, even charisma… The only thing you lack is luck. Isn’t that so? Your goal is victory, your task is to choose the best equipment, and your tool is the dice.

Roll the dice, level up your hero, find powerful combinations  and synergistic builds, and choose the weapon that suits you best. It could be a heavy and mighty spear, or a light and swift dagger. You’ll have a wide selection of items to choose from. Each time, you’ll face new enemies. Every encounter is a fresh challenge, so it’s wise to keep a backup set of gear in your inventory.

Defeat enemies to move forward. I bet you won’t encounter two identical enemies. At the end, a boss awaits. Just be careful - after defeating him, you might unwittingly take his place.

Forge your path to victory. Along the way, you’ll meet regular foes, elite enemies, a blacksmith, a merchant, or even some treasure chests.  Defeat elite enemies and earn 'skulls' to unlock new abilities. Discover new heroes with unique perks, unlock new skills and curses that will make each run more challenging.

• Automatic battles

• Gain items by rolling dice

• 7 unique characters

• 30 unique skills

• 15 modifiers and curses that change the rules of each run

• Procedurally generated items - you won’t find two identical items, except for starting items

• Enemies adapt to your hero’s strength

• Short 30–40 minute runs

• Simple and engaging gameplay
