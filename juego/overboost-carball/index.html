Overboost: Carball

Juego de PC · Acción, Deportes, Free to Play · Acción, Deportes

Car football for up to six teams at once. Boost, jump and dodge into the ball in an arena that reshapes itself for every team count. Take on bots, play four to a screen on gamepads or one keyboard, or go online with a code. Free to play. The whole world is ray-marched from signed distance fields.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Car football with boost, jumps and dodges. Drive into the ball, fly after it, and knock it into the other team's goal: against bots, with friends on one screen, or online.
Two to six teams
The arena reshapes itself for every team count, from a classic two-goal pitch up to six teams at once. Up to three cars a side, and bots - easy, medium or hard - take every seat nobody is in. Every match rolls a fresh layout of boost pads and pickups.
Drive like it's football
Boost across the pitch, jump to meet the ball, and jump again in the air with a direction held to dodge into it. Drift on the ground, roll in the air, and follow either your car or the ball with the camera.
Play together
• Up to four players in split-screen, each on a gamepad or their own strip of one shared keyboard.

• Online: share a session code with a friend, or let Quick match find an open game

• LAN: host and find games on your network

• Everyone against everyone, or all the humans on one team against the bots

Power-ups
Homing rockets and mines freeze whoever they catch, a shield keeps you moving through it all, and a trapped pickup leaves its collector dizzy.
Day and night
Play under a blue sky, or at night under floodlights and a starfield.
Made of maths, not meshes
No meshes, no textures: the entire world is ray-marched from signed distance fields, at a resolution that adapts to hold your frame rate.
