Push your luck and beat the odds in this roguelike deckbuilder that always shows your chances of failing. How much are you willing to risk? Survive and thrive in a vibrant eco-dystopia … or don’t. ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ Gather supplies and grow your community in the post-apocalypse. Do you feel lucky, Punk? 💀

De qué trata

Lucky Punk is a roguelike deckbuilder built for people who don’t like gambling but love taking risks and owning their decisions. Every turn comes with one simple decision: DRAW another card and push your luck – or STOP and play it safe. Where most card battlers hide randomness or over-use RNG, Lucky Punk always shows you the odds. In a game where every choice matters, every decision in Lucky Punk is transparent, every risk is yours, and well, every failure? Completely deserved. 💀

DRAW… OR STOP
So, you're in the post-apocalypse gathering supplies, and you gotta fight enemies by playing a card game. You start with a deck of nine cards, and the only strategy you need to care about is: ⭐DAMAGE⭐ versus 🙁EFFORT🙁.

Each round, you do one of two things:
#1. Draw a card. You get the Damage and Effort points. The card abilities play out, you know how it is. 🤷
#2. Stop and end the round. You 💢hit the enemy equal to the Damage you’ve got. Then the enemy hits you and we go to the next round.
And if you ever get too much Effort? You exhaust yourself. You lose all the Damage you’ve built up and the round ends with only you getting a punch in the face! 👊🏻 We do this until they’re down or you’re out. Got it? That’s it! That’s the game.
FAST RUNS. HARD CHOICES
It’s a fast-paced turn-based roguelite built for quick, high stakes runs, constant tension and instant emotional outcomes that will either have you win or fail spectacularly.
After all, the game always tells you how risky your next draw is. You can even check what's in your deck at any time. It's a game about managing luck, but with perfect information. There's no card counting required! Unless you’re into that, you sicko.
