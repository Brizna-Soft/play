Cow Online World

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Multijugador masivo · Indie, Rol (RPG)

Design your own animal self and meet other creatures in this colourful MMO! Graze with your friends and level skills like Basket Weaving, Trivia, Kindness, and more in a world that humans have left behind.

De qué trata

Features
• Extensive character creation that lets you customize every part of your body! Be a Cow, a Deer, a Cat, a Whale, or combine your animal's features freely.

• Tons of varied skills that enhance your life as a fun-loving creature! Like grazing! Or basket weaving! Or trivia! Some skills will help you in conversations, while others may let you build an instrument or increase your storage space.

• Play working instruments! Put on a concert or jam with your friends!

• Collect clothes and assemble your perfect outfit!

• Ride the bus to the nearby ruined city and explore!

• Feed sunflower seeds to queer pigeons!

Notes from the developer:
The main thing I like in MMOs is how they can act as sort of permanent online public spaces I can chose to exist in whether I have friends online or not. This is something I've missed playing lobby-based multiplayer games. In making Cow Online World I'm aiming to create a safe space on the internet that's always there for people to exist in.
Cow Online World has no subscription costs, buying the game here is what lets me keep it running and updated.
Cow Online World is an MMO primarily made by one developer (though with plenty of help from friends and family). As such there will be some server downtime, and updates will launch in pieces.

Cow Online World is primarily made for mouse + keyboard. Controllers are supported for around ~80% of gameplay, but a mouse will be needed to navigate some menus and a keyboard is needed to chat, answer trivia questions, and play piano.

No AI was used in the creation of Cow Online World and it never will be. (Including no AI-assisted coding)
