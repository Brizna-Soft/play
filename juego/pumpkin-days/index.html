OVERVIEW
Pumpkin Days is a traditional farming dating rpg. Play the game at your own pace with a variety of activities to explore and do. Side with the townsfolk and help stop Jounce Corp. from building a factory on the island by filling out the museum. Or side with the factory and get rich fast, but with some consequence.

FARMING
When you arrive the mayor will put you straight to work farming on your new farm. There are over 90 crops and over 60 flowers that can be grown.

PICK ONE OF THREE TOWNS TO LIVE
Start your farming adventure by picking one of three towns to live in. Will you choose to live in a tropical paradise at Wahoo Beach, the simple river town Pumpkinvale, or high up in the snowy mountains of Diamond Falls? Both towns are populated with over 30 townsfolk to interact with. You can travel to all the towns and seek to buy property in them.

FISHING
With over 100 kinds of fish to catch there is much to discover.
