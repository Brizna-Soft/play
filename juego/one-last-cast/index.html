De qué trata

Place a fishing trip in a corner of the table.
The work has come to an end, and the fishing float has just started to move. The orange cat that accompanies you sits on the shore, and the underwater fish swim slowly - what will you catch this time?

"One Last Cast "is a pixel style desktop AFK fishing game. Let the fishing scene accompany you through daily life, watch the harvest and organize equipment when you want to rest, and then prepare for the next stop. You can fish slowly, or you can carefully consider throwing the rod next time for a desired fish.
Leisurely hanging up, there are also worth pondering next steps
Let the character automatically fish and come back to check the fish basket during busy breaks. Sell fish catches to accumulate coins, purchase fishing equipment, and gradually challenge more substantial goals. Different fishing spots, fish species, and water layers require suitable bait and equipment; Find the right combination to add more anticipation to the wait.
Follow the fishing float to different places

From West Lake in Hangzhou to Lake Biwa, and then to distant lakes and wetlands, each stop has different scenery, fish species, and local commissions. Get to know the fish here and collect anecdotes that belong here, making each stop a memory on the journey.
Collect fish species and wait for unexpected surprises

Gradually light up the fish encyclopedia, break your weight record, and also pay attention to those rare and colorful fish. The next time you harvest, it may be a familiar old friend, or it may be a harvest worth stopping to appreciate.
