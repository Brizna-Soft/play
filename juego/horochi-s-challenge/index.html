Horochi's Challenge

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Free to Play · Indie

Change gravity in six directions and guide crystal orbs to their goals in this single-player 3D puzzle game. Choose any of 100 stages from the start. EXTRA HARD adds XOR gates, water controls and floating platforms in larger mazes. Team up with Meguru and Bimba to earn minimum-move medals!

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Horochi's Challenge is a single-player 3D puzzle game that combines pushing crystal orbs with changing gravity. Guide the orbs to their goals, connect the power circuits, and bring the computers to life.Choose your floor. Change gravity in six directions.

Look around each stage with the mouse, then choose which of its six faces should become the floor. Orbs and characters fall toward the new gravity direction.

Push an orb first, or change gravity first? Use fixed blocks, rail platforms, and moving pillars to work out the right order of actions.All 100 stages selectable from the start

Choose from NORMAL 01–50, HARD 51–75 and EXTRA HARD 76–100 across five worlds: grassland, forest, town, castle and demon castle.

No completion requirement stands between you and EXTRA HARD. Try a tough puzzle first, or switch stages when you get stuck. Separate uncleared, cleared and minimum-move medal markers track your progress.EXTRA HARD: XOR gates, water levels and larger 3D mazes

Take on 25 additional puzzles, with five in each world. Plan switch states, water levels, character positions and gravity changes to navigate larger mazes.

• XOR gates open when exactly one of their two switches is ON. Both ON or both OFF closes the gate, so choose what to leave on each switch. An occupied gate stays open until it is clear.

• Water controls: stand a character on a water control panel and choose low, middle or high water. Floating platforms carry the characters and orbs riding on them up or down.

• Floating platforms follow vertical guides and keep their height when gravity changes. Each water-level change counts as one move; ceilings or fixed obstacles can block it.

• Cutaway view: select a layer to inspect the maze without using a move.
