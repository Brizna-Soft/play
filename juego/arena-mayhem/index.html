De qué trata

Arena Mayhem is a fast-paced fantasy melee arena game where a well-timed block, kick or trap can turn a fight around. Pick a warrior, grab equipment and battle in Free For All or Team Deathmatch. Play online or practise offline against AI opponents.
FIGHT WITH THE ARENA
Trade sword, axe and mace attacks, block incoming blows and kick opponents out of position. Use rotating blades, wall spikes, floor traps and collapsing bridges to turn the terrain against your rivals.
A DIFFERENT BATTLEGROUND
Fight across procedurally generated Nature, Lava and Ice arenas. Climb ledges, cross wooden bridges and hunt for weapons, helmets and armor as the battle unfolds.
YOUR WARRIOR, YOUR TEAM
Choose from ten stylized warriors. Six are free; four are optional paid characters with the same gameplay capabilities. Create a persistent team of up to six players and compete together in Team Deathmatch.
CHARACTER EVOLUTION — IN DEVELOPMENT
A preview of the visual progression planned for our warriors. Arthur’s concept shows five stages, from a lean fighter to a battle-hardened champion. Concept artwork; final in-game appearance may change.
