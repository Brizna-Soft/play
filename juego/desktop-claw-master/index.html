De qué trata

🐾 A shy friend is here
Let me introduce Graboo, the world's most timid claw machine tongs.
Graboo wants to make friends, but she's always alone because she doesn't have the courage. But it can be different when you're around! While you're working or playing games, Graboo plucks up the courage to lower the tongs.

✨ Small claw machine on the desktop
Graboo is always there when you're working, watching videos, or playing other games.
In full Idle mode, Graboo can let you pick your own doll, or click directly to make friends faster.

🧸 the pleasure of collecting and decorating
Diverse Doll Friends: Fill in the drawing board by picking out numerous dolls with different charms.

Graboo Customization: A doll-picked collection of points can make a timid Graboo look glamorous and cute. Which hat looks best on you?

💌 "Make Graboo a Friend!"
Although Graboo is a loner, she won't be lonely anymore thanks to the dolls you picked for her. Please set up a small space for Graboo in the corner of your desktop right now.
