De qué trata

The Charity Shop is an atmospheric, surreal puzzle game where you use your camera to capture the essence of a life lived.
To this end, you have been delivered to The Charity Shop, a place where people donate unwanted belongings—each carrying an imprint of the time they once shared.

Clutch at permanence.
Kick your feet against the rising tide.
When your head is under, it will be over.
Features
• Use your digital camera to take photographs capturing the essence of a life lived, through objects once owned.

• Uncover and interpret your own meaning behind these objects by solving their puzzles.

• Preserve these memories forever. Someone needs to remember...

The Charity Shop has around 1.5 to 2.5 hours of gameplay.
