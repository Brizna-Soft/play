Yukikaki: Snow Plow Simulator

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

Clear the snow one satisfying shovelful at a time, alone or with up to three friends. Start with a single shovel in a snowy Japanese town and end up clearing airport runways. Snow piles up where you throw it and banks up in front of your plow. Your pace: no time limits, no fuel, no tool wear.

De qué trata

The crunch of a shovel biting into fresh snow, the roar of a rotary plow blasting it high over the roadside, and a quiet street left behind you. After a night of heavy snow, a small town in Japan's snow country is buried. Nayu, twenty and happiest under the kotatsu with a game, gets sent next door with a shovel to dig out grandma's steps. It turns out she is good at it. Before long the whole town is calling: the bakery, the school, the station, the mountain road and finally the airport runway, each job with a bigger machine than the last. And you don't have to dig alone: up to three friends can join you.SNOW THAT STAYS WHERE YOU PUT IT
• Every shovelful counts: snow lands right where you throw it, and what you push gathers in front of the blade and spills off the sides. You can watch the yard open up with every pass.

• Every snow feels different: light fresh powder, heavy wet snow, the hard-packed banks a plow leaves behind and slick ice. Crack packed snow with an ice chipper swung over your head, scatter salt to melt the ice, and feel your truck slide on it until the chains go on. Every new machine changes how you work.

• Your pace: the clock only moves when you work. The sun sinks a little with every job, and when night falls you head home and curl up under the kotatsu. No time limits, no fuel, no tool wear, no fines.

FROM SHOVEL TO RUNWAY
• Bigger machines, bigger jobs: start with a shovel and a pusher, then work your way up through snow blowers, an ATV, a tractor, a wheel loader, a plow truck with a wing, a rotary plow that blasts snow far off the road and a runway sweeper. Fourteen tools and machines in all.
