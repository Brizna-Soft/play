Welcome to Puppy Poker

Juego de PC · Casual, Estrategia · Estrategia

Start with a deck where every card has a special effect, and 3 random events take effect each round. Adapt to the events and your hand, build the best poker hands and scores, and win 15 rounds in a row to become a Puppy Poker Legend. Have fun!

De qué trata

Unique Special Effect Mechanics
The game features 24 unique special effects, each with a mechanic of its own, greatly enriching the game's replayability and depth.

Endless Event Combinations
The game features 40 fun events, 3 of which are randomly picked each round to take effect for that round — endless possibilities.

Let Your Imagination Run Wild in Ever-Changing Games
With 3 randomly activated events each round combined with a unique random effect on every card, no two rounds — or even turns — can ever play out exactly the same. You'll have to adapt to the situation as it unfolds, putting together the highest-scoring hands and score combinations you can.
Strategic Economy Management
Each round you win, you earn Dog Food as a reward, which you can spend in the shop on items that have a special influence on your run. Some items permanently affect your deck, while others are one-time consumables — so you'll need to think a little about how best to spend your Dog Food to get the biggest impact on your run.

A Thrilling Difficulty System
To encourage players to think things through and make sure every turn actually matters — rather than just playing cards mindlessly — the game uses a Run Score system. Any points you score beyond the round's target are converted into Run Score, and if a later round falls short of its target, your Run Score covers the shortfall. This gives the game some forgiveness and means the big plays you made earlier never go to waste.
