De qué trata

CURSED PROFIT

You're not an employee. You're company property.

Cursed Profit is a 1–5 player co-op horror game where you and your crew are sent by the company to explore bizarre, hostile locations and steal valuable loot and antiques. Your job is simple: meet your quota, survive, and return to the facility.

Steal too little, and the company has a problem with you.
Steal too much, and the things lurking below have a problem with you.

Meet your quota to earn your freedom. For now, anyway.
STEAL. MEET YOUR QUOTA. GET OUT.

The company sends you down into strange and dangerous locations to recover valuable items. Your crew descends by elevator, searches for loot, and tries to bring back enough value to satisfy the company's demands.

Once your quota is met, the company permits extraction. Until then, you're on your own.

But meeting the quota is only the beginning. Anything you steal beyond the required amount is profit you get to keep. Push your luck for a bigger payday, or play it safe and make it home alive.

Just remember: there's a reason the company keeps sending you down there.
THE GREED METER

Greed is at the heart of everything. The more you take, the more dangerous your job becomes.

• Stealing loot increases your Greed Meter
• Higher greed makes encounters increasingly difficult
• Push too far, and the horrors below become a much bigger problem
• Meet your quota, but decide for yourself how much extra profit is worth the risk

The company wants its money. You want yours. The things below want you dead.

How greedy are you willing to get?
MADAM PHYLLIS — THE DEBT COLLECTOR

Failure isn't free.

Fail to meet your quota, and Madam Phyllis may come to collect what you're missing.
