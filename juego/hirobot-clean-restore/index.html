HiroBot: Clean & Restore

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

HiroBot: Clean & Restore is a relaxing single-player game you can enjoy at your own pace. Clean up dirt, dispose of trash, and restore objects. Reach every nook and cranny with your jetpack, and experience the satisfaction of bringing grimy areas back to life!

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

Take on assignments as a helpful robot named Hiro and aid him in performing various tasks to return grungy locations to pristine condition. Use one of the three tools and the jetpack, and interact with the environment to leave everything sparkling clean. While playing, you can listen to the included, relaxing soundtrack, your own music, a podcast, or just the sound of cleaning.
CLEAN
The Cleaner lets you wash surfaces and objects with powerful cleaning fluid.

RECYCLE
With the Recycler you can remove trash and retrieve displaced objects.

RESTORE
Equip the Restorer to bring displaced objects to their proper place.

USE THE JETPACK
Access out-of-reach areas with your jetpack.

FEATURES
• 3 expansive assignments + tutorial - at least 4 hours of gameplay

• Satisfying gameplay with no fail states

• Replay assignments as many times as you like

• Good in short bursts and long sessions

• Relaxing soundtrack
