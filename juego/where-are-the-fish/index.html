De qué trata

Where are the Fish? is an exploration game where you drive around a surreal desertscape in search for fishes and meet with interesting individuals along the way.
Gameplay includes:
• Driving a vintage pickup truck.

• Fishing in an assortment of unusual bodies of water.

• Conversing with an array of unique individuals.

• Exploring interesting locales.

• Taking in the beauty of the low-poly desert landscape.

ATTENTION!: This is a short game that ends in ~45 minutes.
