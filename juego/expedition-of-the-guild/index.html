De qué trata

Create your own guild and recruit heroes during your journey: Paladins, Mages, Hunters, Priests, and more. Tanks, DPS, and Healers all play important roles in raids. Unlock talent trees, collect powerful gear, and grow your team from a small party into a legendary guild renowned throughout the land!

Choose durable Tanks, powerful DPS builds, and skilled healers to keep your party alive. Build your party your way: Choose safe and balanced raid setups, rush levels with high-damage teams, or experiment with fun combinations to push your limits. Pick your lineup before every raid and overcome challenges through teamwork and strategy.

Fight towering giants and ancient gods hidden within ruins. Every BOSS features unique mechanics and intense battles. Defeat them to loot chests and powerful class-based gear. No random loot rolls — you decide how every reward is distributed. Build the perfect endgame setup for your heroes and enjoy the thrill of classic MMORPG raids once again!
