De qué trata

THE DEVIL LAID A LOT OF EGGS...
One night, the Devil slipped into your barn and possessed your chicken. By morning, she had laid 4,266 eggs. Hidden among the colorful pile are 666 demonic eggs, each carrying a demon. Your job? Sort the good eggs, find the demonic ones to burn them, and save the world.
IDENTIFY & COLLECT 4,266 EGGS

Gather eggs of different colors as you work your way through the barn. Learn to recognize each type and collect them all!
SORT THEM INTO TRAYS

Carefully sort the eggs into their matching trays and keep the barn organized. Don't let the wrong egg end up in the wrong tray!
DESTROY DEMONIC EGGS

Hidden among the 4,266 eggs are 666 demonic eggs. Use the Holy Cross to scan and identify the possessed eggs, then send them into the burner to destroy them. Time to scan, sort, and sanctify!
WATCH OUT FOR THE POSSESSED CHICKEN!

Your chicken won't make this easy. Possessed by the Devil, she'll attack you and knock eggs out of your hands to disrupt your work. Use the Holy Cross to stun her and get back to your holy egg-sorting duties!

FEATURES
• 4,266 Eggs to Sort: Collect colorful eggs, identify the 666 demonic eggs, and destroy them before evil takes over the world.

• Possessed Chicken: Watch out for the possessed chicken as she attacks you and disrupts your sorting duties.

• Holy Cross Abilities: Reveal demonic eggs, perform long jumps, and stun the possessed chicken with your holy cross.

• Egg Highlight: Highlight all locations where eggs of a selected type can be found.

• Storage Highlight: Instantly highlight the correct storage tray for the egg you're holding.

• Auto-Sort Eggs: Automatically sort multiple eggs in your hand by their colors.
