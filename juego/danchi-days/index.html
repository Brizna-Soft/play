De qué trata

Talk to your neighbors to bring your hometown back together in a 5-chapter adventure. Explore a small town with a comedic-yet-bittersweet story about appreciating friends and everyday life, against the backdrop of aging society and family living with dementia. Get to know people to get them to RSVP to your festival, and read their 2000s-era websites on an in-game internet!

Hi! I’m Hoshino, the heroine of Danchi Days! Let's learn how to play using just five simple game mechanics: Explore, Look, Sense, Talk, and “Internet.”
1. EXPLORE
Explore the world of the danchi (a Japanese housing complex) by walking around. Just outside my home is my longtime neighbor, Pesta!

2. TALK
Let’s invite her to the festival by talking to her!

So easy! That’s 1 RSVP down.
Let's go talk to Buck...! But wait... he doesn't want to go. And he doesn't like my enthusiasm, either...

I guess convincing the other 50 neighbors won't be so simple. Sometimes, I need to...
3. LOOK

I can look at the suspicious egg by holding the look button down. This lets me target it with a square-shaped reticle!
Looking at things lets me learn about it and may cause other things to happen.
After seeing the egg, my neighbor Sizz jumps out from the bushes to greet us! Turns out he put the egg there to warn us about how hot the slide was. Thanks, Sizz!

But he doesn’t want to RSVP… now what?
4. SENSE

See those pink sparkles coming from the egg?
By holding the Sense Button down, I can absorb the sparkles.
This will start a Sense Minigame!

If I touch the eggs, I’ll gain points to fill the Sunflower Bar and win. But don't let me run out of Footsteps! Every Sense Minigame has different objectives.
After winning, I eat the egg and learn about it.
