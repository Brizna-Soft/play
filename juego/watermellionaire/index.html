Watermellionaire

Juego de PC · Indie · Indie

Watermellionaire is a dark battle royale fruit-matching game. Pitted against other employees, you compete to become the Most Valuable Packer (MVP) - those who fail are fired. Faced with nothing but a box, some fruit and a guard watching your every move, only one person can come out on top.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Meet Your Quota. Or Else.

Welcome to your first day at the fruit packing facility. Combine the same fruits more quickly and efficiently than your opponents to earn points towards your daily quota.
If you’re the worst-performing employee, or fail to fit all of the fruit in your box, be warned: there’s a guard with an itchy trigger finger who’s ready to fire you at a moment’s notice – literally.
Choose Your Way To Work

Every second counts when all the employees are scrambling to avoid a performance review! That's why the totally safe fruit packing facility offers a variety of game modes.
• Solo Sandbox - a slow-paced sandbox environment to rack up the highest score possible

• Practice vs. CPU - perfect your strategy before you launch your full-time career

• Battle Royale - compete with up to 8 players with customizable match rules

Boost Yourself. Sabotage Everyone Else.

With such high stakes on the line, it’s only natural some of the more…underhanded employees might try to disrupt your workflow!
Merge larger fruit to unlock powerful abilities that can turn the tide of any shift:
• Enhance your dropped fruit

• Throw vegetables in your co-workers' boxes

• Clear sabotages from your own box

• Force unwanted fruit onto your opponents

The company appreciates your continued productivity.
