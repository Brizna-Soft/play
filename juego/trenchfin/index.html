De qué trata

TrenchFin is a 10 minute Student Project

A 3rd person behind the character 3D platformer melee combat game that follows Asha Trenchfin, an anthropomorphic anglerfish on a mission to reach the top of a spiraling reef to save the magic coral tree from a terrifying Leviathan. On her quest, she must use her light to reveal invisible platforms and enemies, jump from coral to coral, dash-bite and groundpound her way through the creepy critters from the deep to progress upwards. Asha must make her way through platforming challenges within pockets of the towering reef, as well as face the treacherous outside.

Key Features
• 5 Levels to explore and progress!

• 24 Fishy Achievements!

• 2 Terrifying critters!
