Needle in a Haystack Simulator in a Haystack of Needle in a Haystack Simulators

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

5,000,000 games on VAPOR, and all but one are clones. From your bedroom PC, screen procedurally generated store pages, stamp each fake on the detail that gives it away, put bots to work, and dig through the haystack for the one original. An incremental hidden object parody.

Kev goes to bed. You don't.Spot the tell, stamp the clone
Every clone gives itself away somewhere, and you click right on it: a zero where the O should be, a sequel nobody asked for ("2: Pin Harder"), Lone Pin Studio LLC, a description that opens with "Certainly! Here's a description for your game:", Overwhelmingly Positive from four reviews, any price but €4.99. Twenty tells in all. The subtle ones need tools: a magnifier to count the fingers on the cover, a Cyrillic detector for the letters that only pretend to be Latin, a reverse image search for the recycled screenshots.Then hire some help
Sell what you dig up on the Community Market and spend it in the Toolbox. A browser extension that beeps at the obvious ones. An intern: unpaid, tireless and slightly too enthusiastic. A Readit megathread: ten thousand strangers, one spreadsheet, zero sleep. A web crawler, a server farm and, one day, a spy satellite that reads store pages from orbit. Nobody asked how.

The bots screen on their own and pass you the pages they can't settle. Settle those doubts to uncover the twelve Fragments of the original, train the bots on each one, and watch five million pages shrink to thirteen.Meanwhile, in your room
Everything you buy turns up in your bedroom, and so do the clones: keep an eye on the plush, the posters and that bean bag. Copies of your own files sneak into Documents.
