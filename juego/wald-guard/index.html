Wald-Guard – An Emotional Adventure as a Bumblebee
In Wald-Guard, you step into the role of a tiny bumblebee and embark on an emotional journey. Each chapter is a meticulously crafted, linear platforming experience with plenty of room for exploration and surprises. What the chapters truly mean? That remains a secret 🤐.
Jump without Run
In this world, the ground = death. As a bumblebee, you must navigate the parkour by flying. But beware: your air jumps are limited, and when they run out, you faaaalllllLLL down.

Q: How do you recharge them?
A: Simply touch a wall, and you can keep flying.

Collect Hearts 💕Gather hearts to increase your maximum number of air jumps.

Each chapter also hides collectibles that unlock cool hats for your bumblebee, exclusive artwork, and old sketches.

Co-op for Up to 4 Players!
Just connect up to 4 controllers and experience the adventure together with your friends.

Be curious, explore the world, and uncover the secrets!
