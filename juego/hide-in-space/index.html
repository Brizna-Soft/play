Hide IN SPACE

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Multijugador masivo · Acción, Indie

Join up to 100 other online players to play a game of hide and seek on mars. You get to choose to either be a seeker or a hider. Hiders may hide anywhere across a 1 for 1 scale mars planet.

De qué trata

This is a variation of the Hide and Seek genre. Instead of hiding on earth, you are hiding on Mas. You can hide anwhere on a 1 for 1 scale mars planet. This is a massive online multiplayer where you and up  to 100 players online can play hide and seek. You must either be a hider or seeker.  As a Hider, you get a certain amount of time to hide each round and as a seeker you have to wait until everywhere has hidden and then you can go try to find them. Because its such a large space to hide it may be difficult to find the hiders and thats what makes this a challenge.

1. You can choose to be either a hider or a seeker online with up to 100 other online players
2. Explore beautiful environments on mars where you have an entire 1 to 1 size planet with the real mars.
3. See mars like never before and play hide and go seek like never before
4. Experience realistic views of space

You choose your character and then play hide and seek on Mars.
