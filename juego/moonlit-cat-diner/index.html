Moonlit Cat Diner

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

A rainy night, a little diner at the end of the alley. Cat customers take a seat, place an order, and open up as you play — six solitaire games, Shisen-Sho, and six puzzle rules. Every story you hear fills your cat codex, and every coin you earn goes into your own diner.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

A rainy night, a little late-night diner at the end of the alley. You're the one behind the counter.

One by one, cat customers come in out of the rain and take a seat. They order, and while the game plays out, they start to talk. The more cards find their place, the more they open up — and the last part of each story only comes if you win.Three trays
• Six solitaire games — Klondike, FreeCell, TriPeaks, Yukon, Pyramid and Spider, on a 90-level track that starts gentle and slowly deepens.

• Shisen-Sho — connect matching dish tiles with a path of no more than two turns. 150 boards, with shapes and stacked layers that keep changing.

• Six puzzle rules — triple match, drink sorting, merge, blocks, path linking and seating, taking turns across 150 levels.

Twenty-six guests, one cat codex
Every guest is a cat: a regular of fifteen years, a kitten waiting for someone, a wanderer who stepped out of a far-away legend. Codex entries aren't unlocked by winning — they fill in only when the cat tells you themselves. Where they're from, what they love, and the worry they find hardest to say out loud.

Regulars don't repeat themselves from the top. They notice the season, remember what they ordered last time, and spot what's new in the diner.Make the diner yours
Spend the coins you earn on furniture, lights, ornaments, the view outside the window, and the owner's outfit. Drag across the wide room and place things wherever you like.And also
• Today's Challenge — the same board for everyone, a new one each day

• Daily quests, nights that change with weather and season, and special named nights

• No hearts, no energy, no waiting. Play as long as you like

• Mouse, keyboard and gamepad support
