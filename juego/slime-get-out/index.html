De qué trata

SLIME, GET OUT!
Survive the waves. Build your power. Keep the slimes away!
Slime, Get out! is a fast-paced, top-down action roguelite where you battle waves of monsters, collect weapons and items, and experiment with different combinations to create your own powerful builds.
With over 300 items across 4 different tiers and 12 playable characters, there's plenty to discover and experiment with in every run!
OVER 300 ITEMS TO DISCOVER
Discover 300+ items spread across 4 tiers, each offering different ways to improve your build.
Mix and match items, weapons, and upgrades to find interesting combinations. Sometimes, even a simple item can become surprisingly useful with the right setup!
12 CHARACTERS, DIFFERENT PLAYSTYLES
Choose from 12 playable characters, each with their own abilities and combat mechanics.
Experiment with different characters and discover new ways to approach each run.
FIGHT THROUGH WAVES OF ENEMIES
Face relentless waves of monsters in fast-paced arena battles. Stay on the move, avoid danger, and take on increasingly challenging enemies and powerful bosses.
SURVIVE, FALL, REPEAT
Every run is another chance to try something different. Experiment with new builds, discover unexpected combinations, and see how far you can go!
KEY FEATURES
• Over 300 items across 4 different tiers.

• 12 playable characters, each with unique abilities and playstyles.

• Fast-paced top-down arena combat.

• Experiment with different weapons, items, and upgrades to create your own builds.

• Challenging enemy waves and powerful bosses.

• Roguelite gameplay focused on experimentation and replayability.

• Colorful 2D cartoon visuals with a lighthearted fantasy atmosphere.
