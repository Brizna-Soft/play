Breeding Pit

Juego de PC · Rol, Simuladores · Rol (RPG), Simulación

You are the Dark Lord. Breed an orc horde in the pit beneath your tower, where every whelp inherits real genes from its sire and dam. Send them to war in retro turn-based battles, and raise a dynasty strong enough to put out the Light.

Tráiler

De qué trata

You are the Dark Lord. The pit is yours.
Beneath a ruined tower, an exiled sorcerer digs a breeding pit and raises an orc host to break the Bright Realms. Every whelp born there carries real genes from its sire and its dam: some awake, some sleeping, some that will only surface three generations later. Breed well and your horde grows stronger with every birth. Breed badly and you'll find out on the battlefield.
Breed them
• Over 130 genes that bend the rules rather than add stats: chain lightning, goblin rabbles, executioners, warg callers, laws that rewrite a whole battle.

• Real inheritance. Dominant, recessive and chimeric genes. Carriers pass on what they don't show. Pair two orcs and see the odds for every gene before you commit.

• Omens. Now and then new blood wakes in a whelp, a gene neither parent had, and an Omen founds a bloodline that its descendants carry.

• Name every whelp the moment it hatches, then follow it from the pit to the front.

Send them to war
Four orcs and their summons on a 3x2 grid, one shared pool of action points in retro-styled RPG battles. When a gene fires, the game tells you whose blood it came from. Raid five realms of Men, Elves, Dwarves, Lake-folk and Eyrie knights, then storm the White Spire and put out the Light.

Raise a dynasty
• Family trees that remember everyone: the living, the sleeping in the Barrow, the ones who fell and who they fell to.

• Grudges and vengeance. When a champion falls, its kin remember the kind of foe that killed it, and strike harder against them.

• Bloodlines founded by Omens, carried by name down the generations.

• The Dynasty poster: your champion, your line and your fallen on one page, saved as a picture to share.
