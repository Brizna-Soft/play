De qué trata

GET CASH. GET MEDS. GET HOME.
Mount Noire is a survival-horror game set in a dead ski resort.
You wake up in a tiny apartment under permanent grey skies, waiting for your weekly paycheck just so you can get meds and hopefully make it back home in one piece.
Explore panel block courtyards, overgrown forest paths and half-abandoned social spots while the town quietly turns against you. Thugs, cops, Strays, Wasters and old Babas all have their own routines. Some can help you. Most will not. Your money and your choices all decide how Mount Noire treats you.
Features
• Urban survival horror inspired by real-life Eastern Europe
Navigate abandoned apartment blocks, playgrounds and a looming ski jump under constant overcast skies.

• Week-based structure
Live through repeating weeks: get cash, get meds, get home.

• System-driven NPCs
Police, thugs, rabid dogs, bottle collectors and neighbors move on their own schedules and react to sound, light and your behavior.

• PS1-inspired visual style
Lo-fi 3D, heavy fog and rough edges designed to feel like a lost horror game from a forgotten console generation.
