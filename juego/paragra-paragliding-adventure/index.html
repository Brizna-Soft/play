De qué trata

Paragra is a 2D arcade paragliding game. Climb in thermals, become an XC league hero and win competitions. Read the sky and don't land in a field too early.

Start with a paragliding course. Level up through the IBBI ranks to unlock XC flights and competitions. Race your friends in multiplayer and become an online leaderboard legend.

• Thermals wake up as the morning goes on, clouds build up, and some of them start to rain

• Soar coastal sites

• Look out for jets, helicopters, sailplanes and angry eagles

• Take part in Hike & Fly races

• Multiplayer: race real people online on different maps

• Beat Kriglung and fly to the top of Teide in Tenerife

• Parakiting at Wagga Beach with 25 tricks to master

• Untangle the lines – a minigame with 30 tangled wings

• Unlock 60 achievements

• XC sites: Mieroszów, Żar, Piła and Trzęsacz in Poland, Bassano in Italy, Algodonales in Spain, Tenerife, Quixadá in Brazil and Bir Billing in India

• Competitions: Kruszewo in North Macedonia, Saint-André in France, Disentis in Switzerland, Roldanillo in Colombia, Z-Pyr in the Pyrenees and Y-Alps in the Alps

• One button to turn, one for speed bar, both for a spiral

• Tap the speed bar fast to flap and stand on obstacles (in parakiting)

• Languages: English, Polish

Controls
• Left mouse / Space – turn

• Right mouse / Shift – speed bar

• Both – spiral

• Esc – menu
