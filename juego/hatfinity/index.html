HATFINITY

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual, Indie · Acción, Indie, Rol (RPG)

TIME TO PUT SOME MAGIC HATS ON YOU BALDY! Hatfinity is a top-down roguelike arcade experience set within a dark crystal cave. Play as a powerful wizard that combines his spells into something greater than their elements. Your choices determine whether you become the man with infinite hats!

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

HATFINITY is a roguelike survivors game enchanted with skill-based mechanics, etched into its varied systems.
In a dark dangerous cave, all kinds of monsters lie in wait for a human capable of wielding powerful spells.
As a wizard trapped in this place for an unknown reason, all you can do is fight until your very last breath and...
GAMBLE ALL THAT YOU HAVE LEFT FOR THE CHANCE OF MORE!

DEFEAT MONSTERS TO GAMBLE FOR GREATER POWER!
Absorb the souls of fallen monsters to level yourself up as a student of wizardry, and gamble their lives to random hats bestowed upon you from the crystals. Each hat contains a unique boost that'll enhance your abilities and stacks on top of others, meaning an infinite array of power lies ahead of your struggles! Let's see if your stack reaches sky high!

COMBINE ELEMENTS TO CREATE EXTRAVAGANT SPELLS
As you grow stronger, you will have the choice to choose what spells to cast as you automatically cycle through your arsenal in battle! By crafting two spells of any element together, you'll be able to wield a stronger combinative spell representing their matter. Try to find all the combinative spells through your grimoire, and create the ultimate personalized arsenal of magic!

BECOME STRONK AS A WIZARD
As a wizard fueled by nothing other than greed for all the hats in this cave, you must strengthen yourself for the stronger waves of enemies to come. Progressing through your run, you'll be able to purchase relics of varying rarities to help boost your wizardly charms and qualities!
