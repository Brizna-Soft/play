Before All Saints Day

Juego de PC · Aventura, Casual · Aventura

One night. One secret. One very curious ghost. Step into a 10–20 minute atmospheric Halloween tale where a human girl hides among ghosts at their yearly festival — and tries to slip away before a mischievous boy exposes what she really is.

About
Ghosts celebrate Halloween.
They dress up, exchange candy, and revel until dawn.
On the night of October 31st, a girl slips into their festival — alone.
No candy. No costume. So why would she risk everything to come here?
When a mischievous ghost boy realizes what she really is —
a story that lasts only one night begins to move.

Story
On the night of October 31st, Chloe slips quietly into a town where ghosts live.
She has a reason for being there—one she keeps to herself.
But in this town, even the ghosts love trick-or-treating.
And Chloe… didn’t bring a single piece of candy.
When one mischievous ghost catches her empty-handed,
things take a turn she never expected.
Features
• Short narrative experience (10–20 minutes)

• Atmospheric Halloween setting

• Light tension and playful interactions with a ghost

• Two languages available: Japanese / English

Warning
Contains mild sexual harassment: a scene where the ghost boy licks the protagonist’s finger to tease her. No explicit content.
