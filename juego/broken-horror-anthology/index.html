Broken Horror Anthology

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Simuladores · Acción, Indie, Simulación, Terror

A collection of four atmospheric horror games about brokenness and malfunction. Inspect toys for defects, cheat on an exam, follow a radio signal, and explore the darkness by the light of matches.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Broken Horror Anthology is a unique collection of horror stories featuring four games from independent developers. Each story explores the theme of brokenness through its own world, narrative, and gameplay mechanics.
THE GAMES:
• holy... XMAS! - play as a Christmas elf who does Santa’s dirty work, inspecting toys for defects. Things get complicated when Krampus appears and curses the toys, changing their appearance and turning some of them into sinister and extremely dangerous creatures.

• Artificial Selection Exam - a psychological horror game about a deadly exam in a dystopian world. Use a limited set of items to cheat, deceive your AI teacher, and survive

• MATCHLIGHT - a psychological horror game about a single father with a crippling fear of the dark. To save his son, he must confront his trauma and make his way through the darkness without losing his sanity. Explore ominous corridors by the light of matches, confront your fears, and beware of the shadows lurking in the depths.soul.

• 99.9 FM - an atmospheric first-person horror game about finding your way out of looping spaces. Pick up the radio, listen closely to the signal, and decide where to go. The roads all look the same, and simply observing your surroundings isn’t enough.
