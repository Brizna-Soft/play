Ironbound

Juego de PC · Indie, Rol, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Ironbound is a turn-based multiplayer strategy game with fast-paced duels and deep meta-game. Upgrade your character and equipment and climb to the top of the multiplayer ladder!

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Ironbound is a turn-based tactical duel game built around RPG class identities and deep itemization. You shape your hero through equipment, not deck building, and develop your play style across matches rather than rogue-like runs.

Learn each class, then shape how it fights through weapons, shields and magic trinkets. Equipment can fundamentally change a hero’s play style. Your opponent’s visible gear lets you read their strategy and plan your response, rather than guess what might be hidden in their deck. Spend your action points well and land the strike that ends it.

FIVE HEROES, FIVE WAYS TO FIGHT
Choose your RPG archetype: the aggressive Berserker, the resilient Crusader, the stealthy Assassin, the cunning Witch or the chaotic Pirate. Each class has its own identity, strengths and abilities to master. Learn how they fight, recognize what makes them dangerous, and discover how different equipment can turn a familiar opponent into a very different challenge.

BUILD YOUR HERO
Your equipment shapes the way you fight. Gold earned in matches upgrades your character and buys weapons, shields and magic trinkets, opening up new combinations to explore. Itemization can fundamentally change how a class plays: build around its natural strengths or take it in a different direction. Your opponent’s equipped items are visible, so learning the gear helps you read their strategy and plan your response. You build a hero through equipment, not a deck of hidden surprises.

CLIMB THE LADDER
Put your hero and your knowledge to the test in turn-based online duels against real players. Read the opposing equipment, manage your action points, and decide when to attack, defend or commit to a decisive strike. Rise through the ranks as you learn the matchups and refine your setup.
