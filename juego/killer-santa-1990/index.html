De qué trata

After the death of your grandfather, you return to the old house where you once spent your childhood. The place is abandoned, covered in dust, and filled with memories you can barely recall. But something is wrong. Strange noises echo from the basement, people begin to disappear, and a DVD you buy from a local video store draws you into a nightmare.
Explore a disturbing house, uncover clues, survive supernatural encounters, and face the terrifying figure stalking you through the night: Killer Santa.
Killer Santa 1990 is a first-person horror experience inspired by gritty 1990s VHS-era slasher films. What begins as a quiet return home becomes a desperate fight to escape a nightmare hiding beneath your grandfather’s house.
