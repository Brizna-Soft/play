Potted Pets is a cozy farming and pet simulator, with an adorable cast of living plant pots. Spend time cultivating a personal garden while growing a personal relationship with your collection of “Potted Pets!” Sit down for a relaxing, customizable experience that brightens up your busy day!

De qué trata

Grow all kinds of different plants from all over the world! Discover new areas as you expand your garden, and collect new types of plants, trees, and succulents along the way!

Make each member of the garden special! With over 1500 combinations for each pot type, a vibrant and colorful garden can be created with unlockable customizations and coloring options!

Get some help from the succulents! Glass pots carry succulents that increase efficiency of each garden, allowing glass pots to water and harvest clay and stone pots, even fighting off pesky crows that are trying to steal from gardens.

Keep your eyes peeled! Crows will swoop in to steal your plants if you’re not paying attention. You’ll also notice minigames popping up around your plots that reward you with money, or… a new customization option!
