Master your new earthbending powers and become the greatest martial artist the world has ever seen! Master the moves, craft your combos, and take to the arena to engage in one of the most intense and exciting 1-on-1 VR fighting games out there!

De qué trata

Experience VR combat like you never have before
RUMBLE's physics-based combat system is both functionally unique, and refreshingly intense!

Improve your newfound EARTHBENDING skills in the GYM
The GYM features all kinds of useful tools to help you learn the basics of bending!

Test your mastery against other fighters in the ARENA
Engage in intense 1 on 1 battles against other earthbenders in the ARENA!

Customize your fighting style with SHIFTSTONES
Shiftstones grant you new abilities that further personalize your combat style!

Unlock NEW powers and equipment as you PLAY
Earn all kinds of rewards like new moves and new cosmetics just by playing the game!

Begin your EARTHBENDING journey with RUMBLE TODAY!
But there is more! RUMBLE also features...

• THE PARK
Where you can train, hang around, or even play Minigames with up to 6 players!

• CROSSPLAY & CROSS PROGRESSION
No matter what platform you play on, you will be able to play with everyone!

• THE ROCK CAM
An in-game handheld camera with which you can create all sorts of fun photos and videos!
Note: The Rock Cam is not available on Linux, and by extension, the Steam Frame.

• AND MORE IS ON THE WAY!
Stay tuned for what's next, we're still actively updating RUMBLE with all kinds of exciting new features!
