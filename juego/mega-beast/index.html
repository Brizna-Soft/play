De qué trata

Prepare to unleash your inner monster.  Become the Mega Beast!  Rip attack tear thru your victims like it's nothing.  Think your powerful now! Wait till you get more traps and/or powerups on your arrival.  Your not just a Beast, Not just a Super Beast! BUT A MEGA BEAST.

Features
• Simple Directional Controls! Game Pad Optional!

• MAME control layout for using your arcade stick.

•  Different upgrades and power ups at your disposal!

• Over 5 mini games.

• 2 Players coop on one pc!
