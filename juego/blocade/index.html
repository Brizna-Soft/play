Blocade

Juego de PC · Indie, Estrategia, Free to Play · Estrategia, Indie

Race your pawn across the board and drop walls to cut off your rival, but never seal them in. Blocade is a free two-player strategy board game: ranked matches against real people on PC and mobile, a daily 128-player tournament, puzzles and a ladder of bots.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Easy to learn. Hard to outthink.
Each turn you do one thing: move your pawn one step, or place a wall. First to reach the far side wins. Walls slow your rival down, but a path must always stay open, so the whole game is deciding when a wall is worth more than a step.
Ranked against real people
• 1v1 ranked matches with a rating, leagues and a leaderboard

• One player pool with the Blocade mobile apps, so there is always a game

• Time controls from quick to relaxed, and a rematch when you want revenge

Royale, every day
A 128-player knockout starts at 12:00 UTC every day. Entry is free. Win your board, move up the bracket, and the last player standing takes the crown.
Knockouts with your friends
Start a private 4 to 16 player knockout and share the code. Friends join for free.
A ladder of bots
Computer opponents with their own styles, from a gentle first game to the Nightshift crew at the top. Keep a daily streak to unlock the hardest tiers.
Get better every day
• Daily puzzles with a streak to protect

• Game review that replays your match and shows the move it turned on

• A narrated tutorial and five coaches to learn from

• Pass & Play for two people at one screen

Blocade Plus (optional)
Blocade is free to play, with no ads. Blocade Plus is a one-time DLC purchase:
• Unlimited ranked matches

• Full review and analysis after every game

• A reserved seat in every Royale

• Host your own Knockouts

• Unlimited hints and takebacks in Assisted mode

• Every board theme and the full quick-chat tray
