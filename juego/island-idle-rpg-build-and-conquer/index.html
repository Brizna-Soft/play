Island Idle RPG: Build and Conquer

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Rol · Estrategia, Indie, Rol (RPG), Simulación

Lead a team of fox workers to build a thriving settlement, click to gather resources or let them work, and craft powerful gear. Prepare your leader to explore dungeons, defeat bosses, and bring home valuable loot as you conquer new islands in this relaxing idle RPG with clicker mechanics.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Lead a team of fox workers, grow your island village, and send your hero into dungeons for rare loot. A relaxing idle RPG about crafting, building, and conquering new islands at your own pace.

Click when you want, idle when you don't: click trees, mines and fishing spots to gather (up to 5 clicks per second), or sit back and let your workers keep the village running. Grow a small village into a thriving kingdom by assigning workers to gather resources, craft useful items, and keep production moving while you expand at your own pace. Unlock new islands, strengthen your economy, and prepare your leader with gear and simple RPG classes to face dungeon battles, bosses, and earn valuable loot to further improve your island.
A light and relaxing blend of idle progression, worker management, crafting, and adventure — designed to be easy to follow, satisfying to optimize, and comfortable to play at your own rhythm.
