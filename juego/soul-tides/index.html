De qué trata

Soul Tides is a dark-fantasy tug-of-war strategy game where players summon armies, build shrines, and push the battlefield toward their enemy’s stronghold.
Soul Tides brings the classic tug-of-war strategy formula into a modern standalone game.
Tug-of-War Combat – Deploy minions that fight automatically, pushing the frontlines toward your enemy’s stronghold in a relentless battle for control.

Build & Expand – Construct Shrines and fortifications to unlock powerful Blessings and units.

Customize Your Army – Research and assign unique abilities to your minions, creating devastating synergies.

Pray for Power – Unlock new units and abilities to dominate the battlefield.

Deep Tech Tree – Unlock new strategies with a dynamic research system that ensures no two matches play out the same.
Multiplayer Mayhem – Challenge friends and rivals in intense PvP battles, where strategy and adaptation are the keys to victory.

Resource Management – Balance Gold, Supply, and Souls to maintain an army capable of overwhelming your foes.
Dark Fantasy Atmosphere – Immerse yourself in a world of forbidden magic, demonic bargains, and relentless warfare.
Trials — Single Player Challenge Mode
Beyond the battlefield lies a path of Trials.
In this single-player challenge mode, you will face a series of encounters designed to test your mastery of strategy and adaptation. Each Trial presents new enemies, modifiers, and powerful guardians that stand between you and victory.
Choose your shrines, shape your army, and push the frontlines forward as you battle through a map of Trials. Defeat formidable bosses, experiment with different builds, and discover powerful combinations of units and Blessings.
Victory is only the beginning.
