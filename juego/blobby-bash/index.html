De qué trata

Blobby Bash is an online multiplayer party game where you use your body to bounce around the environment, dashing, ground slamming and bashing your friends in 7 different game modes requiring varying skills.

There are 7 different game modes, all testing different skills and forcing different interactions between players. Ranging from Last Bert Standing to Bertball, these game modes test friendships and encourage competitive fun with quick rounds lasting mere minutes.

As you move around your bubble grows bigger, and with it your power, unleash everything you’ve got with both horizontal and vertical dashing, need a quick escape or deal the finishing blow to a friend? High in the sky ground slamming can be a quick and definitive end to all players in the impact area.

With 7 game changing powerups, you can reclaim victory at any time. What powerup will you get this time, a Jackpot giving infinite dash recharge or a homing missile aimed directly at a friend (or foe).

Customise your Bert to your liking with outfits galore! Swap head, face, torso, arms, legs and mouth accessories to create a multitude of costumes!

All customisations can be acquired by playing and winning games with no in-app purchases required.
