DWARRF: A Pinball Roguelike

Juego de PC · Acción, Rol · Acción, Rol (RPG)

Grab your balls and set sail on your 'pirate' minecart into the depths of the mines. Fight enemies in a unique blend of Pinball & Pachinko, with turn-based combat whilst collecting game altering balls, relics, & runes that ensure no two runs are alike.

Tráiler

De qué trata

A Pinball/Pachinko/Bubble Shooter mashup where you play as "Arf", a so-called pirate dwarf who must adventure alone into the depths of the mines to slay the evil that lurks below. Unlike the other dwarves, he prefers to battle with an array of unique balls he blasts out of his cannon - from a safe distance within his "pirate ship".

Firstly, you must launch your ball into the mine-shaft, hitting pegs and bumpers alike to power up your ball. Striking at ores will generate 'frenzy', further increasing your potential damage. Use your flippers to keep the ball in play and look out for unique objects on the board, such as extra cannons or even a wyvern head?

Once you've powered up your ball, unleash it onto the battlefield and wreak havoc. Balls have unique traits that can help in a fight - such as exploding, electrification in an area, and can even summon a trusty dwarf to fight by your side!

Fight your way through 4 levels and craft your perfect loudout. Explore the combinations of relics, balls, and runes and take advantage of synergies to dominate your foes.

Progress through challenges to repair your once magnificent stronghold, unlocking new balls, runes, and relics to power your future runs. Also, unlock powers such as freezing the balls in the shop for later purchase!
Feature List:
• Ball board effects: These can effect the gameplay in the mining phase. Such as multiball, scattershot, flint and steel that makes fireballs when they rub, with 50+ balls in the game already.

• Ball battle effects: These can effect the gameplay in the battle phase. Such as totems, shields, area damagers, charm, taunts.

• 100+ Relics: These can effect gameplay at any time.
