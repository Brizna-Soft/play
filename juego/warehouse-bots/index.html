Control warehouse robot traffic with road signs and configurable traffic lights. Manage orders, build and expand your warehouse, and organize storage to keep deliveries moving. Solve traffic jams, upgrade equipment, and grow your operation through the campaign or in sandbox mode.

De qué trata

Warehouse Bots is a logistics management game where you build and run automated warehouses. Design rooms and roads, place storage, docks, and equipment, deploy delivery robots, then accept orders and watch goods move through your operation. As traffic builds and equipment wears down, use road signs to restrict turns and filter cargo, configure traffic lights, upgrade your equipment, and expand carefully to remove bottlenecks and keep every delivery moving.
CONTROL THE FLOW
When robot traffic starts to jam, redesign the road network and take control of every intersection. Restrict specific turns, direct different cargo types with filtering signs, place yield signs, and configure traffic lights to separate competing flows and keep deliveries moving.

Configure traffic-light phases to coordinate busy intersections. Control competing traffic flows, reduce waiting times, and prevent heavy robot traffic from locking up your warehouse.

BUILD YOUR WAREHOUSE
Shape each warehouse around the demands of your operation. Build rooms and roads, place truck docks, storage areas, chargers, and equipment, then expand and reorganize the layout as larger orders put it under pressure.

AUTOMATE EVERY DELIVERY
Accept distribution orders and put your fleet to work. Watch delivery robots unload incoming goods, carry packages to the right storage areas, and bring them back to outgoing docks—then add robots, chargers, and equipment as demand grows.

CHOOSE YOUR ORDERS
Choose from distribution orders with different cargo types, quantities, storage requirements, deadlines, and rewards. Make sure you have enough capacity, build specialized storage for frozen, flammable, and chemical goods, and accept only the orders your warehouse is ready to handle.
