Artifact Reforged

The classic online strategy game of empires and ancient artifacts, reborn. Found cities, raise armies of thousands and race rival players across a living world to seize the legendary Artifacts. Free to play, with one account on PC, browser and phone.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Artifact Reforged brings back the beloved SamuGames empire builder, rebuilt from the ground up by some of the early team members. Found cities, grow your people, raise armies of thousands and race rival players across a vast, living world to seize the legendary Artifacts. Hold them all long enough, and the world is yours.
Real players, real wars
• Persistent online worlds that keep running while you're away. Your empire grows, and gets attacked, around the clock.

• Alliances and betrayals. Team up, share vision, defend each other, or turn on your ally at the worst possible moment.

• Clans, chat, polls and notes, with a friendly community and Trainers who help new players get started.

• Get a message when you're attacked, even while you're playing in another game.

Build an empire
• Farms, lumber mills, quarries and furnaces feed your economy. Schools, theatres and hospitals keep your people trained, happy and rested.

• Markets, roads, canals, walls and guard towers.

• City Governors run a city for you, as much or as little as you like: pick a focus (grow, trade or fight) and let them build.

Command your armies
• Infantry, archers, cavalry, pikemen, mounted archers, scouts, catapults, settlers, galleys and transports.

• Formations, patrols, rally points, raids and scouting.

• Find the Artifacts, take them, and hold them against everyone.

For new and veteran players
• A hands-on tutorial, Beginner games and step-by-step goals.

• Casual mode (governors handle the busywork) or Advanced mode (every lever is yours).

• Blitz games for a fast weekend match, Standard games for the long war, and Lone Wolf games where it's every player for themselves.

One account, everywhere
Play on PC through Steam, in any browser at , or on your phone, all on the same account.
