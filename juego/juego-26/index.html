De qué trata

Run a Bunny Bakery—Right on Your Desktop!
A desktop management sim built for busy lives—docked at the bottom of your screen, never in the way of work！
Hire cute bunny staff, set your menu, and grow your shop your way.
Between tasks, restock, upgrade, and check the mailbox for customer reviews.
Discover mini-stories, satisfy picky customers, unlock new dishes.

Theme your restaurant!！
Minimalist or lavish? Flowers or stars? Market or jungle? Halloween or carnival...
Tons of theme sets to pick from! Each with special effects!
Boosts efficiency and decorates your desktop shop.
Arranging furniture is pure joy.

(Display only what you like—buffs aren't tied to theme furniture!)

Combine your skills!
Chain Petting, Customer Trading, Petting-to-Massage, Culinary Division...

Learn and upgrade all kinds of chef skills!
Flashy skills while idling—a feast for the eyes.

Don't forget! Hire bunny employees!
Food runners, cash collectors, customer wranglers, stove pokers,
matchmakers (?), spellcasters (??), and those who pet every customer on sight (???)
Each a master of their craft... but combos work miracles!

And! Upgrade your shop!
Better suppliers, bigger menus, staff training,
even a turbo engine for your spatula? ...

Lead your bunnies to better quality, service, and efficiency—go big!

Do poorly, and you can't escape the customers' ... feedback ...

Bunny customers leave reviews, though some are... emmm...
