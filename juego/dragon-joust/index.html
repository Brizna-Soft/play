Dragon Joust

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie

Two riders. Four dragons. One sky to rule. Pick the Orc King or the Sorceress Queen, saddle up a fire, ice, poison, or magic dragon, and joust your rivals out of the clouds. Raid villages, summon storms, call a Titan, or just fly for high scores, solo, online or tournament with 8 players .

De qué trata

Mount your dragon. Lower your lance.
DRAGON JOUST is aerial combat at full gallop. 2 Modes to choose from , Tournament or Open World . Charge rival riders with fire, frost, poison, or arcane breath, then drive them clean out of the saddle with your jousting pole. Choose your dragon, master its elemental breath, and conquer castles, islands, and endless skies, solo or against your friends, compete in a Tournament or just watch AI battle it out.
Choose your rider. Choose your dragon. Ride as the brutal Orc King or the cunning Sorceress Queen, then take the reins of one of four dragons: the Red Fire dragon, the Blue Ice dragon, the Green Poison dragon, or the White Magic dragon. Every pairing brings its own breath attack and its own way to rule the sky.
Fight from the sky, the saddle, and the ground. Unleash breath attacks on enemy dragons, riders, and anything below. Fire your bow mid-flight or on foot. Cast spells to strike your opponent, heal or shield your dragon, or even call a Titan to fight at your side. Take off, soar, dive, and land with full flight control. Dismount to fight on foot, then climb back into the saddle and rejoin the battle. If you get knocked off your dragon , call him, get back on and rejoin the battle !
Pick your side. Attack the villages or defend the villagers who call them home. Then take your rivalry into open-world jousting, where every stretch of sky is a battlefield.
Not in a fighting mood? Fly your dragon for points in the endless runner and see how far you can soar.
Ride alone or settle the score. Take on the skies in single-player, or challenge your friends in multiplayer and prove who rules the air.
Command the weather.
