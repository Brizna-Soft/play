De qué trata

Robot Basketball League is a gritty sports game featuring a modified version of 1v1 Basketball. This League has been around for 23 years. Play exhibition matches in both single-player and local multiplayer. Discover the bizarre lore of this Robot Basketball world when playing through Season Mode. Try to win as every team!
CORE FEATURES
░▒ Local Multiplayer Fun:
-Play Robot Basketball League with your friends! (Requires 2 controllers)
-Make sure they know how to play the game first! Open the controls menu to see which button does what.
░▒ Season Mode:
-Play a 9 game season and see if you can make the playoffs!
-Get in-game currency (Basketball Bucks) for winning playoff games!
TEAMS of the ROBOT BASKETBALL LEAGUE

The league started out with only a few teams, until the expansion in 2009. There are now 10 original robot teams. Each team has their own unique history. Each team has their own future to be written by you, the player. This game keeps track of which team won and what year they won the championship. Each team has their own unique colors.
MEET REFBOT!!! (Refbot version 3.0)

Hello! I am Refbot, your Robot Basketball Referee.
The rules of the Robot Basketball League are a little bit different from Professional Basketball rules, which is played by human athletes in human stadiums full of human spectators. The rules here are more relaxed, more like streetball rules one might say. I get a bit distracted sometimes, so forgive me if I miss a few calls here and there. If the basketball explodes into pieces, don't worry! I will bring out a new ball right away.
