About This Game
Prison Fury is one of the most complex and difficult co-op games on Steam, where you and a friend play as ex-convicted cats trapped in a hellish prison.
You and your friend must work together to escape before time's up.
There are multiple traps, multiple paths, highly complex puzzles, and A LOT OF DEATHS! If this game doesn't test your friendship, nothing will.
Why you’ll love it
• Complex puzzle to figure out together

• Pure teamwork, Communication is key to figure all the puzzles.
