De qué trata

One Thief. Four Residents. One very bad night.
Not In My House is an online 1v4 home-invasion party game. The Thief has a shrink gun, a drone and night vision. The family has traps, their feet and a phone hidden somewhere in the house. Every round ends one of three ways: the Thief banks $5,000, the Thief sucks every Resident into his gun, or the police arrive.

How a round goes
• Scout. For 45 seconds the Thief flies a tiny drone through the house, spots loot and fires tracking darts at Residents. Residents can kick the drone away.

• Lights out. The power dies. Residents get flashlights. The Thief gets night vision.

• Rob or run. The Thief carries loot to his box while the Residents find the phone, call the police and hold out until they arrive.

Play the Thief

• Shrink gun. Shrink a Resident, then get close and suck them into your gun. Catch the whole family and you win.

• Steal big. Shrink the big loot too, so it fits in your pockets. Three jackpot items are worth a fortune.

• Cut the call. Find the phone and hang up on the police before it's too late.

• Tricks. Lock your loot box, see the Residents' traps, and disarm them by hand.

Play the Residents

• Traps. Banana peels, mouse traps, Lego, a rope topple, a fart cushion and a rubber duck. Plant them where the Thief will run.

• Kick him. A good kick knocks the Thief flat for a few seconds. When he gets up, back off: he sprays shrink.

• Call the police. The phone is in a different room every round. Find it, call, and survive until they arrive.

• Shrunk? Keep going. Tiny Residents squeeze through pet flaps in the doors, hide, and talk in a squeaky voice.

• Hide. Walk-in wardrobes, cupboards with real doors, and CCTV cameras to watch the house.
