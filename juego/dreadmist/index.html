DreadMist

Juego de PC · Indie, Rol, Simuladores · Indie, Rol (RPG), Simulación

DreadMist is a seamless open-world fantasy sandbox simulation. A deep mechanical sandbox where you can begin as a lowly beggar, recruit more characters, master crafting, trading, fighting, and buy property to create your own place in the world.

De qué trata

A seamless open-world fantasy sandbox simulation. Take control of one or multiple characters and exist as an adventurer, craftsman, trader, farmer, thief, and many other possibilities. Start with a single beggar, work up to a skilled team, buy properties, and build homes and businesses.
