De qué trata

Hand-me-downs is a roguelike deckbuilder where you play against your own old hands.
Your opponents don't have their own deck, so they borrow your cards!
After every face-off, you switch cards with your opponent, and you must face off against the very hand you just played the next time you encounter them.

Each opponent brings a unique twist to the table, so be careful which cards you let them have.
Whether it's The Bouncer restricting which cards you can play, or The Painter changing the suits after the switch, your opponents will make sure to complicate your plans.
And remember: their cards are your cards.

Each suit has its own flavor, and each rank its unique character.
Throughout the game you'll get familiar with the no-nonsense Clubs, the fortunate Coins, the jolly Cups and the ruthless Swords.
Face cards and aces have powerful effects that can shut down your opponent, or score a ridiculous amount of points.

Acquire powerful trinkets to manipulate your deck and counter your opponents' shenanigans.
Whether by increasing your score, suppressing your opponents, or manipulating your deck, trinkets play a crucial role in keeping up with your opponents.

Use totally-not-cursed tickets to corrupt your cards and gain an edge over your opponents.

Holding the corrupted cards makes you feel funny?
Do not worry about it.

The Shopkeeper insists on sharing their curses with you.
They make it sound like a good thing but nine out of ten curse experts agree: it is not a good thing.
In any case, refusing is NOT an option, so don't think about it too hard.
