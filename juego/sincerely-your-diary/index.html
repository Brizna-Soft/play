Sincerely, Your Diary

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

A quiet autumn evening in a small seaside town. The soft flicker of a monitor, drizzle outside, and your favorite tracks playing in your headphones. A simple "hi" from a stranger breaks the quiet. What starts as a chat to pass the time becomes a fragile thread connecting two very different worlds...

De qué trata

Sincerely, Your Diary is a linear visual novel that unfolds entirely on Matt’s computer. The story begins in 2009 in Clarenville, a small town in Newfoundland.
• Chat. Read and send messages in a late-2000s messaging app.

• Browse Matt’s folders. Open the photos the characters talk about in chat and learn more about Matt’s life through his personal files.

• Move on to the next day in the story. As the date changes, so do the views of Clarenville on the animated desktop wallpaper and the sounds of the seaside town.

What to Expect
This is a quiet, slow-paced story where ordinary chats between two teenagers about music, school, and photos slowly become the one place where they can say what they can’t share with anyone else.
There are no branching choices, puzzles, horror elements, or dating-sim mechanics. The full story takes about 6 hours of unhurried reading (around 2 hours for the free demo), and is best experienced in the evening, with headphones on.
