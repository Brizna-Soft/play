Esports Manager: FPS

Run a fictional tactical-FPS esports club from the bottom of the world rankings to the trophy. Sign players with real personalities, call the buys and timeouts on a live tactical radar, and see the reason behind every change in your season.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Esports Manager: FPS puts you in charge of a fictional tactical-FPS club. You start near the bottom of the world rankings and decide everything between you and a trophy: who plays, what they buy, which plan they run and when to stop and regroup.Watch the round, not a dice roll
Every round plays out on a live tactical radar. Players walk real routes across the map, hold angles and only fight what they can actually see. Smokes block sight, flashes blind, defenders rotate when they get information, and after a plant they regroup and retake. Trades and clutches happen because of where your players stand, and the round feed tells you who won each duel and where.Write the playbook
Give your team a named call for every map and every buy: a default, a fast execute onto one site, a split, a fake or a rush on attack, and a stack or an aggressive setup on defence. Run the same call too often and opponents start reading it. Share your playbook with a code, and see over the season which plans actually win you rounds.
Call it live
Between rounds you make the buy: save, force, half, full or a double AWP setup. When the team drops rounds in a row, burn a tactical timeout and steady them.
Know your odds
Ratings reflect what a player actually brings to the server, so a stronger team on paper is a stronger team in the match. Before every match you see your win chance, and it moves as you prepare: counter their style, anti-strat their star, pay for a VOD review.Draft Cup
Want a quick run instead of a full career? Draft a five from a random pool on a fixed budget, one player per role, and take them through a knockout cup. Every draft is different.Sign people, not numbers
Every player has a personality as well as ratings.
