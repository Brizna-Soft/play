颂声·市井小神仙

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Rol · Indie, Rol (RPG), Simulación

Your parents left you their old inn in Yueming Town. Hire staff, cook new dishes and balance the books. Befriend 87 neighbors, haggle at the market, trade across eight towns, teach at the school, play matchmaker and grow flowers. An ink-wash Song-dynasty town, and a life of your own.

An old inn is now yours

Your parents said "off traveling" and vanished, leaving you the inn. You've never run a shop: you learn the books, handle difficult guests and fix the roof yourself. Hire staff, send buyers to the market, take orders and settle up, then close the ledger at dusk. As business grows the inn rises in rank, and a finished upper floor brings new staff and private rooms.

Skill at the stove

New dishes are worked out at the stove in a hands-on minigame. Famous recipes come from caravans, auctions and cricket fights. Regulars have their own tastes, and their praise or complaints go into the guestbook.

87 neighbors, 87 lives

Fishmongers, teachers, waiters... each of Yueming's 87 neighbors has a trade and a temper. Grow from acquaintance to friend to confidant, unlocking their inner thoughts and perks of their trade: daily fish deliveries, or a quiet tip on tomorrow's prices.

Small gifts, warm hearts

Give a candied haw or a bunch of flowers and they'll answer back, and their feelings for you will shift.

Heart red, reason blue

Every choice leans toward warmth or principle, and it all gathers into a red-and-blue tassel the town remembers.

Tricks of the market

Seven kinds of food rise and fall in price each day. Friends share forecasts, vendors offer daily delivery, and Liu Yi will split bulk orders to bring the price down.

Flowers and greens out back

Grow balsam, morning glories, radishes and crown daisies. Order rare seeds from out of town, send flowers to someone by morning, and fill your album.

Guild, estates and auctions

Become a guild patron: send caravans to Bianjing, Yunjin, Rongwan and more, and push Yueming up the eight-town rankings.
