Seek out gifted beautiful girl warriors, form them into a powerful force, and lead them to defeat monsters, obtaining top-tier weapons and items. You can also freely build your base and make it your dream home.

De qué trata

Core Gameplay：
This is a game focused on hack-and-slash combat, loot farming, and character collection and development. Seek out powerful warriors, subdue them, and recruit them into your team to fight by your side. As character attributes continue to improve, the special effects and bonuses of top-tier weapons will grant ever greater combat power and an increasingly exhilarating combat experience.
Game Features：
The appearances of all female characters in the game are randomly generated and unique.You can collect the characters you like according to your own preferences.
Build your home freely：
On the game's main map, you can build freely. The game offers a rich variety of building modules, as well as natural landscape models such as plants and rocks. you can create your dream home. On the main map, you can also enable a weather system and freely set the weather and time to experience different lighting and atmospheric effects.
