A free-to-play fantasy strategy card game of bluffs, timing and clever deckbuilding. Four factions, over 200 cards, a four-act story campaign, five AI difficulties and ranked leagues. Win two rounds out of three to take the match.

De qué trata

Runewards is a free-to-play fantasy strategy card game of bluffs, timing and clever deckbuilding. Play the story campaign, test your decks against the AI, or climb the ranked leagues against players on PC and Android.

Every unit you put on the Battlefield has Power. When a round ends, the side with the higher Total Power wins it, and the first to win two rounds takes the match.
The real game is in the choices. Every card has an ability that can strengthen your line, weaken your opponent's or turn the whole board around. Do you Pass early and save your best cards for the next round, or put everything on the table now?

• Humans, Nature and Monsters each bring their own Heroes, units, spells and style of play.

• Mercenaries fight for anyone and fit into every deck.

Combine abilities, chain them together, and build the deck that fits the way you play.

Try a new deck against the AI first. Five difficulty levels take you from Easy to Insane.

Climb the ranked leagues all the way to the top: Archon. Add friends, see who's online, chat, and challenge them to a friendly match.

Collect cards and build decks for every faction. Craft the cards you're missing and salvage the ones you don't need.

Earn new cards just by playing. Daily quests and achievements reward you with Gold, and Gold opens chests full of new cards.
