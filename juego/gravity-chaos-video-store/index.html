Gravity Chaos: Video Store

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

Gravity is back. The tapes aren’t. Sort thousands of scattered VHS tapes in a neon-lit retro video store. Match movies to their shelves, unlock unusual upgrades, and take a break with retro computer games. Turn chaos into order, one satisfying row at a time.

De qué trata

A gravity event. One messy video store.
Small gravity holes appeared without warning, pulling objects loose and scattering them before disappearing. While the rest of the world tried to explain what happened, your video store was left with its entire collection on the floor.
The shelves need filling again.

A first-person video store organization simulator
Gravity Chaos: Video Store takes place in a neon-lit retro rental shop with 3,920 VHS tapes scattered throughout the store.
Use movie titles and cover designs to find matching tapes, then bring them to the correct genre section. Each row holds eight copies of the same movie. The store computer’s catalog helps you keep track of the collection.
Completing rows earns you gravity bubbles, which you can spend on upgrades at the Anomaly—a strange remnant of the gravity event.

Turn a scattered collection into a stocked store
• Satisfying progress: Turn piles of scattered tapes into complete rows of matching movies. Each finished row brings the store closer to being fully stocked.

• Plan your approach: Focus on one genre, collect matches across several sections, or finish partly filled rows. Decide which upgrades will help you most as you work.

• A retro setting: Sort through the collection in a video rental shop filled with neon lighting, colorful movie covers, and old computer games.

• An extra challenge: Work toward a faster completion or finish the entire store without buying any upgrades.

Upgrades from the Anomaly
As you complete rows, you can unlock abilities that make finding, carrying, and shelving tapes easier.
• Carry Capacity: Hold more tapes at once and bring larger stacks back to the shelves.

• Copy Collector: Gather nearby copies of the same movie into your hands.
