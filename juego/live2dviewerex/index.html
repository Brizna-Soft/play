Live2DViewerEX is an unofficial cross-platform Live2D viewer, it supports Windows, MacOS, Android and iOS.Features
• Cross-platform (Windows, MacOS, Android, iOS)

• Three modes: Wallpaper Mode, Desktop Mode (Fullscreen, Work area and Borderless window) and Window Mode
