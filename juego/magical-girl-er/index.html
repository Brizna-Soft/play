Magical Girl ER

Juego de PC · Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

A medical simulation where you examine and treat magical girls injured in kaiju disasters. With limited hospital beds and stamina, decide who to accept, who to refuse, and which treatment to choose.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Magical Girl ER is a horror medical simulation and decision-making adventure game where you treat magical girls injured in kaiju disasters. Each playthrough takes approximately 20–40 minutes.
Your decisions determine whether the magical girls live or die, the hospital's reputation, and which of the 11 endings you'll reach.

Each patient may be suffering from a different hidden condition, such as physical trauma, internal damage, poisoning, mental instability, or magical contamination. The correct answer is not shown directly. You must infer what is wrong from examination results, dialogue, appearance, documents, and other clues, then choose the appropriate treatment.
Your decisions determine whether the magical girls live or die, the hospital's reputation, and which of the 11 endings you'll reach.
