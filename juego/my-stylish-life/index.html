My Stylish Life

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

It's 2008, and you just sat down at the family computer. Run your own boutique, build your dream wardrobe, make new friends, fall in love, and explore a colorful world where every day is yours to style. Welcome to My Stylish Life.

De qué trata

Your stylish life is waiting...
My Stylish Life is a fun and cozy 2000s-inspired fashion life sim where getting dressed is only the beginning. Run your own fashion boutique, build your dream wardrobe, experiment with hair and makeup, shop around town, decorate your apartment, make friends, go on dates, and live each day your way!
Fashion is at the heart of everything, so what will your stylish life look like?

Run Your Own Fashion Boutique!
Take over your very own boutique and style customers for a wide variety of requests! Work within their style and/or budget, earn money for great looks, and grow your Boutique to unlock new fashion collections, customers, and become the talk of the town!

Build Your Dream Wardrobe!
Shop hundreds of fashion pieces across different brands and boutiques, discover secondhand treasures at the thrift store, and build a wardrobe that's completely your own. Mix, match, and dress for whatever the day brings!

Make Every Look Yours!
Complete your look with Beauty! Change your hairstyle, hair color, makeup, accessories, and more whenever you're ready for something new.

Live Your Stylish Life!
Make your apartment your own by customizing it, explore the city, grab a coffee, work side gigs, attend special festive events, and spend each day at your own pace. There's always something to do, but no need to rush through it all! Along the way, experience an unfolding story as you settle into your new life and discover more about the people and world around you.

Make Friends & Find Romance!
Meet characters around town, exchange messages, receive gifts, and discover their stories through light visual-novel style elements as your relationships grow! You never know... you might even find someone special along the way.
