Calvar - The Darkest Gate

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie, Rol (RPG), Simulación, Terror

CALVAR – THE DARKEST GATE is a first-person psychological horror game created by a two-person indie team. Explore a world of fractured memories, ritual corruption, disturbing creatures, and an abandoned asylum where reality itself begins to unravel.

De qué trata

EARLY ACCESS — CURRENT CONTENT & PLAYTIME
CALVAR is still in development. The full game is not yet playable. The current version includes the Prologue, Chapter 1, and the first part of Chapter 2, offering approximately 40–90 minutes of gameplay, depending on your pace and exploration.
The game will continue to grow throughout Early Access, with additional content updates expanding the playable areas and continuing the story. Your feedback helps us improve and shape the experience along the way.

CREATED BY A TWO-PERSON INDIE TEAM
Calvar is a passion project built by two people—and your feedback helps us improve it throughout Early Access. Found a bug, got stuck, or have a suggestion? Let us know through the Steam Community discussions, our Discord, or email. A description, screenshot, or short video helps us investigate. Thank you for helping us shape Calvar.

BEYOND THE GATE LIES ONLY MADNESS.
Leonard has left the asylum, but his past has followed him home. His wife, Tatjana, is missing. After stopping his medication, unsettling experiences begin to disturb his search—and his understanding of what is real.
Calvar – The Darkest Gate is a first-person psychological horror game focused on exploration, environmental puzzles, and disturbing encounters. Follow Tatjana’s traces and uncover the connection between Leonard’s past, the asylum, and the mystery of Calvar.

FOLLOW THE PATH INTO THE UNKNOWN

Follow secluded forest paths and investigate abandoned places as Leonard’s search draws him further from home. Discover documents, personal belongings, and hidden clues that reveal fragments of the story. Look carefully: the obvious path may not hold every answer.
