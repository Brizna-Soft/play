De qué trata

• Unknown Hero's Travel Diary is a third-person action-adventure game. You play an obscure adventurer, taking on contracts, exploring and fighting your way across a vast world. As your journey deepens, you gradually uncover the hidden rules and darker secrets of this kingdom — and when its fate hangs in the balance, a choice falls to you.

Large cities of very different character, and many small villages, await your exploration.

Non-player characters live by their own daily routines.

Answer the King's bounties and hunt down the monsters that have taken over the roads.

Or take on the harder contracts, and face the legendary creatures behind them.

And when the time comes, the fate of the kingdom hangs in the balance: throw in with a faction and tip the balance of power, or stand alone and challenge the mightiest foe.
Every choice you make may rewrite the legend that will be yours.

The game is currently in Early Access and already includes core exploration, combat and a contract system, along with a portion of the playable regions. Maps, story, NPC interaction and gameplay depth will continue to expand in future updates.
