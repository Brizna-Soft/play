De qué trata

Sinkhole Outlaw Chase, the first suspense-adventure live-action interactive game under Tianxia Bachang's IP, is coming soon!

Cross the mountains—fate can be reversed!
Deep in the primeval forests of Laoyeling in Northeast China, a joint scientific expedition team of Chinese and foreign scholars ventures into the mountains for exploration—only for the entire team to vanish without a trace, leaving no word behind.

An emergency rescue mission is immediately launched. You are Zhao Gong, a rescue team leader summoned at a critical moment, skilled both in communications repair and Japanese translation. Joining you are the seasoned hunter Da Saibangzi, guard Xiao Chen, archaeologist Shirley, and a Japanese research team including geology professor Takagi Ichiro, assistant Nakayama Ranko, and Kobayashi Kenji. Together, you rush into the dense forest to search for the missing expedition members.

The treasures of the Golden Forest and the secrets beneath the earth—dig deep, and the final outcome is yours to decide.
Undercurrents stir within the team: Chinese and foreign members hold differing stances and conceal unpredictable intentions; deep-rooted prejudices of the mountain villagers and mounting internal conflicts within the rescue team threaten to erupt at any moment. As tensions reach a standoff, strange occurrences begin to unfold in the dark forest: an unseen mysterious force drives the group away, eerie flying monkeys launch repeated attacks, and danger closes in step by step.

A sudden accident sends the entire rescue team plunging into an isolated, hidden sinkhole. Forbidden mountain grounds and underground mysteries emerge layer by layer, while all manner of bizarre events follow one after another.
