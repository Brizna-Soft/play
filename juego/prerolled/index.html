Prerolled

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie

Sell boxes of dice to silly clients in this cozy roguelike deckbuilder! Each die has unique effects based on the positioning of other dice in the box! Create your own crazy combos, make numbers go up, and overcharge shamelessly to pay rent!

De qué trata

Prerolled is a silly roguelike deckbuilder where you own a dice shop and pull lots of social powermoves!

Dice, Stickers, Legal Loopholes

• Discover and abuse 130+ unique dice faces

• Slap some stickers on dice to replace their faces for good

• Roll a nat twenty on a 6 sided die

• Create mind-boggling combos with 60+ shop upgrades

• Prove to the FBI that you are legally overcharging clients by thousands of dollars

• Get rewarded for being clever and breaking the game

• Unlock new dice, new upgrades, new boxes and extra features!

• Spice things up with optional challenges of increasing difficulty

• Change identity at the sight of any minor inconvenience

• Meet derpy clients with oddly specific needs

• Solve special puzzle boxes or talk to random strangers!

• Pay rent!
