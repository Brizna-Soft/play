A big trip for a small raccoon! In this lovingly crafted and colourful Match 3 adventure you travel all the way to the Amazon River. You'll accompany Ricky Raccoon. This young raccoon is going to embark on his very first treasure hunt! Luckily, Ricky's grandpa, himself a notable and famous treasure hunter, is going to join you, too. Will they ever find the long-lost Aztec treasure?

In this game you'll explore five detailed exotic locations and solve exciting puzzles in a mysterious Match 3 adventure.
