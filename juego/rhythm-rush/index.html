Description!
Play your own songs to three different game modes on, newly updated to, eight different maps, or fight the three unlockable bosses! Play on four different difficulties to get a range of challenge or create your own with the new custom difficulty! Keep track of your stats and compare them to your friends! Earn all the achievements by playing and getting better at it! Play more songs on harder difficulties to rank up faster! It is easy to grasp but hard to master! Play any song you want by just dragging them into a folder! The game reads MP3s, OGGs, and WAVs!

Three Game Modes To Play!
Play "FOLLOW" to follow a line that fluctuates with the song while dodging objects spawning with the beat of the song!
Play the classic mode "DODGE" and dodge obstacles that spawn with the beat of the song!
Play "FIGHT" to follow the line and kill waves of enemies that spawn with the beat of the song!
