Rocks to Riches

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

Start with a pickaxe and a plot. Dig ore, haul it to the trucks and pour the money into a bigger operation, then sell the whole thing and start again richer (or don't)! A cozy mining tycoon that takes you from a backyard mine to a lost jungle city and out to the asteroid belt.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Dig. Haul. Get rich. Sell it all. Do it again. Unlock more places, become more efficient..
Rocks to Riches starts with you the miner, your trusty pickaxe and the mine. Break and gather the ores, carry huge loads around, and purchase upgrades to create even more satisfaction.
Build an operation
Various resources across different zones
Employees, and technologies to unlock to help you work even faster
Complete contracts including rare legendary jobs
Fill a museum with the strange specimens your explorations turn up
Sell up and start again richer
When the operation is worth enough, sell it. Every sale raises your multiplier (or don't if you really enjoy the grind)!
Three places to strike it rich (So far)
The Yard - An abandoned old mine
The Lost City - The city hidden deep in an ancient forest, untouched for thousands of years
The Belt - Mining.. but in space! Asteroids, drones, rockets, and comets..
Made to be satisfying
Moving large piles, gathering feels good, and of course getting more powerful is the goal. Plenty of miner customization options. Vehicle customization options planned in the near future!
