De qué trata

An online RPG where you catch dinos on the island of Moruah with a snare and raise them as pets. Your pet attacks whatever you attack. Pets are the main force in battle.Catching pets
• 48 kinds of pets live on Moruah. Each dino has a behavior (Placid, Skittish, Wary, Curious or Hostile) that changes how it fights.

• The lower a dino's HP, the easier it is to catch. Higher-grade dinos need better snares.

• Lv 1 pets appear only at 48 fixed spots on the island.

• Pets raised from Lv 1 grow more than pets that gained their levels in the wild.

Raising pets and Bloodlines
• Fight alongside your pet. Petting it, feeding it and defeating enemies together raise its Bond and make your pet stronger.

• Every pet has a Temper: Fierce, Stout, Nimble, Tenacious or Balanced. The Temper decides which stats grow best.

• Hatchkeeper Bedan appraises your pet and reveals its Bloodline. There are five Bloodlines, from Sand (D) to Obsidian (S), set when the pet is caught.

• The better the Bloodline, the more stats the pet gains each level. The top Bloodline is Obsidian (S).

• When a pet is appraised as Obsidian, hunters in the same area are notified.

• Link your pet in chat and others can open its Pet Card to compare its stats with the average for the same kind and Temper.

Unique Pets and Tinted dinos
• The island has day and night. A game day lasts 20 minutes, and the weather changes between Clear, Rain and Thunderstorm. Rain and storms affect Water, Fire and Thunder attacks.

• Six Unique Pets appear only at set places, at set times of day, in set weather or after certain conditions are met.

• Unique Pets can only be caught with a Blackstone Snare. Bring the materials to Mason Torke and he will make one.

• Rarely, a wild dino appears as a Tinted dino.
