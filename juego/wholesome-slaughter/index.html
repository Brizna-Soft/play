Wholesome Slaughter

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie · Acción, Indie

Wholesome Slaughter is a retro style FPS set in a fantasy world! After stumbling into an unknown dimension, you must fight your way through hordes of the cute and innocent inhabitants of the land.

De qué trata

A Modern Retro Shooter Set In A Unique Environment:

Wholesome Slaughter brings our protagonist to Sugar Land, the happy home of several different mythical creatures who are excited to welcome you as one of their own. Unfortunately for them, this new menace, [You], does not share the same views of Love and Positivity, something they will quickly learn as you begin your journey.

Enjoy A Wide Variety Of Weapons:

• Combat Pistol

• Pump-Action Fun Gun

• Mood Enhancement Rifle

• Firework Launcher

• Unicorn Shiv

• Holy Hand Grenades

A Linear Campaign Experience:

Wholesome Slaughter includes a mini-campaign, featuring 4 distinct levels, each bringing a new field to paint with Blood!

Battle Through Waves Of Enemies In The Arena:

In arena mode, the only end in sight is death! Fight off as many waves as possible with scaling difficulty and health/ammo respawns.

An Original Soundtrack:

Rip and tear to the original soundtrack of Wholesome Slaughter, produced by musical composer John Fio!

About Us:

This game is being made by a student development team! Check out the team behind the slaughter and feel free to reach out to us through our official Twitter account.
