珠宝之王

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Estrategia, Indie, Simulación

Run a vintage jewelry business: hunt for bargains, haggle, appraise and sell to the right customer. Build your collection from thousands of pieces, balance stock against the bills, and solve 500 jewelry puzzles to earn new treasures.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

A beautiful piece is not always a good deal.

King of Jewelry is a single-player game about trading vintage jewelry, building a collection and solving logic puzzles. From a ring found at a flea market to a matching set in your vault, learn to buy, appraise and sell while keeping enough cash for tomorrow.Find the piece. Make the deal.
Visit flea markets, antique fairs, private collections and more exclusive venues as your business grows. The jewelry is there to see, but its value takes judgment. Is the asking price too high? Does the material match the claim? Has a flaw been overlooked? Use your expertise to find a reason to haggle, then decide whether to buy or walk away.Appraise before you commit
Bring your purchases back for appraisal and discover what they are worth. Inspect further or accept the current estimate. Sell to a buyer now or keep the piece for another opportunity. A poor purchase can tie up your money; a well-chosen one can keep the business moving.Find the right customer
Customers have different tastes, and their offers may disappoint. Accept a price, negotiate again or keep a favorite in your vault. Take on collection orders, gather pieces that match the requested materials and categories, and deliver the set when it is ready.Grow your skills. Keep the books balanced.
Choose specializations in sourcing, appraisal and sales to shape your approach. Stock on the shelf is not cash in hand: weekly fees and interest still need paying, and tomorrow brings another buying trip. Celebrate reaching one million in-game cash, then keep trading for as long as you can sustain the business.500 jewelry puzzles, at your own pace
Enter the puzzle mode directly from the main menu.
