De qué trata

Supports Japanese and Simplified Chinese text. Includes both Japanese and Chinese voice audio.
『The Guestbook of the Otherworld』 is a hot-spring horror simulation that blends horror, visual novel storytelling, and simulation gameplay.
The story takes place in a world that belongs neither to this shore nor the next — the Otherworld.
In this realm, forgotten things drift ashore and wander as tsukumogami. There, a single hot-spring inn quietly stands.
You awaken as an employee of the inn, with no memories of who you are.
You do not remember how to do your job. And yet, strangely, your body still knows how to move.

The guests who visit the inn are beings that have fulfilled their roles in the human world, been forgotten, and become tsukumogami.
Each of them carries Kegare, a sinister impurity that eats away at the soul.
By soaking in the hot springs, enjoying warm meals, and resting in peace, their Kegare gradually falls away.
But the Kegare shed by the guests does not simply disappear.
It accumulates within the inn, and each night, it manifests as supernatural horrors that come for you.

By day, tend to the inn and heal the tsukumogami
By day, your role is to welcome and care for the tsukumogami who visit the inn.
Prepare guest rooms, help with meals, manage the hot springs, and patrol the building.
As you work and speak with the guests, you will gradually uncover the Kegare they carry and the pasts they left behind.
The choices you make during the day will influence the supernatural events that occur at night — and your chances of survival.

By night, survive an inn overflowing with Kegare
When night falls, the Kegare shed during the day begins to overflow throughout the inn.
Corridors stained red. Guest rooms that should be empty.
