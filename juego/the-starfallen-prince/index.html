De qué trata

Play as the Young Prince Belden
Step into the role of Prince Belden and fight to survive a world thrown into chaos. Armed with his Father's sword and the determination to protect his people, guide the young Prince through dangerous battles and help reenact vengeance for the loss of his Father.

Fight Waves of Enemies
Face relentless waves of enemies as they close in from every direction. Fight your way through each encounter, grow stronger, and survive increasingly dangerous swarms as time goes on.

Level Up, Power Up!
As you fight your way through each encounter, gain Experience to level up and choose from a selection of Upgrade options. Each Upgrade offers a different way to strengthen your abilities, giving you the freedom to shape your build and adapt to the challenges ahead.

Strength Beyond The Battlefield
Not all progress ends when the run does. Use the Experience you earn from your encounters to unlock permanent upgrades, strengthening The Prince and giving you new advantages for future expeditions.
