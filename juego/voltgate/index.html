De qué trata

Small robots. Loud arenas. Big knockouts.
VOLTGATE is a free-to-play arena shooter about tiny, toy-bright robots that hit way above their weight. Build your robo, pick your fight, and play to a crowd that wants a show. First or third person, mouse or controller.

Pick your fight
• 1V1. The classic duel. Every round ends in a knockout, first to three takes the match, and the killcam rides your rocket all the way in.

• FREE FOR ALL. Four robots, one arena, respawns. First to 10 knockouts, or the most when the five-minute clock runs out.

• 2V2. Bring a partner. Two teams, rounds like the duel, first to three. Your bolts never hurt a teammate.

• HEADLINER. Co-op for up to four. Survive the opening waves, take a commercial break, then face the night's headliner on THE DUNES. Hard, and only beatable together.

• Parties. Up to four friends through a Steam invite or a party code. Queue together, and empty seats fill with CPU robots so nobody waits forever.

• Private rooms. Send a friend the room code and settle it.

Every online match runs on our own match servers. No host advantage.

Build your robo
Five slots: body, primary, secondary, jetpack, dash. Mix them however you like.
• Bodies: Spark, the all-rounder. Bulwark, the heavy that soaks it up. Scout, fast, small and fragile.

• Primaries: the Volt Carbine, steady and accurate. The Rapid Gun, which sprays and runs hot. The Scatter Cannon, brutal up close. The Rail Gun: hold to charge, let go to end someone.

• Secondaries: the Rocket Launcher, one slow rocket and a very big boom. The Missile Pod, three homing missiles. The Grenade Launcher, for corners and cover. The Vortex Shield, which catches shots and throws them back.
