Winter Wonderland: Tidy Up Together

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

After the busiest night at Winter Wonderland, everything has ended up in one giant pile. Team up with up to 8 friends to sort nearly 3,000+ festive treasures back where they belong, hang the baubles and switch the magical Winter Wonderland lights back on, one cosy armful at a time.

De qué trata

The party's over… now for the tidy-up!
The Winter Wonderland had its busiest night ever. Now the visitors have gone home, the snow is falling, and every bauble, present, mitten, mug and teddy bear in the park has somehow ended up in one enormous pile in the middle of the square.

Grab your friends, climb the pile and put everything back where it belongs before morning. Every item you return brings a little more magic back to the park.

One giant pile, 3,000+ things to sort
• 60+ kinds of festive things across 16 categories: baubles, presents, fairy lights and lanterns, ice skates, sledges, skis and snowboards, hats, mittens and scarves, mugs, plush prizes, market gifts, sweets, wooden toys, decorations, bakery treats and lost property.

• 35 places to return them: the Giant Christmas Tree, little trees to decorate, Santa's sleigh, ski and sledge racks, the skate hire hut and a whole market of cosy wooden chalets.

• Watch the pile shrink as you go, and climb it to reach the treasures at the top.

Bring the park back to life
• Finish a category and its part of the park lights up again: the Giant Tree, the ice rink, the fairy-light festoons and more.

• The carousel starts turning, the Ferris wheel lights up and the light-up reindeer glow as the whole crew makes progress.

• Fireworks for the big milestones, and a sparkling finale when the Wonderland is fully restored.

Better together
• Online co-op for 1–8 players. Host a public, friends-only or invite-only park and join friends through Steam.

• Built-in voice chat (push-to-talk or open mic) and text chat.

• Tips are shared by the whole crew. Spend them together at the Wonderland Office.

• Pack a snowball and lob it at your friends. Purely for team morale.
