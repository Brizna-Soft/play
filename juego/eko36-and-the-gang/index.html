De qué trata

Eko36 and the gang is a driving and drifting game about good roads, sideways driving, and having a good time behind the wheel.
Take your car out for a drive and explore winding touge roads, tracks, city streets and parking lots. Drift through corners, link your slides together, chase your friends, or just cruise around and enjoy the scenery.
DRIVE YOUR WAY
Forget chasing perfect lap times. Find your own lines, experiment with the cars, and get better at keeping the slide going.
BUILT FOR DRIFTING
Drifting is at the heart of the experience. Experiment with different cars, tune your setups, find the right lines and push your skills as you learn to control the slide.
DRIFT, RACE, OR JUST CRUISE
Take on different roads and tracks, go for a high-score drift session, race your friends, or simply head out for a drive with no particular destination.
BRING THE GANG
Jump online with your friends and drive together. Tandem drift, race, cruise, mess around, hit each other's cars, or turn an ordinary drive into something completely stupid.
CARS WITH PERSONALITY
Build and drive a collection of iconic cars with different handling characteristics, setups and personalities. Tune them to suit your driving style and take them wherever you want.
Stylised low-poly visuals, loud engines, questionable builds and plenty of smoke.
No need to take it too seriously.
Grab a car, find a good road, go for a drive and have a good time.
