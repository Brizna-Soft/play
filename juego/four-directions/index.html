De qué trata

"Four Directions" is a turn-based strategy game that builds upon hardcore tactical combat with a unique twist: a rotatable Rubik's Cube-style battlefield. This dynamic terrain system keeps the battlefield evolving, while cleverly integrated mechanisms and card skills deepen the strategy and enhance level design.

• The rotatable cube elevates the battlefield from 2D to 3D, creating ever-changing tactical opportunities. Master its rotations to turn the tide in extreme situations.

• With up to a 10x10 grid, the game preserves the grand-scale, iron tide warfare of classic tactical games across complex terrain.

• Command 15 unique heroes—like Swordsmen, Axemen, Spearmen, and Archers—each with weapon-triangle advantages to break through enemy lines.

• Multiple team compositions and progression paths allow you to train and reclassify your destined heroes, evoking the deep customization hardcore fans love.

• Each chapter features carefully designed mechanisms. Uncover their secrets, use them to ambush foes, and carve a path to victory.

• Remember: healing mechanisms restore enemy HP but also grant you EXP—making your growth unstoppable.

• Utilize various card skills, including stat boosts, cloning, invisibility, position swap, and teleportation.

• Teleport cards let your heroes strike from anywhere, eliminating key targets instantly. Deploy these skills wisely to reverse the battle’s outcome.
