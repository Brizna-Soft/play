ABOUT THIS GAME
The Ice is Watching is a first-person psychological horror experience that plunges you into a desolate, frozen nightmare. You don't play as a hero. You play as a desperate woman driven by a single question: Where is he?
Tracing your lover's last known location to a remote mountain cabin, the temperature drops and a chilling realization sets in: you are not alone. A looming figure known only as "The Ice" is here. And he's been expecting you.

BREAK THE CYCLE
Your journey begins in the artificial warmth of a city hotel, fueled by social media clues and fragile hope. But as the rain turns to blinding snow and the road vanishes into white nothingness, hope shatters.
You reach the cabin only to find your escape sabotaged: the battery is gone, the tires are missing. You are no longer the seeker; you are the next target for a frozen collection. To break the cycle, you must face the stalker who watches from the pine tree shadows.

SURVIVE THE NIGHT
Seen through an immersive first-person view, the core experience focuses on survival, heat management, and stealth.
Thermal Survival. The cold is your primary enemy. Manage your body heat by scavenging firewood and keeping the hearth alive.

Unpredictable Stalker AI. The Ice, a mysterious and terrifying cowboy, is no scripted jumpscare. He observes, stalks, and hunts dynamically based on your noise and movement.
Environmental Puzzles. Scavenge for vehicle parts to make your escape while navigating psychological traps and deciphering chilling audio logs.

KEY FEATURES
• Atmospheric Terror: The vast, suffocating scale of the snowy wilderness contrasted with the claustrophobic dread of a cabin hiding dark secrets.
