De qué trata

BLUNDERBUSTED is a fast paced roguelike gallery shooter for modern lightguns, mouse (one-handed!) or touchscreen (SteamDeck support in progress) with arcade style quick play featuring strategic powerups, interactive stages, and tons of action. Created with 100% human-made content.

EARLY ACCESS FEATURES
• Three unique base weapons each with distinct strategic upgrade combos
- Blunderbuss scattershot for hitting dense crowds with huge stopping power
- Pistol for accurate headshots, critical hits, and lucky ricochets
- Repeater machine gun with huge magazines to spray and pray

• Branching path system with seven stages including unique boss levels
- Farm
- Graveyard
- Mines

• Classic arcade style action with quick play sessions

• Massive replayability with new gear unlocks for varied playstyles

• Support for all major light guns (Blamcon, OpenFire, Sinden, Gun4IR, Retro Shooter, Ultimarc, etc.)

UPCOMING FEATURES IN PROGRESS
• New Forest biome with 3 new stages

• Additional enemy types
- Wolves
- Lumberjacks
- Rat swarms

• Achievements / Quest system

• More interactibles

• More gadgets including passive auto-attackers
- Cthulu tentacle summons
- Model T road rage
- more wacky stuff!

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10 (64-bit)

• Procesador: Intel Core i3-10100 / AMD Ryzen 3 3300X

• Memoria: 8 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB / AMD Radeon RX 580 8GB

• DirectX: Versión 11

• Almacenamiento: 5 GB de espacio disponible
