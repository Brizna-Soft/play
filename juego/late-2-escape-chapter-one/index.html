LATE 2 ESCAPE : Chapter One

Juego de PC · Acción, Acceso anticipado · Acción

Locked inside your own home on Halloween night, you wake up searching for your missing daughter while PTSD induced hallucinations consume reality around you.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Woken up suddenly, you play as Michael Salvatore, a father tormented by the sudden disappearance of his daughter, Haze..As you investigate Haze’s room, you’ll uncover tools like the axe embedded in her bedroom wall  that may seem like  the only way forward.

LATE 2 ESCAPE: CHAPTER 1 includes a a twisted compelling story that gets better through each chapter.While playing Chapter 1 you will collect items that belongs to your daughter Haze.You will have to use matches, an Axe and other limited items to survive.

Light the Way
Matches are your main sources of light and your only comfort. Use them to
ignite candles that reveal hidden symbols, paths, or ward off the oppressive
darkness creeping in around you."Matches don’t grow on body bags, use them
like your life depends on it".

PTSD Moments
Step into Michael’s fractured reality, where trauma, guilt, and haunting memories twist the world around him. Experience multiple PTSD states, each bringing its own disturbing hallucinations, unsettling sounds, and psychological effects that blur the line between reality and nightmare. Find and use a syringe to suppress the symptoms and regain a sense of control. Or  refuse to take it and face the consequences as Michael’s mind continues to unravel. In Late 2 Escape, the deeper you descend into his memories, the harder it becomes to trust what you see, what you hear, and what is truly real.

Key Objects
Not everything in the house is what it seems, and some things are more important than others. Search your surroundings, uncover hidden items, and figure out what you need to keep moving forward. In Haze’s room, you’ll find an axe stuck in the wall, a movie prop that you’ll have to pull free yourself in a first person cinematic.
