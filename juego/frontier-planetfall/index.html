De qué trata

Make a home on an alien frontier
Frontier Planetfall is a single-player, first-person survival adventure about preparing for the journey ahead. Gather wood, stone and ore, craft tools and weapons, and turn a small camp into a home among the dinosaur herds.Explore a world of ruins and wildlife
Set out from Hearth Meadow into distinct regions, from Copperwood's giant trees and the turquoise landmarks of Bloomrock Wilds to the obsidian landscape of Blackglass and the machinery of Ironroot. Search ruined settlements, follow regional investigations and restore ancient machines to unlock new materials and equipment. Peaceful herds wander together, while predators and other hostile creatures make each expedition a risk.Build, craft and prepare
Construct shelters, place crafting stations and store supplies for your next trip. Hunt for food and materials, cook meals and keep your equipment repaired. Bring the tools you need to harvest trees, break stone and mine ore, then use what you bring home to improve your equipment and camp.Read the warning. Choose your moment.
Fight with spears, swords and bows against creatures with different attack patterns. Watch the marked ground, listen for attack cues and dodge before a strike lands. Regional guardians test your preparation, while the T-Rex in Blackglass and Stormwing above the floating islands bring larger encounters with changing attack patterns.Take your expedition into the sky
Progress beyond the ground regions to unlock a glider and build a survey aircraft. Reach floating islands, recover their archives and prepare for the dangers above.Return to the world you built
Save your expedition and return to your shelters, supplies and progress.
