Butterflies & Bloom

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

A cottagecore typing companion. A transparent flower garden lives just above your taskbar and blooms as you write, code, and chat. Sell flowers for coins, attract bees and butterflies, build a garden out of the hours you already spend at the keyboard.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

Butterflies & Bloom is a quiet typing companion that lives above your taskbar. A row of flowers grows there, one seed per slot, blooming a little more with every keystroke. There is no full-screen world to enter, no level to start. The garden is simply there, alongside everything else you're doing — writing, coding, replying to emails, working on a thesis — turning the hours you'd spend typing anyway into something soft and cumulative.
How It Works
Open the Shop, buy a seed for a few coins, plant it. Every key you press nudges that flower's growth meter forward. When it fully blooms, sell it back for twice what the seed cost — and use the profit to buy rarer, prettier, more expensive seeds. Rinse and repeat at your own pace. Whether you're a hunt-and-peck typist or hammering out a hundred words a minute, the garden keeps up with you.
The Garden Loop
• Buy seeds with coins

• Plant your garden

• Type — the more you type, the more they bloom

• Sell bloomed flowers for a profit

• Repeat

Bees, Butterflies, Dragonflies, Ladybugs
Four kinds of garden visitors drift through, each with a different gameplay effect: bees nudge growth forward without you typing, butterflies multiply your sell value, dragonflies make every keystroke worth more coins, and ladybugs slowly drip passive income. Each comes in five rarity tiers (Common through Legendary) with distinct color palettes — and every visitor is animated procedurally, so no two are exactly alike.
720+ Flowers to Discover
Twelve archetypes × six rarities × ten color palettes = 720 unique flowers. Rarer flowers take more keystrokes to bloom but sell for far more. The shop rotates every eight hours; the Mythic-tier flowers cost a small fortune and reward a small fortune. Patience is part of the loop.
