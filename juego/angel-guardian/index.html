De qué trata

Angel Guardian is a sci-fi side-scrolling action shooter built around fast combat, smart movement, and constant pressure.
After a sudden attack cripples the Space Station kingdom of Alterra, its systems turn against you — and survival becomes the mission.

Survive using multiple tools and playstyles:
• Tight, fast-paced shooting built for constant movement.

• Collect 8 double-upgradeable rifles plus a sword.

• Stealth options to avoid combat or strike quietly.

• Hacking systems to disable defenses, crack codes, and gain access to hidden areas.

• Deadly enemies and boss encounters that demand quick reactions.

Angel Guardian features Twitch Chat Integration that lets viewers affect the game in real time:

• Spawn enemies right in your path.

• Drop ammo when you’re desperate.

• Trigger Eiji’s superpowers.

Every run becomes unpredictable —share the IDs with your chat and let them unleash mayhem live while you fight to survive.

You play as Kita, a warrior guardian sent to extract a young prince after the attack.
As the journey unfolds, the prince fights alongside you, unlocking powerful abilities while you help him awaken his true potential as the kingdom’s rightful heir.
This is not an escort mission. This is a fight to take control back.
Tested by 100+ players across multiple closed test sessions.
