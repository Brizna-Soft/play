Wrong Tank!

Juego de PC · Casual

Three tanks. One arriving fish. Read its request, inspect the habitats, and send it to the right home. Build clean placement chains across 50 handcrafted shifts in this colorful pixel art aquarium puzzle game.

De qué trata

Welcome to the Fish Placement Department!

The fish keep arriving. Each one has an idea of home. Your job is to find the tank that actually fits.

Read the fish's request, inspect three aquariums, and choose the right one. A floor pattern, a low plant, a shell, an arch, or another small detail can make the difference. Later requests ask you to notice what a tank must not contain, too.

Audit shifts turn the task around: the fish are already in their tanks, and you must find the tank with a fish whose request is not met.

Read the clues. Find the right home.

• Read the signs. Match each request to the visible floor and objects in a tank.

• Keep the arrival belt moving. Place fish in sequence and build a clean combo.

• Explore 50 handcrafted shifts. Every shift presents three distinct aquariums. Complete all 50 on each of the four difficulties for 200 shift-and-difficulty completions.

• Choose your challenge. Play in Easy, Medium, Hard, or Hardcore mode.

• Come back for another assignment. A Daily challenge and an unlockable Endless mode bring more fish to place.

Rewards for a job well done

Earn up to three stars per shift, collect difficulty badges, and unlock 32 Steam achievements. Get paid for tougher work: first clears on higher difficulties award extra credits.

Make an aquarium your own

Earn credits from shifts, shop for fish and decorations, and arrange your own aquarium. Take a break to feed your fish and reward the crab.

Wrong Tank! is a colorful pixel art puzzle about noticing the one detail that tells you where the next fish belongs.
