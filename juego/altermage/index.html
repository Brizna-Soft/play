AlterMage

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Step onto the shores of AlterMage, a first-person action-platformer featuring spellblade focused combat. Embark on a harrowing journey to learn sorcery, explore labyrinthian passages within a forbidden citadel, and uncover the truth of a dark past.

Tráiler

De qué trata

You have come seeking sorcery? All desire power, but few have the wits to wield it. The forbidden isle has broken countless fools upon its shores. Do you think you shall fare any better?
If you are determined to try, then so be it. Those who pursue the occult will know sacrifice.

Story
In ages past, an exiled sorcerer built a great citadel upon a remote island. Concealed within this fortress, the sorcerer crafted spells abhorrent to life itself. Six scions of light arose against darkness and defeated the heretic. Now they lie long forgotten, and an ominous energy rises from the citadel once again.
Embark on a harrowing journey to explore the sprawling labyrinthian passages of the island. Set within a Magepunk world blending sorcery with technology; face arcane mechanized forces that scour trap-laden corridors. Every step may be your last as you traverse complex, dangerous environments that will challenge your spatial reasoning as often as your wits and reflexes.
Features
Explore a massive, interconnected world that offers branching paths, multiple parallel routes of progression, and a deep web of secrets. Decide your own course and discover artifacts that change how you traverse the environment and enable access to new locations.

Alternate between melee and magic seamlessly using an arsenal of blades and spells. Dodge, block, cast, and slash your way through foes as you expand your capabilities and craft your own strategies for overcoming the wide array of challenging enemies.
Experiment and redesign your spells using a unique spell alteration system. Fuse together different spell effects to create over one million possible combinations.

Every step, every spell, and every jump grants experience in a corresponding skill.
