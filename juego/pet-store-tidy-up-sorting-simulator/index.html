De qué trata

Welcome to Pet Store: Tidy Up Sorting Simulator! 🐾
Step into a messy pet store where everything is out of place! Your job is to clean up, sort items, and organize shelves to transform the store into a neat and welcoming paradise for pets and their owners.
From scattered pet toys and food bags to colorful accessories, every item has its perfect spot. Relax, take your time, and enjoy the satisfying feeling of putting everything back where it belongs!
Features:
• 🐶 Tidy Up! – Pick up scattered items and restore order to your messy pet store.

• 🧸 Sort & Organize – Arrange pet toys, food, treats, and accessories on the correct shelves.

• 🐱 Satisfying Gameplay – Enjoy relaxing sorting mechanics and cozy store environments.

• 🦴 Find the Perfect Spot – Place every item where it belongs and bring order to the chaos.

• 🐾 Relax & Unwind – No rush, just peaceful and satisfying tidying up!

Can you turn the messiest pet store into the tidiest shop in town?
