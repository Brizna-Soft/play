De qué trata

Factory Works Idle is an incremental factory builder where you grow a manual workshop into an automated empire spanning the full arc of technological history, then push past the Singularity into realities that don't obey the same rules.

Start by gathering wood and stone with your own hands. Construct mines, mills, and laboratories. Automate production across dozens of resources and buildings. Research your way through ten distinct technological eras, each with its own visual identity and unlocks, from primitive tools to quantum computing, fusion, antimatter, and beyond.

Build. Automate. Optimize.

Construct and upgrade facilities, from lumber mills and iron mines to quantum labs and singularity cores. Each building scales hundreds of levels deep. Deploy robot-powered automation modules to manage production and research while you plan your next expansion. Active players get combo multipliers, critical hits, and click upgrades; idle players watch automated chains compound while they're away.

Ten Eras of Progress
Advance through stone-age gathering to the ultimate technological transcendence. Every era introduces new resources, buildings, research, and visual themes. The path from manual labor to automated singularity is long, layered, and built for players who love watching numbers climb.

Beyond Reality: The Multiverse
When your factory outgrows this dimension, the Reality Forge awaits: a multi-phase mega-project that tears open parallel worlds. Each dimension you discover operates under its own rules: unfamiliar resources, unique production systems, and hostile forces that fight back against your expansion. Survive, adapt, and turn each new reality into another branch of your empire.

Link conquered worlds with cross-dimensional logistics.
