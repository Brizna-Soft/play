D.A.M.N!

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie

Outplay your friends with cards instead of out-rolling them! In a cheerful race to the finish: help your partner, get in your rivals' way, combo, knock out, freeze, use ults... Collect awards, compare stats. Solo, in a team, with bots, hot-seat, cross-play online or offline — no limits!

De qué trata

Outsmart them instead of counting on the whims of the die!
"He was one step from the finish — I froze him, and my partner picked him off."
"He brought out his mighty Tank, and I put a rocket into it, straight onto a mine that sent the big guy back to base."
"I needed a three and nothing else — my partner cast the Underdog's ult and left the number panel to me. Sadly, a rival knocked the Underdog out a step before my win. Why did I forget to raise the shield?! :("
That is what almost every turn looks like here: every move has an answer, and every answer has one of its own.
Before we made this game, we spent years inspired by the classic — and arrived at rules where, on that same classic board, skill really does decide a lot.
Play solo against bots, with friends at one screen, online with cross-play — or mix it all in one match. With a mouse, a gamepad, or a finger on a touchscreen.
The card mode
Plan combinations several turns ahead, so every turn sets up the next one. Run the game with cards instead of letting the die run you.
Everyone plays the same deck: movement cards, action cards (some of them very strong!) and junk cards you'd rather avoid. The card mode has three difficulty levels — they set how much junk clogs the deck, and the Scout class becomes your filter against it, which pays off most at the higher ones.
Hands are open, which is what makes playing at one screen easy — and what lets you "suggest" your rivals' moves worse than the ones they were hoping to find.
The die
It hasn't gone anywhere: three of the rule sets still use it. Two of the four leave the classic untouched — out of respect for it, and for the people who love it. Or for anyone who is simply more comfortable with a die.
