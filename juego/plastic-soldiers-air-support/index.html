Plastic Soldiers: Air Support

Air Support is a top-down arcade helicopter shooter in a world of plastic toy soldiers. Fly a Green Army gunship over backyard battlefields, blast the Tan Army, winch up cargo, and bring a friend in co-op.

De qué trata

Air Support is a top-down arcade helicopter shooter set in a world of moulded plastic. You fly a Green Army gunship low over picnic blankets, sandpits, snowbound prison camps and garden ponds, and everything down there wants you dead: tank columns, anti-air guns, gunboats, dug-in infantry, and the occasional giant ant.
Fast, readable, chunky action. Line up a strafing run, dump a salvo into a convoy, and winch a crate out of the wreckage before the next wave arrives.

Every mission is hand-built around a job, not a kill count.
• Sink a flotilla.

• Escort a steam train through hostile hills and find the battery it needs to finish the run.

• Light a chain of beacons across a night-time valley.

• Gas the ant hills before they overrun the camps.

• Free prisoners from a frozen camp.

• Hunt down propaganda towers hidden in a meadow of giant tree stumps.

• Hold a beachhead until the landing craft are ashore.

Start with the chin gun and earn the rest from weapon crates scattered across each mission. Rockets and cluster missiles punch holes in armour, napalm and the flamethrower set the whole field alight, and the Hail Mary ends the argument with a mushroom cloud. Grab a crate of door gunners and two soldiers lean out of the cabin and pick off targets on their own. Pick the right tool for what is shooting at you.

The nimble Huey or the heavier Apache. The Huey is a little faster; the Apache can take a little more damage. Pick the one that suits the mission before you take off.

Lower the rope to lift supply crates, rescue soldiers, carry a battery to a stalled train, or drag an enemy's stolen documents home. Your cargo swings on the end of the rope, so fly smooth.

Fly the whole campaign with a friend. Each of you picks an airframe; the enemy picks on both of you.
