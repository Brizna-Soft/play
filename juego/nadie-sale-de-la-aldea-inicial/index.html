De qué trata

Every adventure begins here. Yet no one ever truly leaves.

The lights are always on in the border town of Wayrest.

Cheap beds, steaming suppers, guild guides who patiently explain the rules, and simple commissions so carefully chosen that nothing ever seems to go wrong. For someone setting out on their first adventure, it is almost perfect.

People fondly call it the "starter village."

Only one thing seems wrong: no one who comes here ever seems to truly leave.

Seventeen-year-old Loren knows nothing of this. He knows only that his parents have recently vanished, and that an unsent letter of warning they left behind points to Wayrest.

He leaves Ping Village, where he grew up, determined to pass the Adventurers' Guild trial as his rite of passage while secretly searching for his parents. Once he earns his qualification, he can take up his sword and venture beyond the map, just as his father did when he was young.

But before his first commission is over, cracks appear in Wayrest's peaceful facade.

A departure record that does not match the facts. Someone who should have left long ago, still standing on a street corner. And a body in the mud outside town that no one is willing to identify.

Names are blotted out in the archives. Empty rooms still hold the fading warmth of their last occupants. Someone who spoke to you last night still remembers your name today, but has forgotten which hand they use. Deeper below, a mysterious voice calls to Loren from beneath a seal, claiming to know where his parents are.

With the help of guild guide Elia, Loren begins tracing the records that have been erased. But the truth is not only hidden in a locked archive.
