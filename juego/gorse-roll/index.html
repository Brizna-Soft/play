Gorse Roll

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual, Indie · Acción, Estrategia, Indie

Steer a ray-traced glass marble along floating roads of ice, mirrors and spinning floors. Race the clock through five worlds, hunt hidden sun crystals to earn a sixth, then build your own courses in an editor that traces every change as you make it.

De qué trata

Gorse Roll is a ray-traced marble game with a construction kit. Roll a marble over courses of stone, ice, mirrors and belts that float high above a rainforest in the morning mist, sea ice, a sea of cloud and a field of lava, through five worlds from a morning heath to gardens above the clouds, and a sixth that has to be earned. Every course can be changed, and new ones built, in the editor.
== One marble, and nothing to hold on to ==
You roll the marble like a trackball, with the mouse, a gamepad or the keys. There is no jump button, and nothing to steer with once you leave the floor: a marble flies where it was going, so every ramp, springboard and bouncer is decided before you reach it. Too slow and you fall short. Too fast and you fly over.
== Light you can look through ==

Every pixel is ray traced on your PC's own cores, so no ray-tracing graphics card is needed. The glass marble is a real lens: it bends the checkerboard under it, throws a small rainbow on the floor and shows up in the mirrors beneath it.
== Sunlight is a tool ==

Rest the glass marble on a sun plate and it focuses the sunlight like a burning glass. The ice wall of that colour melts, and the way is open.
== Floors that fight back ==

Discs and crosses turn and fling you outwards, the harder the faster they spin. Rockers tip from side to side. Sliders carry you across the gap if you get on at the right moment. Bouncers throw you back off their top just once, and your speed and your angle decide where you land. Add gates that stay open for seconds, lifts you have to race, crumbling tiles and climbs that only speed can get up.
== Three marbles ==

Glass focuses sunlight, but a long fall smashes it. Steel is heavy: chasers can't move it and long drops don't break it.

Requisitos

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 11, 64-bit

• Procesador: Quad Core, Intel Core i5-4460 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200

• Memoria: 2 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: Any graphics card, 1280 X 720 or higher

• Almacenamiento: 200 MB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: Any Windows sound device

• Compatibilidad con RV: no

• Notas adicionales: Graphics are ray traced on the processor, so the CPU sets the frame rate, not the graphics card. Expect 60 fps at 1280 x 720 and on most courses at 1920 x 1080.
