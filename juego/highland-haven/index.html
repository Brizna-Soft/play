Highland Haven

Juego de PC · Casual, Simuladores · Simulación

Discover your own private haven in the Scottish Highlands and bring this once thriving home for hairy “coos” back to life. Craft goodies to sell, optimize your farm, and grow a pasture of cute highland cows roaming free on the bottom of your screen in this relaxing idle game.

De qué trata

Welcome home to your own private Highland Haven! Your pasture has seen greener days, but with a little love and your first Highland cow, you can revive it into the farm of your dreams.

Highland Haven offers a cozy idle game perfect for everyone – from laid back gamers to farm simulation addicts. You can optimize your outputs to the max 📈 and spend your screen time with cute cow buddies 🩵.
Care for Your Cows 🥰
• Brush their coats and feed them hay to increase your friendship bond

• Shear their hair to gain textiles at the loom

• Gather their milk to make food at the dairy churn

Craft, Sell, Upgrade 🪙
• Craft materials from cow hair and milk

• Sell products to pay for cows, upgrades, and customizations

• Unlock upgrades and crafting stations to make more exciting (and valuable) items!

Restore Your Farm 🏡
• Upgrade the cottage to bring back its former beauty

• Hire helpers on your Haven to automate and optimize tasks

• Buy more cows to produce more resources (and increase screen-cuteness)

Unlock CUTE Accessories 👒
• Dress up your cows in cozy hats and accessories!

• Customize your cottage with unique colors and details!

• Stay tuned for more cuteness and customizations to come!

Your Haven awaits – get a mooo-ve on!
