Forbidden House

Juego de PC · Aventura, Free to Play · Aventura

Forbidden House is a first-person puzzle adventure with over 40 puzzles to solve.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Forbidden House is a free, first person point and click puzzle adventure game, inspired from old puzzle game classics like Myst, 7th Guest, Shivers. It features a mystery manor for you to explore and discover its secrets.

Enjoy:
• Classic point and click gameplay with first-person experience.

• Non linear exploration of the house.

• Learn the story of the 10 sacred gems.

• 2.5D pre-rendered backgrounds with real-time 3D characters

• Original music and narration

• 40+ puzzles to solve with many of them with three diffent difficulty levels which can be changed during the game play.

• For each sacred gem, solve also a tangram puzzle in a secret room.
