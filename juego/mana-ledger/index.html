Mana Ledger

Juego de PC · Free to Play, Contabilidad, Utilidades

A free financial and reference terminal for your Magic: The Gathering collection built around Secret Lair drops and preconstructed decks. See what you own, what it's worth, and whether it paid off. Local-first: no account, no cloud, your data stays on your PC.

De qué trata

The drop and the deck are the units that matter
Most collection trackers treat a Secret Lair as just another set code and a preconstructed deck as a pile of loose cards. Mana Ledger models the drop and the deck as things you bought at a price. It can answer the questions other trackers can't: Which drops made money? Should I crack this sealed box or keep it? Is the new drop worth buying at MSRP, or are the singles cheaper? What am I missing from this deck?
Secret Lair Explorer
• Every drop and superdrop, finish-aware; non-foil, Foil Edition, and Rainbow Foil are distinct SKUs with their own ownership and value

• Per-drop P&L: MSRP paid → current value → gain, with completion bars

• Crack-or-keep verdicts on sealed drops, and singles-vs-sealed comparisons while upcoming drops are estimated from each card's cheapest existing printing

• The SL Index: your Secret Lair holdings charted as an asset class

• An Upcoming strip of announced drops, with MSRPs and grouping synced from the community wiki

Precon Explorer
• Every physical preconstructed deck ever sold, 1993 to today including Commander precons, Challenger, Duel, Theme, Intro, and Planeswalker decks, Guild Kits, and more

• Full decklists in Gallery or sortable Table view, with finish-aware ownership

• A "Worth it?" panel: assumed MSRP vs. singles value vs.
