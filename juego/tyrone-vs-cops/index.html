TYRONE vs COPS

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie · Acción, Indie

Awaken the latent power of your melanin and kill cops in this thought-provoking game on police brutality in America.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Tyrone was minding his own business, when the cops rolled up with weapons drawn, claiming that they had a warrant for his arrest... It's time to teach those pigs a lesson! This is an experimental game exploring the reality of police brutality in America.
ONLINE CO-OP
Play single player or bring up to four thugs and enjoy online co-op play.
MANY CHARACTERS & MAPS
6 playable characters and 5 maps, with more being added weekly. Play objective, endless, and heist style maps.
KEY FEATURES
• Play as Tyrone

• Hold your gun sideways

• Shoot it at cops

• Many different types of law enforcement officers, including BOSS COPS

• Awaken the latent power of your melanin using kills from your past lives

• Receive assistance from cute Federal Agents

• Ranked progression system and unlockable cosmetics/emotes

• PvP system to settle gang related disputes
