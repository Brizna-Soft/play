De qué trata

Welcome to Mysteria Island!You have been summoned to a magical island called Mysteria Island, home to a variety of lively and adorable monster girls with distinct personalities — some gentle, some playful, and some bursting with energy.
The ruler of the island, the succubus demon lord April, makes a request to you:
“Help me make these slightly stubborn monster girls more united! In return, I’ll send you back to your original world.”A joyful journey combining strategy simulation and fantasy adventure begins!

Core Gameplay: Dice Board Strategy Game
• Classic yet deeply strategic board battles: Roll dice and move forward like in sugoroku or Monopoly, but with rich strategic layers.

• Land management and resource gathering: Purchase empty plots, upgrade lands, monopolize areas, and earn abundant magic power.

• Item tactics and interference: Use fun items to change dice rolls, disrupt opponents, skip turns, and engage in clever mind games.

• Over 10 unique maps: Each with different fantasy themes, bringing fresh adventure every time.

Characters
By playing multiple exciting matches, you’ll gradually deepen your friendship with the monster girls!
The island is home to 10 unique monster girls, each with their own dedicated stories and heartwarming interactions:
• April — The elegant and wise succubus demon lord who rules the island.

• Airi — A reliable all-purpose maid-type golem girl.

• Chachamaru — An energetic dog-ear girl full of vitality.

• Sakura — A gentle big-sister type taur girl.

• Mone — A dreamy dryad girl who loves flowers.

• Tromea — A cheerful party-loving matango girl.

• Damuda — A cute and airheaded zombie girl.

• Narika — A always-hungry slime girl.

• Nia — A mischievous ghost girl who loves pranks.

• Mizet — A battle-loving dragon girl.
