Auto Battle Buddies

Juego de PC · Casual, Estrategia · Estrategia

A cozy puppet-show auto battler where you accessorize your heroes with unique gear, build powerful teams, and battle other performers on the grand stage. Customize your puppets, climb the ranks, and create the ultimate Battle Buddies squad.

De qué trata

Manage gear, build your team, and get in the battle, Buddy!

• 15 classes(5 basic, 5 advanced, 5 master) with 2 new classes releasing each month for free

• 4 pieces of gear per class(head gear, armor, boots, weapon)

• 2 unique skills per class

• Combine your gear to create your favorite skill combos, or use full class sets to gain stat bonuses

• Tons of build variety

• Compete at your own pace in asynchronous battles against other battle buddy teams

• Win and raise your Battle Rating to climb the leaderboard

• Compete in a season to earn special rewards

• Fight seasonal boss battles

• Dress your avatar puppet in the dressing room to show off your style before the fight
