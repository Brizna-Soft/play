De qué trata

Terminus: Day Zero is a short zombie survival adventure about the first day of the outbreak, seen through the eyes of Daisy, a young girl who wakes up to an empty house.
At first the house is just too quiet. Daisy calls for Mom and Dad, and nobody calls back. Then the dead find a way in.

Daisy can't fight back. What she has is what every kid knows: the best hiding places in the house, and which doors still close. Squeeze under a bed and hold your breath while something shuffles past, close enough to touch. Shut the door behind you, back away from it, and hope it holds.

This is not a story about soldiers, or survivors with a plan. It is about a girl who doesn't understand what is happening, only that the people outside are wrong somehow.
Daisy talks to herself to stay brave, the way kids do when they're scared and trying not to show it. Mom and Dad are out there somewhere. Should she stay put and wait for help, or get out while she still can?

Daisy's street is full of strangers now. Every one of them was somebody once: a neighbor who waved from the porch, a face she passed on the way to school. Some stand in the yards as if they forgot where they were going. Others kneel over the people who didn't make it. In the thin fog, with cars sitting where their owners left them, across the street has never looked so far away.

Sooner or later, hiding isn't enough. Daisy can't stay put and wait to be found. She has to get out. So Daisy runs. She knows every yard and fence around here better than anything chasing her.
Terminus: Day Zero is a story about one girl, one neighborhood, and the people who used to live there. Help Daisy get through the longest day of her life.
