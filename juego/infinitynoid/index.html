Infinitynoid

Juego de PC · Casual

Break an infinite field of bricks in every direction. Every ring is a new level, every level unlocks a new chiptune track, and the shop turns one lonely ball into a storm of exploding, block-shredding chaos. Then zoom out. Forever.

De qué trata

A brick breaker with no walls
Infinitynoid takes the classic brick breaker and removes the edges. Bricks stretch out in every direction around the starting point, arranged in colored rings. Clear a ring to open the next one, and zoom out as far as you like to see how far you've come.
Grow from one ball to a storm
Every broken block is a point. Spend them in the shop:

• Extra balls, faster balls and longer-lasting balls

• Balls that explode when their time runs out, and bigger blasts

• Penetration: shots that tear through several bricks at once

• The Block Destroyer, which zaps bricks on its own

• Or just click bricks and break them yourself

Prestige
Restart from level 1 with every upgrade kept, and every block is worth more. But each prestige seals a level forever. Ten prestige levels: how far can you push it?
30 original chiptune tracks
Every level you beat unlocks a new track: rock, synthwave, funk, drum & bass, shuffle, marches and more. The final level brings Zero Hour, a ten-minute finale anthem.
Infinity mode
Clear the game to unlock Infinity: no prestige, no level cap, no end.
