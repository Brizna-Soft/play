De qué trata

Shades of Fear
Shades of Fear is an episodic horror series. Each episode is one person's own account of something they experienced.

Episode I: North Loop
The first episode of Shades of Fear is the North Loop, a camping trip where a group of friends try to relive their college days in the wilderness.

Inspired by a true story.
Shades of Fear - North Loop is a first-person psychological horror game.
October 2004. You live out of your car and haven't seen your friends in a long time. They finally talk you into a weekend together, so you pick North Loop, the trail you all camped on as kids.
It was supposed to be a chance to catch up. Some things you bring with you. Some were already out there, waiting.

THE GAMEPLAY
This game is primarily a first-person narrative walking simulator with exploration, driving, camping, fishing and conversation mechanics.
Features
• Conversations with choices. Talk with your friends and the people you meet on the trail.

• Your phone. Texts come in from your group of friends.

• Camping and fishing. Set up camp, keep the fire going, and fish to your hearts content.

• Shot on tape. A VHS look, like somebody for "Horror Enthusiasts".
