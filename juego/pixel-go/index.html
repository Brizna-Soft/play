Pixel Go

Juego de PC · Estrategia · Estrategia

Enjoy Go at your own pace in a warm pixel world. Play a relaxed game in single-player mode, solve a life-and-death puzzle, guess the next move in a master's game, or take a quiet moment to review your own. Even if you're new to Go, you can explore one stone at a time.

Tráiler

De qué trata

A game of Go. A moment of calm.
Two colors, one board, endless possibilities. Go can be a relaxing pastime or a battle of wits. When to attack, when to give way, what to keep, and what to let go—every game brings something new to discover.
Pixel Go invites you to explore this timeless game without pressure. There's no rush to win or learn every rule before you begin. Make a cup of tea, place a stone, and enjoy its gentle tap on the board. Take your time. The next move can wait.

Learn Through Play
• Single-player Go & Assistance: Settle in for a relaxed game. When you're unsure, explore suggested moves, from quick instinctive picks to deeper search-based analysis. Turn on the territory overlay for a clearer view of how the board is taking shape.

• Life-and-Death Puzzles: Find the crucial move that saves a group of stones—or captures your opponent's. Enjoy a flash of insight, or take your time working through the possibilities.

• Guess the Next Move: Follow games played by masters and try to predict their next move. Compare your ideas with the moves they played, and discover the ingenuity behind each game.

Explore a Little Further
• Game Review: Revisit a game move by move. See how each move affected the score, explore AI suggestions, and follow the shifting balance of play. Find the turning points—and the good moves worth remembering.

• Puzzle Exploration: “What if I played here?” Try your own moves within a puzzle and use AI analysis to explore variations beyond the solution.

Your Time at the Board
• Warm pixel art creates a welcoming space for play and reflection.

• Includes the KataGo Go engine for offline play and analysis.

• Whether you're placing your first stone or have years of experience, play at your own pace.
