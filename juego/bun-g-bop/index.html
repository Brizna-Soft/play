Bun G Bop

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Casual · Acción, Aventura, Indie

Bun G Bop is a low-stakes high-chaos co-op platformer about flinging your friend with a bungee cord while tagging a big tower. We built this game to be fun for all duos, even ones who suck at platformers.

De qué trata

Bun G Bop is a local 2-player co-op platformer about bungee cord wielding buns climbing and tagging industrial sites. Duos of all skill levels can have a good time in this low-stakes casual game. Find clever workarounds with the physics based bungee cords, fling your bud off a cliff, and tag stuff.

Bungee Chaos
Screw over the devs by finding ways around problems they didn't think of using the dynamic bungee slingshotting. Screw over your friends by launching them to their doom as a silly epic gamer prank.

Criminal Crimes
It's not a crime if it's sick as hell. All those "no trespassing" signs are meant to hype you up, why else would there be so much concrete begging to be tagged? Get climbing!

Features:
• 2 player co-op

• 3 hours of gameplay for a non-completionist run

• 1000' of tower to throw your buddy from

• Dynamic physics based bungee cord mechanics

• Platforming for duos of any skill level

• Conveyor belts for launching your buddy

• 10 tag stencils to collect

• 10 hard as hell side areas to bypass to get those stencils

• Disgruntled rats

• Boppin music

• Ghosts

• BUNS
