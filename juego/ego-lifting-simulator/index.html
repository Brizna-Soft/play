Ego Lifting Simulator

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Deportes, Indie, Simulación

A physics-driven lifting career sim where you physically control every rep. Train hard, train smart, manage your work-life balance and diet, and become the next lifting champion.

De qué trata

Ego Lifting Simulator is a physics-driven weightlifting career sim.
KEY FEATURES
• Fully physics-driven lifting — your form actually matters

• Explore a small open-world island — move bricks for cash, eat with the locals, and compete against your rival

• Manage your work-life balance and, last but not least, your diet

• Your body visibly transforms as you progress

• Upgrade to a high-end gym

• Compete for the weightlifting championship

• Unlock new exercises
