Juego de PC · Aventura, Multijugador masivo, Rol · Aventura, Rol (RPG)

Trails of Starlight is a Japanese-style fantasy RPG with real-time combat. Explore a starlit continent with lost astral ruins and elemental Stellar Spirits. Travel with companions across kingdoms, snowfields and deserts, and uncover ancient truths beneath the starry sky.

De qué trata

Classic Fantasy · Heroic Epic
The shadow of mighty dragons, strife between kingdoms, and secrets of ancient civilizations all intertwine across this vast fantasy continent.
You set out as a free wanderer, fight shoulder to shoulder with trusted companions, confront formidable foes on your cross-kingdom adventure, and forge your very own heroic legend.

Stand With Me · Slay the Dragon
Follow the trail of the great dragons and face legendary foes head-on. Fight in unison with your companions, seize victory through skillful play, tactical wit and coordinated teamwork, and complete the glorious crusade worthy of an adventurer.
Gather your party — stand with me, and slay the dragon!
Journey Across Realms · Allies of All Races
Meet companions from diverse races, and step into kingdoms with distinct customs and cultures. Attend grand royal celebrations, listen to the pleas of local residents, and resolve the crises troubling each realm.
From first encounters with peoples of other races to fighting side by side as comrades, every arrival ushers in a brand new story.

Trials of Adventure · Heroic Growth
Hone your skills in battle and build your strength through adventure. Upgrade your class abilities, nurture your companions and star spirits, and challenge ever more formidable enemies.
From a greenhorn wanderer to a self-reliant hero, every battle and exploration stands as a testament to your growth.
Ensemble Storytelling · Bonds of Fellowship
Every companion boasts a unique background, personality and conviction. Through shared adventures and personal story arcs, you will learn of their pasts and aspirations, and forge deep, genuine bonds with them.
They are not merely fighting forces at your side — they are co-authors of this heroic saga.
