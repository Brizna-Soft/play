De qué trata

A confused creation awakens deep underground, exploring a strange and perilous world alone.
Light Devourer is a beat ’em up action role-playing game featuring intricate hand-drawn pixel art, fluid and satisfying combat, abundant collectibles, and a dark fantasy narrative.

Fast-Paced Combat
Unleash relentless attacks and dodge with quick dashes to avoid danger! Accumulate Dark Energy and unleash powerful Rage Shocks—combine your moves skillfully to defeat deadly foes!

Intricate Exploration
Travel through a beautifully interconnected world—journey through emerald crystal caverns, lush green forests, the grim thunder castle, and the void among the stars!

Rich Items
Equip powerful weapons and emblems! Collect a variety of weapons and ancient emblems scattered throughout the world to gain unique abilities.

Custom Builds
Absorb the light energy of fallen enemies to enhance your own attributes! Forge your own combat style based on your preferences.

Map Guidance
Enjoy breathtaking scenery and immersive music that bring an unforgettable atmosphere to the world.
Never lose your way during exploration! Once you discover landmarks, you can view the map at any time to track your position and locate unexplored regions.

For whatever reason you've come to this world—
defeat all your enemies, and devour all the light.
