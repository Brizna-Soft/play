Reverie

Juego de PC · Producción de audio, Utilidades, Producción de vídeo

Reverie is a music player and visualizer. Play your own songs or visualize anything playing on your PC, tweak the visuals with lots of controls and presets, and record the result as a small, smooth video with one key.

De qué trata

Your music, as light
Reverie is a music player with a visualizer you'll actually want to look at. It is the rework of Powelus's Audio Visualizer, rebuilt around a new renderer and a new interface. Open your music folder, hit play, and the visuals move with every beat. You can shape them however you like. If your music lives in Spotify or YouTube instead, switch to System audio and Reverie will visualize that too.

Light on your PC
The original drew every visualizer point as a light, which was too heavy even for powerful PCs. Reverie draws the whole thing in a single shader, so it's far lighter on your graphics card and stays smooth.

Play your own music, or anything else
• Your library: MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, AAC and M4A. Covers, artists and BPM are read automatically.

• System audio: Spotify, YouTube, games, streams. If your PC is playing it, Reverie can visualize it.

Make it look how you want
There are five display modes: Ring, Line Spectrum, Waveform, Particle Field and Concentric Rings. Ring mode can be a circle, rose, limaçon, star or lemniscate, or you can type in your own formula. Size, motion, smoothing, speed, glow, bloom, colours, gradients and the frequency balance are all adjustable with knobs, and scrolling over a knob gives you finer control. You can also use your own background image and set how bright it is.

Share your presets
Start from the Factory presets or make your own, and keep them organised in banks. A whole bank exports as one file, so you can send it to a friend or post it for others, and importing one takes a click.

Record videos
Press Ctrl+Shift+R to record the visualizer with sound. Encoding happens on your graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), so the frame rate stays smooth and the files stay small, about 6 MB per minute at 1080p60.

Requisitos

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10 64-bit

• Procesador: 4 cores, 2.5 GHz (Intel i5-4xxx / Ryzen 3)

• Memoria: 4 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: DirectX 11 GPU with 2 GB VRAM

• DirectX: Versión 11

• Almacenamiento: 500 MB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: Any Windows-compatible sound device

• Notas adicionales: System audio mode needs Windows 10 or later. Without a hardware video encoder, recording falls back to the CPU and is slower.

Recomendados

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10/11 64-bit

• Procesador: 6 cores (Intel i5-8xxx / Ryzen 5)

• Memoria: 8 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD RX 580 / Intel Arc or newer

• DirectX: Versión 11

• Almacenamiento: 1 GB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: Any Windows-compatible sound device

• Notas adicionales: Video recording uses the graphics card’s encoder (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF or Intel Quick Sync).
