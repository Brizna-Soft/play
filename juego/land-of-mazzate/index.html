Land Of Mazzate

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie, Rol (RPG)

Hold the arena with sword and shield! Land of Mazzate is a fast pixel-art hack and slash where endless waves of warriors, archers, mages and monsters pour in. Parry, dash, master 24 weapons, unlock 25 heroes and fight side by side in local or online co-op, or face your friends in PvP.

De qué trata

Grab your sword and shield and hold the line! LAND OF MAZZATE is a fast, brutal 2D pixel-art hack and slash. Wave after wave of warriors, archers, mages and monsters pour into the arena, and your only job is to survive and smash them all.HACK, BLOCK, DASH
Swing your weapon, raise your shield and dash out of danger. Time your block right as a hit lands for a PERFECT PARRY: arrows and magic orbs fly back at your enemies and whoever hit you is stunned. Watch your stamina, dodge the purple runes and the boss slam, and never stop moving.ENDLESS WAVES, EVER-GROWING POWER
Every wave is bigger and nastier than the last. Between waves you pick one of three powers: more damage, more health, faster dashes, elemental blades and much more. Your weapon levels up too, all the way to level 30, unlocking ranks that change how it fights: flying slashes, double attacks, pillars of light and more.25 HEROES, 24 WEAPONS
• Start as the Knight and unlock a whole roster: berserker, ranger, pyromancer, paladin, shadow, thief, bard, sorcerer, cleric, monk, necromancer, druid, lancer, samurai, alchemist, dragon hunter, engineer, frost barbarian, shaman, duelist, summoner... plus three secret heroes

• Every hero has a passive ability and a special skill

• Swords, hammers, flails, spears, bows, daggers, katanas, scythes, fire and lightning staffs, bombs, lutes, tomes and more

FIRE, ICE, LIGHTNING AND POISON
Elemental enemies burn, freeze, shock and poison you, and some explode when they die. Turn the tables with elemental upgrades and combine them for devastating effects. Block a hit and you block its element too.BOSSES AND RIVALS
Fight a different boss in every biome, up to the King of Mazzate himself.
