De qué trata

Barro 2020 is the sequel for Barro with 9 new challenging race tracks.

Game modes:
• Arcade: Do your best and finish the race as the winner.

• Time trial: You against yourself, finish the laps as fastest as you can.

• Multiplayer: Play the multiplayer (split screen) with a friend and have fun.

Game:
• 4 types of cars, choose the one that fits you better.

• 9 new race tracks

• Single player or up to 4 players local split screen

• New and improved time trial mode

• Up to 8 cars on the grid
