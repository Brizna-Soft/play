Ball Guys: Merge Party

Juego de PC · Casual, Acceso anticipado

Merge bouncing Ball Guys into massive, satisfying combos in this chaotic physics puzzler where every drop can make — or break — your run.

Tráiler

De qué trata

DROP. MERGE. SABOTAGE. REPEAT.
Ball Guys: Merge Party is a chaotic physics-based merging game where every drop can trigger a massive combo… or completely ruin your run.
Drop your Balls into the cup. Match two of the same kind to merge them into the next ball up. Build ridiculous combos, discover new balls and keep everything under control as your cup gets increasingly crowded.
Sounds easy?
It isn't.

MERGE YOUR WAY TO THE TOP
Every drop matters.
Plan your placement, use physics to your advantage and create huge combosas your balls bounce, roll and squash into each other.
Push your luck for a bigger score — but be careful. If a Ball Guy escapes the cup before checking out, your run is over.
Think you've mastered it?
Climb the leaderboards and prove it!

FIX YOUR MISTAKES… WITH A SNIPER
Made a terrible drop?
Take matters into your own hands.
Use special abilities like the Sniper to rescue a run, set up the perfect merge or deal with that one Ball Guy that absolutely refuses to cooperate.

MAKE MERGE YOURS
Customise the chaos with custom game modes.
Change the cup. Change the balls. Change the rules.
Share your creations & see who beats your challenges!
BUILT WITH THE BALL GUYS COMMUNITY
Ball Guys: Merge Party grew out of our popular Merge mini-game on iOS and Android, and we're expanding it into a full Steam and console game.
We're building it alongside our community, so download or wishlist the game, join us and help shape what Merge Party becomes.
TURN MERGING INTO A PARTY
[coming soon]
Challenge your friends in chaotic multiplayer modes built around the same simple rule:
Merge better, faster or more consistently than everyone else.
Race against the clock, sabotage your opponents and survive increasingly ridiculous challenges.

No Ball Guys are harmed during gameplay.
