PIERCING

Juego de PC · Acción, Aventura, Indie · Acción, Aventura, Indie

PIERCING is a rhythm-action game where you smash every panel to the beat. Fail, and the Contract forces you to give your childhood friend a piercing. A yuri dark fantasy.

Tráiler

De qué trata

【Estimated playtime: About 2 hours per playthrough】
The story will continue even if you fail the rhythm game, but your ending will change based on your performance.

【3–5+ hours total to see everything】
• 5 different endings

• Endless mode: chase high scores across randomly generated stages while the BPM shifts wildly

• Achievements and leaderboards to conquer

◆ Story
You play as Lin, a woman who works as a tailor in town.
When a monster suddenly destroys your hometown, you stand frozen in shock. Your childhood friend Asha takes your hand and helps you escape. With no food, you wander in search of a new home on what feels like an endless journey. Just as you near your limit, you meet an eerie Sage, who declares:

"Play this game for me, and I'll give you food and water whether you win or lose.
But if you fail, you'll have to wear this earring."
◆ Piercing Simulator
A sensual simulator that takes you through every step of the piercing process. There are 30 types of earrings.

◆ Rhythm Action
Bring a pointed tool down to the beat, smashing the panels in sequence in this violent rhythm game. 59 stages in total. Reach an ending to unlock endless mode.
The rhythm action offers 5 difficulty levels, so everyone from beginners to experts can enjoy it. Failing never means game over. The story keeps moving, and your performance determines which ending you reach.

◆ Endless mode unlocks after you clear the game
Chase high scores across randomly generated stages while the BPM shifts wildly. Build up enough combo to earn Continues and Combo Guards that help you push further.

◆ A Short Yuri Tale of Ruin
Journey with Asha, who loves you to death, and survive a doomed world together.
