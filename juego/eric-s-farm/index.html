Eric's Farm

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual, Indie · Acción, Indie

Feed 'em... or get BONKED! Toss ridiculous snacks, dodge hangry animals and smash through ten chaotic days in a slapstick 3D farm game. Take on bosses, five minigames and Endless Mode, then unlock The Back Forty: grow crops, drive tractors and build your own farm.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Eric's little farm is a finalist for Farm of the Year. The judge arrives in ten days. All Eric needs to do is fix the fence, tidy the yard and keep the animals fed. Unfortunately, the animals have other plans.
Chickens want spaghetti. Goats want traffic cones. Alpacas want disco balls. Grab their increasingly ridiculous orders and toss them a snack before their patience runs out. When they get HANGRY, dive out of the way of flying dropkicks, charging headbutts and incoming spit.

Every day is a new opportunity to land in the pond. Eric tumbles, bounces and crashes through fences, furniture and just about everything else on the farm. Earn coins, buy useful upgrades and questionable hats, then get up and try again.
• Survive ten story days and two oversized boss battles, then keep the chaos going in Endless Mode.

• Unlock five bonus minigames: Egg Catch, Goat Bowling, Spit Storm, Minivan Mayhem and the French horn rhythm game Le Talent Show.

• Discover 20 secrets, collect 17 hats and build your farm survival kit.

• Play with a controller or keyboard and mouse.

Then unlock The Back Forty
Finish Eric's ten-day story to unlock a whole farming simulator. Start with Ol' Rusty, your trusty orange tractor, and grow your farm: mow and bale hay, plant and harvest crops, haul your produce to town, and buy bigger machines and more land.
Keep the animals fed, recruit them as farmhands, and enter county fairs for blue ribbons, giant pumpkins and tractor pulls. Expect the occasional tornado, runaway animal or uninvited goat passenger. Simple automatic tool controls help you get started, with optional Pro Controls when you want more control.
From flying dropkicks to tractor mishaps, Eric's work is never done.
