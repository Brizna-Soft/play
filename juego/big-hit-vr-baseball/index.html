De qué trata

Big Hit VR Baseball is a pick-up-and-play mix of accurate baseball simulation and arcade-y action. Do you have what it takes to hit a big league fastball? Step up to the plate!Key Features

• Baseballs move using the real physics of baseball, including spin

• Face off against Fastballs, Sinkers, Curveballs, Changeups, Sliders, and Cutters

• Hone your swing in Batting Practice or go for the fences in Home Run Contest. More batting challenges coming soon.

• Fun for any age or ability: Increase the baseball size, slow it down, or juice it to hit massive home runs!
