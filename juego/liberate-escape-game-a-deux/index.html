Liberate : escape game à deux

Juego de PC · Aventura, Casual, Indie · Aventura, Indie

Two prisoners, two neighbouring cells, one wall between them. Each on their own device, no network: describe what you see, listen, guide your partner. 52 co-op puzzles played by voice, in an abandoned laboratory.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Two cells. One wall.
Two people wake up in neighbouring cells of an abandoned laboratory. They cannot see each other. They can only hear each other. And neither will get out without the other.

Liberate is a co-op escape game for two players, each on their own device. No network, no connection between your devices: everything goes through your voice. Play side by side, or remotely on a voice call.A game you play by talking
• Each player only sees their own cell, objects and documents.

• The answer is always on the other side of the wall: describe what you see, listen, guide your partner.

• Read a game code out to your partner, and you start together.

• The solutions change with every game.

A story in three chapters
Iris and Leo, a sister and her brother, are subjects of the TANDEM programme, an experiment run for decades deep inside a bunker. Who is watching them? What does the Voice in the loudspeakers want? And why does this place feel so familiar?
• 52 puzzles, about 3 hours for a duo new to the genre.

• Progressive hints when you are stuck.

• Several endings: at the end of the tunnel, each of you chooses alone, in secret, whether to wait for the other.

One purchase for two
A free demo gives access to chapter 1. The full game has all three chapters, and only one of you needs it: the buyer invites their partner into the game with a few digits read out loud (the free demo accepts the invitation).

• Two devices: one per player. Your partner can play on a different kind of device (computer, Steam Deck, phone or tablet).

• Controller and keyboard-mouse, touch on devices that have it.

• No account, no ads, no data collection.

• In English and French.

• Unsettling atmosphere, no gore.
