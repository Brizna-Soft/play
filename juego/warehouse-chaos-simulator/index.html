Warehouse Chaos Simulator📦

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

Warehouse Chaos Simulator is a satisfying warehouse organization simulation game you can play solo or in online co-op with up to 4 friends. Sort identical products into boxes, organize shelves by category, and unlock skills to optimize your workflow.

Dónde comprarlo

Tráiler

De qué trata

The night shift starts in chaos.
Thousands of products are scattered across the warehouse floor. Every one of them belongs somewhere — and putting it right is your job.
Pick things up, fill your boxes, and bring the warehouse back into order.

Grab. Box. Shelve.
Take an empty box and start collecting. The first product you drop in decides what the box is for.
Fill it, carry it, and take it to the right shelf. Put it where it belongs and it clicks into place. Put it somewhere else and it lights up red.

Push the cart.
You don't have to carry every box on your back. Grab the cart parked in the warehouse, load your boxes onto it, and push it down the aisle.
It holds four boxes to start — six with the Cart Rack skill. Half the walking, twice the sorting.

Three warehouses. Your call.
Every shift starts by choosing where you work:
Small Warehouse — 480 products. Clear it end to end and finish at 100%.
Medium Warehouse — 2,000 products. Four times the work, the same satisfying finish.
Mega Warehouse — 4,000 products. Fifty shelves full. The real scale of the mess.
Each warehouse keeps its own save. Leave one half-done, switch to another, come back exactly where you left off. Finish one and your best time sits on top of it.

Ten aisles. One simple rule.
Learn the layout and sort every product into the aisle it belongs to:
• Produce

• Dairy

• Butcher

• Frozen

• Pasta & Pantry

• Snacks

• Drinks

• Cleaning

• Personal Care

• Books

No hidden rules. Whatever the shelf sign says, that's where it goes.

Over 4,000 physical products.
Every product has its own model and its own physics. Drop it, throw it, stack it — or accidentally make a bigger mess than you started with.

Build your career.
Every product you shelve earns XP.
