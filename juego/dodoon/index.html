DoDoon

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual, Indie · Acción, Indie

Master a unique cannon trajectory and blast away incoming enemies! Defeat enemies, upgrade your castle, and create your own build as you fight through all 30 Waves.

De qué trata

Fire Your Cannon and Defend Your Castle!
DoDoon is a roguelite defense shooter where you control a cannon with a unique trajectory to defend your castle from waves of enemies.
Shoot enemies, collect coins, and upgrade your castle and cannon.
Combine powerful allies and skills to create your own unique way of fighting.
Each Wave lasts around 30 seconds.
Fight your way through all 30 Waves and see how far you can go!

Your Shots Don't Go Where You Click!
Your cannonballs don't simply fly straight to where you click.
Learn the cannon's unique parabolic trajectory, read enemy movements, and lead your shots to hit your targets!
Your shots may not land where you expect at first. But as you learn the trajectory, your aim will steadily improve.
Once you can consistently land your shots and take down enemies one after another, the game becomes incredibly satisfying!

Shoot, Defeat, and Power Up!
Defeating enemies earns you coins.
Use your coins between Waves to upgrade your cannon and castle.
Increase your attack power?
Raise your critical hit rate?
Carry more ammunition?
Reload faster?
Your choices shape how you fight during each run.

Fight Alongside Your Allies!
Your cannon isn't the only thing defending the castle.
Knights, Wizards, Crossbows, Catapults, Mystic Light, and more...
Various allies and defenses automatically attack enemies for you.
Combine your cannon fire with automatic attacks and fend off wave after wave of enemies!

Change the Way You Fight with Skills!
Collect Diamonds by playing and use them to unlock a variety of permanent skills.
Turn your cannonballs into explosive shots.
Make your shots bounce.
Turn your Knight into a Heavy Knight.
Increase the amount of coins you earn.
