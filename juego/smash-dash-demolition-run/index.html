About the Game
Smash Dash is a single-player PvE shooter with a 3D top-down view.
Fight your way through, smash buildings for loot, and make it out alive. If you successfully extract, you keep the items you found.

Core Loop
• Deploy → Explore/Fight → Smash & Loot → Manage Inventory → Extract
• Currency is always kept.
• If you extract successfully, you keep the items earned in that run and use them for future attempts.

Features
• Top-Down Tactical Shooting — sightlines, positioning, cover, and routing decide survival.

• Destruction & Looting — smash buildings to uncover loot, consumables, and currency.

• Inventory Management — decide what to take with limited space.

• Trading/Selling — sell loot to secure currency and strengthen your character through upgrades.

• Single-Player PvE — focused combat against AI enemies.

Controls
Keyboard and mouse recommended.

Additional Notes
• The game may receive updates and some content may change.
