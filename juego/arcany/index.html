De qué trata

A Persistent Online World with a Cozy Pixel Art Style

Explore the world of Gravenmoor together with other players in a shared online adventure. Immerse yourself in a warm and atmospheric pixel art world filled with magic and mystery.
Forge your own adventure: train a variety of skills, from combat and crafting to fishing, cooking, and enchanting with more skills coming in future updates.
Quests reveal hidden secrets, unlock new paths, and help you uncover the mysteries of Gravenmoor.

A World Full of Secrets

Following a magical experiment gone wrong, a new dimension called the Arcane was created — a mysterious realm where time and oxygen do not follow the same rules as the normal world. Only prepared adventurers can venture inside, as some missions can only be completed within the Arcane, and staying too long can be dangerous. Explore, progress, and master your skills to uncover all the secrets of Gravenmoor.

Build Your Own Land & Collect Skins

Claim your own land in Gravenmoor and shape it however you like. Use hundreds of decorations and structures to customize your territory and make it truly unique. Throughout your adventures, collect souls scattered across the world to unlock unique powers. A wide variety of clothing is available in Gravenmoor: you can craft them, buy them from other players, or earn them by completing challenging missions. Express your style, show your personality, and leave your mark, both on your land and throughout the world of Gravenmoor.

Compete in PvP

Enter dangerous PvP zones or friendly duels to test your strength, strategy, and skill. Outplay rivals, earn glory, and leave your legacy in the online world of Gravenmoor.
