PegLotto!

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Estrategia, Indie, Simulación

PegLotto! is a roguelike that combines slots, pachinko, and deck-building. Pick your items, place them on the board, and pray that luck is on your side! Sit back, enjoy the view from the train window, and ride the waves of fortune!

De qué trata

Slots x Pachinko x Deck-Building x Roguelike !!!
PegLotto! is a roguelike where you use items to manipulate the "probability" of slots and the "physics" of pachinko, racking up scores through explosive synergies.

Story
You ride a train powered by the score earned whenever pachinko balls collide with pegs, pushing ever onward.
A slot machine is built into the top of the pachinko board, and its results determine how many pachinko balls are released. Choose and place items, clear the quotas set before you, and head for the goal along a line with no turning back.
How to Play
Controls are simple.
• Choose an Item – Pick one item from three choices.

• Place the Item – Set your items between the pegs on the board.

• Spin the Slots – Once your items are ready, just spin! And pray it all works out!

Key Features
• Discover Devastating Combos Among 150+ Items

Items come with all kinds of effects: some raise the odds of specific slot symbols, some spawn a flood of items when they break, others create special balls, and much more.
Find powerful combos among more than 150 items and craft a build that's all your own.

• Chill x Dopamine: Just Feel Good

When pachinko balls hit pegs and rack up your score, a soothing rhythm rings out. As the train moves on, the scenery outside the window gradually changes.
Savor the rush of watching your score skyrocket from masses of pachinko balls and item synergies, all against a backdrop of chill scenery.
