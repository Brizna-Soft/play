De qué trata

Step into the eerie wilderness in Raccoon Hunter, a chilling horror-hunting experience about a man and his mysterious pet lurking beneath the well. Armed with a double-barreled shotgun and a trusty axe, you must hunt raccoons to keep your pet fed or you might be next.

In Raccoon Hunter you can..
• Hunt raccoons in the woods to feed your pet.

• Collect varying amounts of raccoon meat each day based on your pet’s hunger.

• Venture into the wild, but beware of wolves and other threats that roam the forest.

• Discover the story behind the creature in the well and your twisted connection to it.

A Short Horror Experience
Raccoon Hunter is designed to be played in a single sitting, with an estimated playtime of 30–45 minutes. Please note that the game does not feature a save system.
