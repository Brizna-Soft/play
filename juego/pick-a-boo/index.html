PICK A BOO

Juego de PC · Acción · Acción

PICK A BOO is an online co-op roguelike escape action game for up to 6 players. Explore ever-changing stages and haul furniture and cargo with magnetic power. Upgrade your character, evade enemies, or fight back. How greedy will you get? When will you escape? It's up to you!

Tráiler

De qué trata

Work together with up to six players as members of the resistance group “YONIGE” in this rogue-like action game. Slip past the management system’s surveillance and pursuers, and secretly recover furniture and keepsakes filled with the precious memories of dolls who have awakened to a sense of self.
A quiet escape operation begins—to protect the “hearts” and “places to belong” that have begun to grow within these once-lifeless dolls.
Up to 6 Players, One Chaotic Moving Job!

Features
➰🔴 Online co-op for 1–6 players
➰🟠 Fast-paced action as you haul multiple items at once
➰🟡 Varied maps that change with every run
➰🟢 Level up by hauling more cargo
➰🔵 Mix and match upgrade parts freely
➰🟣 A wide variety of skins to customize your doll
➰🟤 In-game voice chat
➰⚪ Turn into a pinball and blast enemies away!
Move It All Together

Use magnetic power to gather and haul precious furniture and keepsakes all at once.
Level up by moving cargo to strengthen your magnetic power, allowing you to carry heavier items and gather even more at once.
Survive the Dangers Ahead!

All kinds of enemies and dangerous gimmicks stand in your way.
Whether you help a teammate in trouble is up to you!? Just grab the cargo and make your escape!
Go Wild as a Pinball!

Take aim and launch yourself straight into the action!
Turn yourself into a pinball and blast your enemies—and sometimes even your teammates—away.
Power Up Even More with Upgrade Parts

Collect upgrade parts from mission rewards and the shop, then mix and match them however you like.
Shoot flames, take to the air, or blast enemies away—it’s up to you. Take on each getaway in your own style.
No Two Getaways Are Ever the Same

The map changes randomly with every run, bringing different routes and dangers each time.
