Winky!: Find the Golden Ticket

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Estrategia, Indie, Simulación

1 Billion chocolate bars! One golden ticket is hidden in a mountain of Winky chocolate bars. Open them, rewrap the chocolate as your own knock-off brand, Wonko!, and sell it. Hire staff, build production lines and grow a factory big enough to find the ticket, alone or with up to three friends.

De qué trata

1 Billion chocolate bars! Somewhere in a mountain of Winky chocolate bars there is one golden ticket, and whoever finds it wins the Winky chocolate factory down the road.

Until then, every bar without a ticket is still chocolate. Tear it open, peel off the Winky paper, wrap the chocolate in your own brand (Wonko!), box it six to a box, and sell it. Then do it again. Then pay somebody else to do it. Then build a machine that does it a thousand times faster.

Winky! is a first-person factory builder that starts with one kid at one table and ends in a roaring factory, alone or with up to three friends.

Open it by hand. At your work table every Winky is opened the long way: grab, tear, peel, unfold the foil, check for the ticket, wrap the chocolate as a Wonko!, pack the box. Hand the full box to a courier, and they come back with your money.

Hire a crew. Workers at benches open bars for you, runners keep their trays full, couriers carry the boxes out, and drivers take the diggers and forklifts. Run them all from your tablet: stop them, send them outside, put them back to work.

Build the line. Knock through the walls and lay out a real production line, wherever you want it: hopper, cutter, ticket scanner, separator and shredder, Wonko! wrapper, packer and palletiser, joined by belts, splitters and mergers. Place it, turn it, copy a whole line and paste it next to the first.

Feed it and ship it. Diggers scoop the Winky mountain into the hoppers, forklifts carry full pallets to the trucks at the docks, and every truck that comes back pays for the whole load.

Find the ticket. There is one ticket in the whole heap. When it turns up, everything stops.

Every factory needs a break.

Requisitos

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10 64-bit

• Procesador: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600

• Memoria: 8 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: DirectX 12 GPU with WebGPU support and 2 GB VRAM (GTX 1050 Ti / RX 560 or better)

• DirectX: Versión 12

• Almacenamiento: 1 GB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: Any DirectX-compatible

• Notas adicionales: ESTIMATE - not verified on hardware. Large factories with many staff are CPU-heavy; use the Low/Medium quality tier on older processors.
