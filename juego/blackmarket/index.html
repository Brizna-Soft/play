BLACKMARKET

Juego de PC · Acción, Indie, Free to Play · Acción, Indie

BLACKMARKET is a first-person roguelite room runner. Race the clock from saferoom to saferoom. Learn the one rule each creature lives by, and master slides, wall-kicks and pounds to stay alive.

De qué trata

WAKE UP, RUNNER
BLACKMARKET is a first-person roguelite room runner. You are a runner in EDEN, a buried facility of numbered doors, sodium light and poured concrete, floor stacked on floor. They tell you there is a way topside. Run.
THE CORE LOOP
Go through numbered rooms, one after another, against a timer. Reach the next Saferoom before it hits zero: drop through the hatch and you're safe for a moment. Then the next rooms open, and the timer is shorter.
If the timer runs out, something comes down the halls for you.
MOVEMENT IS THE GAME
• Slide: carry speed under shutters, down stairs and through low gaps

• Wall-kick: kick off walls mid-air and turn a fall into forward speed

• Pound: slam down from height and bank the impact into momentum

• Vault and climb: mantle crates, ledges and catwalk rails without breaking stride

• Swim: wade, dash and pull yourself out of flooded halls

THE APOSTLE
Every Saferoom has a screen, and on it is the Apostle. The Apostle deals: changes to how you move, gear, keys, favours. Some cost Scrip, some cost seconds off your clock, some cost worse.
• Spring Heels: jump higher

• Spare Grip: one more wall-kick before you land

• Greased Soles: slides carry further

• Stair Diver: downhill slides pick up speed

No two runs build the same runner.
EARLY ACCESS
BLACKMARKET is free, made by a small studio in Osaka and built in the open. The full loop is here now. New sectors, entities and deals come through Early Access, shaped by how you play.

Inspired by Roblox games "DOORS" by LSPLASH and "GRACE" by The Fartering Few, "Pizza Tower" by Tour De Pizza, "The Binding of Isaac" by Edmund McMillen, "Inscryption" by Daniel Mullins, "FAITH" by Airdorf Games, Western esotericism, Kabbalah and the Holy Bible.
