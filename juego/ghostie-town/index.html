De qué trata

Welcome to Ghostie Town, a cozy game about restoring a forgotten town. You’re a small ghost on a big mission: fix fences, water plants, sweep streets, and help ghostie residents settle in as the town comes back to life. A heartwarming, story-rich, repair game.
Make Ghostie Friends

New ghosties can be found around every corner, waiting to tell you their stories. Get lost in the deep lore and history weaved into every character, signpost, and building.
Nourish The Town

The town needs your help! You'll need to be resourceful to mend the structures and water the plants around town, but with the right tools, you'll start to feel the magic found in every little act of restoration.
Build Your Identity

The journey won't be easy, but at least with a fancy new hat, you can bring back the town in style. With your favourite tool in-hand and hat on your head, you can explore the cute, hand-crafted town in any direction you like and enjoy plenty of side-quests, mini-games, and collectables.
