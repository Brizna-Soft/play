Traveloot

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie

Traveloot is a run-based incremental game where you guide a growing team of adventurers across islands. Gather resources, fight enemies, unlock branching skill tree, and find new party members to join your team. Play solo or with a friend in local co-op.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Traveloot is a run-based incremental game focused on exploration, team building, and upgrades. Embark on a journey solo or with a friend across distinct islands.
Journey
Travel across four distinct islands, each with its own biome, enemies, and resources. Guide your team with the cursor, attacking enemies and collecting resources as they travel automatically.

Upgrade
Progress through a branching upgrade tree that shapes how you play. Unlock new abilities, enhance your team members, and upgrade your skills.

Team
During your journey, you’ll encounter adventurers who can join your team. Each one brings unique abilities, and you’ll have to choose who to bring on your next run.

Solo or Co-op
Play solo or with a friend in local couch co-op. Work together to gather resources, fight enemies, and progress through upgrades as a team.

Features
• 4 unique islands to explore

• 8 recruitable adventurers

• Branching upgrade tree

• Run-based incremental progression

• Single-player and couch co-op

• Controller and mouse support
