Flying Island Miner

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

De qué trata

Rise above the clouds and leave your everyday worries behind. Island Miner is a relaxing digging simulator set on a floating island filled with treasures and curious finds.

Settle into the soothing rhythm of digging. Peel back layer after layer, watch the sand tumble away, and see the outline of a buried object emerge. What could it be? A familiar find, or that one piece missing from your collection?

Unleash the full potential of your tools

Turn your first earnings into some serious equipment. Unlock new tools and work your way through the upgrade tree to dig faster and more efficiently. Put each new purchase to the test and feel just how far you've come since that very first swing.

Unearth every last find

A full bag doesn't mean a complete collection. Sell your loot, build your collection of curiosities, and keep searching for the missing pieces. Enjoy the satisfaction of finally unearthing something you've been hoping to find.

From your very first dig to the last grain of sand.

Requisitos

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10 64-bit

• Procesador: Dual-core 2.5 GHz with Hyper-Threading (Intel Core i5-7200U or equivalent)

• Memoria: 4 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: OpenGL 3.3, Intel HD Graphics 620 or equivalent (clouds off)

• Almacenamiento: 400 MB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: any

• Notas adicionales: Mouse required. On integrated graphics turn off clouds in Settings.

Recomendados

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO: Windows 10/11 64-bit

• Procesador: Quad-core 3.0 GHz (Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 1600)

• Memoria: 8 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: OpenGL 3.3, 2 GB VRAM (NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti / AMD Radeon RX570)

• Almacenamiento: 400 MB de espacio disponible

• Tarjeta de sonido: any

• Notas adicionales: Mouse required.
