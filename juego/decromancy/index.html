Decromancy

Juego de PC · Indie, Rol, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie, Rol (RPG), Terror

In this morbidly funny dark fantasy rogue-lite card battler: Use necromancy to summon and fight zombie animal cards, plan your route to prepare for the final encounter, use essence gained from attempts to trade for collectable cards, flavorful foods, legendary items, and starting decks.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Decromancy; an 8 bit 2.5D game about seeking the fountain of youth,
fighting encounters with your deck of zombeast cards filled
with the essences of previously living creatures along the way.

The over arching goal is to survive a series of encounters with opponents and their own unique decks.

In between combats, make decisions about if you would rather in the moment;
Have more maximum health, mana, or inventory space, legendary items
with game changing effects, food that can heal and other effects, or more cards.

Your survival depends on the choices you make.

Juggle persistent health and poison levels, inventory space,
all while upgrading and infusing cards in your deck with abilities from other permanently sacrificed cards.
If you feel an ache from your travels, be sure to visit the Peddler,
a merchant druid who will sell you items to add to your collection
in exchange for essence that you have gained along the way.

Also Features:
Procedurally Generated Maps and Encounters
120 Collectable Cards and Items
9 Hidden Steam Achievements
