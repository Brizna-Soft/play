Liftoff® - where pilots are made!
Step into the thrilling world of FPV drone flight with Liftoff®: FPV Drone Racing, the ultimate simulator for both seasoned pilots and newcomers. It offers realistic and fully simulated physics and advanced flight dynamics for an authentic single and multiplayer experience. Customize your drones and create and share unique tracks and drones while enjoying seamless integration with transmitters. Driven by community feedback, Liftoff® is continuously evolving.
