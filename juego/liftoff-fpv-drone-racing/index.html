De qué trata

Liftoff® - where pilots are made!
Step into the thrilling world of FPV drone flight with Liftoff®: FPV Drone Racing, the ultimate simulator for both seasoned pilots and newcomers. It offers realistic and fully simulated physics and advanced flight dynamics for an authentic single and multiplayer experience. Customize your drones and create and share unique tracks and drones while enjoying seamless integration with transmitters. Driven by community feedback, Liftoff® is continuously evolving. Get ready for an exhilarating FPV adventure!

Mínimos

• Requiere un procesador y un sistema operativo de 64 bits

• SO *: Windows 7

• Procesador: Intel 8th gen / AMD Ryzen 1st gen - 2.4GHz or faster.

• Memoria: 8 GB de RAM

• Gráficos: Geforce RTX 2060 / AMD RX Vega 56 - 4GB VRAM or more.

• DirectX: Versión 11

• Red: Conexión de banda ancha a Internet
