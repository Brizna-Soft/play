De qué trata

DYNAMIC is a physics-driven third-person action platformer where precision hand-ball mechanics meet challenging obstacle courses and arcade-style gameplay.
Journey through 300+ single player handcrafted levels, each designed to test your timing, accuracy, movement, and creativity with emphasis on flow-state.
You can also challenge your friends online with 12 multiplayer game modes, competing and collaborating with friends in a variety of fast-paced arcade experiences.
Packed with content, DYNAMIC embraces experimentation, skill, and persistence.
