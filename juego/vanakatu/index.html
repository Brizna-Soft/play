De qué trata

DISCLAIMER: I completed this video game for my capstone project in university - it's my first video game project ever so it's pretty buggy, but I'm still proud enough of the work I put into it to showcase it publicly. To make it fair for everyone, I decided to make the game free to play, but if you would like to support me, you can find my Instagram here - @pixie.eats.people, where you can DM me/ donate to me!

This is an open-world story about VAN CAT (that's his name, no affiliations with vehicles), a duduk player from a regular family, who tracks down his CULT-LEADING UNCLE to talk him out of creating a WORLDWIDE DICTATORSHIP!!!

This Game Includes:
• Unique visuals by the developer herself

• Beautiful music

• Guitar Hero inspired battles

• Puzzle solving - using pattern recognition to unlock doors and find hidden keys

• A duduk!
