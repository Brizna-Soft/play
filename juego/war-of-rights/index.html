De qué trata

War of Rights is a multiplayer game set during the perilous days of the American Civil War, in the Maryland Campaign of September, 1862. Campfire Games is devoted to presenting the gruesome and glorious elements of the period, while maintaining the highest level of historical accuracy and realism as is possible with the wonders of CRYENGINE.

⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀⠀
- Massive battles: Take part in engagements consisting of 400 players.

- Tailored for line formation teamplay: Custom formation buff mechanic system rewards sticking together by lessening the suppression effects, the morale cost for the team when dying and the flag bearer respawn rate.

- Multiple army branches: Fall in on the line as an infantryman, saddle up with the cavalry to screen the army’s advance or shatter the fighting spirit of the enemy as an artillery crewman!

- Historical accuracy: Period correct uniforms, weapons, manual of arms animations, battlefields, objectives & drill camps.

- Regimental pride: 135 researched and recreated regiments & batteries all supporting uniforms for all ranks (Private to Colonel) and player classes.
⠀⠀
- Immersive communication: In-game 3D voice system.

- Recreated battlefields: 47 historically accurate skirmish areas of the Battles of Antietam and South Mountain as well as the Siege of Harpers Ferry to play on and 3 Drill Camps to perfect your formation forming abilities or simply exchange battle stories at the campfire.
- Unique Game modes: Skirmishes, Picket Patrol, Conquest & Contention have all been built from the ground up to support teamwork on a massive scale.
- Player classes: Private, NCO, Flag Bearer, Drummer, Bugler, Fifer & Officer playable from every regiment & battery featured and covering all ranks from Private to Colonel.
