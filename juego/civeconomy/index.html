CivEconomy

Juego de PC · Indie, Simuladores, Estrategia · Estrategia, Indie, Simulación

A Stone Age strategy game on a 3D world of hexes. Every person is simulated, every calorie counted. Neighbours live by the same rules you do. Build your village, teach your people, fight raids yourself. There is no victory: there is the story of your people.

Tráiler

De qué trata

CivEconomy is an early civilization sim about a people at the edge of history. You are its leader. You decide where the hands go, what the people learn, what is built, where your families settle and when to fight. They live with the result.
People with names, ages and families. Food counted in calories, sickness that comes with the seasons, tools that take real work and real knowledge to make. Nothing is invented for free, and every number answers "why?".
A world you walk, claim and build on
• A living 3D world. Generated maps from Small to Huge, with rivers, forests, game and deposits. Fog of war: you only know what your people have seen.

• Seasons that matter. Rivers rise in spring and freeze in a hard winter. Nobody explores in deep winter. Nobody marches with the harvest still standing.

• The village is real work. Choose where the hearth stands, then enter the village and build it: huts, stores, workshops, a palisade, a gate, a watchtower. Every work needs materials and hands, and the woods come down tree by tree.

From one village to a realm
When the land around your village can no longer feed your people, send young families to found a village of your own blood. It has its own fields, crafts and chief, and it heeds you as long as someone keeps walking the road between you.
• The road is the bond. Send an envoy to live there, help in a hungry season, ask a tribute from what it can spare. A village that hears nothing from you forgets you, and faster the farther away it is. You are always told before it goes its own way.

• Your word travels. A loyal village with your envoy in it passes your word on to the villages beyond it. The map draws the road of your influence to each one.
