De qué trata

Command your dice to glorious victory in this strategic rogue-lite. Cast devastating spells, recruit powerful dice, and prove yourself worthy to challenge the great Commander Talis.

TACTICAL COMBAT
Execute satisfying strategies using a variety of dice, spells, and passives to prove your worth as a master tactician.

EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT
With over a hundred dice and twenty five spells, there are limitless combinations to experiment and craft. Unlock new combos to humiliate enemy commanders.

GROW STRONGER
Level your dice up to unlock new abilities and opportunities.

CREATE YOUR OWN DICE
Leverage the all powerful soul forge construct custom dice mid-run.

PROVE YOUR WORTH
Show Commander Talis you are his worthy heir with devastating magic and synergistic passive items.
