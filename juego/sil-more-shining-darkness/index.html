De qué trata

You're a great hero but who are you?
Sil-Morë is a tactical turn-based rogue-like role-playing game. You're one of the greatest heroes of the First Age and your task is to descend into Angband, the citadel of the Greatest Enemy, and bring back the Great Jewels from the Enemy's crown. Each run gets progressively harder as the Enemy bestows more and more curses upon you. Choose heroes, play to their strengths and employ clever strategies to deal with the ever-dwindling power of your characters as your true identity is gradually revealed over the course of the game.

Features
Descendant of the Angband family of games. Play an heir of a genre-defining old-school classic, created for the modern day.
Heroes of the First Age with distinct identities. Each hero is carefully thought out with lore in mind — and the game mechanics are made to follow, not the other way round. Sheer strength, weapon mastery, skillful smithing, or singing powerful songs — each hero has their own proficiency.
Tactical turn-based roguelike gameplay. Wipe whole levels of monsters or never kill a single orc — it's a choice which depends on your personal play style and on whatever your particular hero is good at. Devise clever tactics to overcome your enemies — or to slip past them, unnoticed.

Reverse rogue-lite progression built around getting weakened. Contrary to most rogue-likes, each successful run you get weaker, not stronger, as the Enemy gets angrier and curses you even harder. Plan your gameplay around it and choose heroes wisely as each hero can be played only once.
Curses, oaths, blessings, and storytelling. But if it becomes too hard, the Gods are to help. Each death contributes to a pool from which you can get blessed and helped with the next runs.
