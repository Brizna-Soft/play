Dr Snipes Hockey VR

Juego de PC · Deportes · Deportes

Take the ice in first-person VR hockey. Handle the puck, pass, defend, and shoot with two-handed controls in DSHL matches. Play goalie, sharpen your skills, and chase wild Trick Shots and arcade challenges. Ringette is included as an extra mode.

De qué trata

Take the ice in Dr Snipes Hockey VR, a single-player hockey game built around puck control, passing, defending, shooting, and saves from ice level.
PLAY THE DSHL
Face computer-controlled teams in exhibition games, seasons, and cups. Take shifts as a skater, set up one-timers and tips, or step into the crease to play goalie. Indoor and outdoor rinks give each game a different setting.
PRACTICE YOUR SKILLS
Learn two-handed shot controls in the guided tutorial, then work through shooting, passing, skating, defense, and goalie activities.
HIT THE ARCADE
Try bank shots across the expanded Trick Shot tour, break targets in China Shop, survive waves of rink monsters, race through Blizzard Dash, and play Table Hockey and other challenges. Playful pickups and the occasional goat add surprises on the ice.
MORE WAYS TO PLAY
Ringette offers its own matches and commentary as an additional mode. Earn XP, collect rewards, and customize your look as you play.
