InCowMental

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie · Indie

InCowMental is a cute and colorful tiny incremental game where you collect milk from your cows to sell it and make cheese. Upgrade your farm, and between upgrades, play mini-games to boost your production and sales!

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

Deep in the French countryside sits your farm. You've got just 3 cows and one wildly unreasonable ambition: to feed the whole planet with your milk and cheese.

Hover over your cows to milk them by hand. Call in a truck to sell your milk for your first "CowCoins". Order some wheat to feed your growing herd.

Grow your own wheat, hire farmhands to do the milking for you, bring in harvesters, unlock new grain varieties and new cow breeds. Open your cheese dairy, then your marketing department. Each step brings a new screen, a new resource, a new way to keep the machine running.

Everything you used to do by hand eventually runs on its own so you can focus on what's next.

Mini-games are there to boost your production:
• a rhythm game to find the musical style that gets your cows moo-ving

• a molecule-mixing game to engineer wheat that slaps

• a minesweeper in the aging cellar to save your wheels from rotting

• a flappy bird where a cow steers the milk market

• ...and many more to discover

We're Alagaëzia (artist) and Skorvaltz (developer), and we work together on every aspect of the game, from art to development, including music and sound design.

InCowMental is our very first game, but we're pouring our whole hearts into it. We truly hope you'll have as much fun playing it as we had making it!
