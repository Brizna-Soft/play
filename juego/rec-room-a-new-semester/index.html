De qué trata

Party up with your friends and explore a world of endless fun. Hang out in official rooms, discover community-created rooms, or create unforgettable memories with players from around the world.
Whether you're looking to socialize, play games, or build something entirely new, Rec Room: A New Semester gives you the freedom to choose your own adventure.

Play thousands of unique experiences created by the community.
From quests and story adventures to PvP arenas, horror maps, obstacle courses, and casual hangout spaces, there's always something new to discover.
Don't see the experience you're looking for? Build it yourself using the Creator Pen and share it with the community.

Design your own rooms with intuitive building tools.
Create social hangouts, games, roleplay worlds, events, puzzles, and more, all without needing years of development experience.
Your imagination is the limit.

Everything is better together.

Jump into your favorite experiences with friends or meet new people along the way.

With new community creations appearing every day and ongoing development throughout Early Access, there's always something new waiting to explore.
