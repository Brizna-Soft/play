Yeat

Juego de PC · Casual, Free to Play

Yeat is a minimalist clicker game where every click counts. Choose between TONKA and YEAT, build up your individual and combined click totals, and watch your numbers grow. Your progress is saved locally, so you can continue clicking whenever you return.

De qué trata

About This Game
Yeat is a simple, minimalist clicker game focused on building up your numbers, one click at a time.
Choose between two buttons, TONKA and YEAT. Each button has its own click counter, while a combined total tracks every click you've made.
There are no complicated controls or lengthy tutorials. Just launch the game, choose a button, and start clicking.
How to Play
Click the TONKA button to increase your TONKA counter, or click the YEAT button to increase your YEAT counter.
Switch between the two whenever you want. Every click also increases your overall total, allowing you to track your combined progress.
Your click counts are saved locally, so you can close the game and continue building your totals when you return.
Features
• Two clickable buttons: TONKA and YEAT.

• Individual counters: Track the number of clicks for each button.

• Total click counter: Monitor your combined clicks.

• Click animations: Visual feedback when pressing the buttons.

• Saved progress: Your click totals are stored locally between sessions.

• Simple controls: Play entirely through straightforward mouse clicks.

• Open-ended gameplay: Play for a few minutes or keep clicking to reach higher totals.

The Goal
There's no complicated objective. Challenge yourself to increase your click totals, balance your TONKA and YEAT counters, or simply see how high your numbers can go.
Click. Count. Repeat.
