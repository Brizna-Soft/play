Sloppy Forgeries

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual · Acción

Sloppy Forgeries is a fast-paced competitive painting game. Practice your skills by butchering iconic masterpieces in one of the few drawing games that measures accuracy pixel-by-pixel in real-time.

De qué trata

◇─◇── Free demo available! ──◇─◇

Copy the Mona Lisa! With a mouse! In three minutes! "That's impossible," you say?! Yes.
But wait! There's more! You get:
🎨 4-6 colors! 🙂
🖌️ 3 brush sizes! Medium, big, and too big! 😐
🚫 No undo! 😡
🚫 No fill! 🤬
🎯 A score based on every pixel you paint! 💯 😂
◇─◇──◇────────────────────────────────◇──◇─◇
Practice your skills by brutally butchering iconic masterpieces in one of the few drawing games that measures accuracy pixel-by-pixel in real-time. 🫠
◇─◇──◇────────────────────────────────◇──◇─◇

Have an art degree and are thinking, "Now what?" Or can't draw, but want to laugh at bad art with friends? Well, grab a mouse, choose from 50 meticulously abstracted paintings from art history and get to work.
PARTY MODES!
• Online Multiplayer with Private Lobbies. Use a join code to play with up to 7 friends online. 🌍

• Mouse-v-Mouse Local Multiplayer!?! Have two mice laying around? No? Well tell your friends it's BYOM and go head-to-head on the same computer. 🐭🐭*

• Random Variants. Play with absurd variants like: "Tiny Brush," "Drawn and Quartered," and "Pointillism." 🛠️

PAINTING FEATURES!
• Assist Tools. Use time-limited tools to help you spot differences (e.g., pins, grids) 📍

• Real-time Feedback. See bar go up and down as you paint. 📈

• Keyboard Shortcuts. Get all hackery and use keyboard shortcuts for everything, most things, some things. ⌨️

• Pretty Music. Listen to 8+ randomly-selected fast-paced piano songs that end dramatically to keep you focused. 🎹

• Wacom Tablet Support. Use a tablet... or two... at once! ✍️

SINGLE PLAYER MODE!
• Masterpiece Catalog. Track forgery scores and stats across a catalog of 50 masterpieces. 🗃️

• Painting Archive.
