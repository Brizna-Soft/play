EscapeFromSeoul

Juego de PC · Indie · Indie

Explore, fight, and extract from a Seoul built from text and symbols. A real-time, top-down PvE extraction shooter for solo play or online co-op with up to 3 players. Scavenge gear, customize weapons, complete contracts, and upgrade your hideout.

Tráiler

De qué trata

Explore Seoul. Gear up. Get out alive.
EscapeFromSeoul is a real-time, top-down PvE extraction shooter set in a Seoul built from text and symbols. Explore its streets and wilderness alone or with friends, engage enemies, and gather new gear and supplies for your contracts. The loot you bring home helps develop your character and hideout, preparing you for the next area.
Explore the streets and outskirts of Seoul
From Myeong-dong and Yongsan to Namsan and Guro, head into areas with different terrain and enemies. Cross streets, explore forests and the grounds around facilities, collect supplies, and carry out jobs for traders.
Finishing an objective does not have to end your raid. Search for a few more parts, pick through the gear enemies leave behind, or extract with what you have. The choice is yours.
Combat that does not wait for your turn
The battlefield may be made of characters, but movement, gunfire, and enemy attacks all happen in real time.
Listen for footsteps and watch how enemies move. Keep track of your ammunition, reload during firefights, and find a moment to treat your wounds. When the shooting stops, the danger may still be there.
If an enemy is too much to handle, you can fall back. You do not need to win every fight to make it out alive.
Find the gear for your next raid
A weapon taken from an enemy, an attachment you have been looking for, the last supply needed for a contract. There are plenty of uses for the loot you find in the field.
Fit attachments to your guns and build a loadout around your weapons and ammunition. Keep what you need and sell the rest to traders to fund your next raid. With better equipment, you can return to areas that once forced you to retreat.
