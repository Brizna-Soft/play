De qué trata

∞ "Amnea 28" is a visual novel about a group of young people who go to attend a course of lectures by a respected professor of psychology. Their destination is "Amnea", an old hotel which ceased to fulfill its main functions ten years ago and now serves as the professor's residence. A week of studying didn't promise any problems, yet the atmosphere felt more unsettling with each passing day.
∞ Why did the students' behavior suddenly become strange? What are they hiding? The protagonist will have to solve all the mysteries of "Amnea", otherwise the walls of the old hotel won't let her leave alive.

∞ 30-50 hours of gameplay
∞ 5 unique routes
∞ 38 different endings and 19 unlockable epilogues
∞ more than 100 CGs and 38 music tracks
∞ the character routes are NOT romantic (the protagonist can't romance anyone); however there are certain romantic subplots in the story
∞ the choices you make shape up the character's mental state, the changes can be observed via a special stress scale. Don't let stress destroy the poor thing's mind, or their not-so-great qualities will manifest.
