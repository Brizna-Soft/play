No Signal in the Sewer

Juego de PC · Acción, Acceso anticipado · Acción

A guy staring at his phone falls into an open manhole. Armed with nothing but a flashlight, fight your way through dark sewers full of rats, bats and slimes, collect junk flushed down the drain to grow stronger and face the Stone Golem in this pixel art action roguelike.

Dónde comprarlo

De qué trata

A guy stares at his phone, misses an open manhole and falls right in. His phone lands in the sewage, the lid closes above him, and the only thing he finds is an old flashlight. From now on, it's both his light source and his weapon.Fight Your Way Up
To get out of this hole, you have to brave the pitch-dark sewers. Your flashlight shows you the way, but outside its beam you only see the glowing eyes of whatever is watching you. Rats, bats, slimes and other dwellers of the deep stand in your way, and at the end waits the Stone Golem, who does not like visitors at all. If you die, you start over from the beginning with nothing but your flashlight.Everything That Went Down the Drain
Along the way, you'll find items that someone flushed down at some point. Each one makes you stronger in some way, even if it's just a roll of toilet paper.The Cleaning Service
The cleaning service pays you coins for every enemy you defeat. With those coins, you can upgrade your gear to make your trips through the sewers easier. Best of all, you keep your upgrades even when you die.Features
• Dark sewers lit only by your flashlight and a few flickering torches

• Rooms, enemies and items shuffled in every run

• Rats, bats and slimes, each with their own attacks

• Boss fight against the Stone Golem

• Permanent upgrades from the cleaning service

• Pixel art
