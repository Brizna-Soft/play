De qué trata

48 Games. Countless Challenges. One Mouse.
Why settle for one game when you can play 48?

The Great 48 is a collection of 48 handcrafted, hand-drawn micro-games, each offering its own unique challenge. Test your reflexes, sharpen your timing, think quickly, and adapt to constantly changing gameplay. From quick reactions to precision challenges, there's always something new to master!

Play Your Way
Jump into any of the 48 games individually and work toward mastering each one, or mix things up with randomized selections that keep every play fresh.
Whether you have five minutes or an entire afternoon, there's always time for another challenge.

• Games are shuffled and increase in difficulty over time

• 3 lives to get as far as you can!

• Play any single game where it gets harder based on your score

• Each game has unique modifiers not found in Standard mode!

• Play a special selection of games that only end when you lose

• Difficulty increases over time!

...and one more hidden game mode!

Chase the High Scores
Every game is an opportunity to improve. Push for higher scores, beat your personal bests, and work your way toward mastering the entire collection.
Unlock achievements along the way and see just how far your skills can take you!

Can You Conquer All 48 achievements?
Easy to pick up, challenging to master, and packed with variety. Grab your mouse and find out if you have what it takes to conquer The Great 48!
