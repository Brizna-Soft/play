De qué trata

A hundred years ago, a meteorite tore open the sky and fell upon the continent. It brought this world an energy source generations ahead of its time — draconite — and with it, raised the curtain on a new era...

Draconite takes place on the hundredth anniversary of the meteorite's arrival. The Sixth Draconite Technology Summit is about to convene, and you'll attend as a member of the Draconite Research Institute's Joint Security Force, charged with maintaining order and safety for the duration of the event. Beneath that orderly surface, however, undercurrents are quietly stirring.

A side-scrolling adventure game built around exploration and storytelling, Draconite lets you move freely through the Institute, explore at your own pace, talk with the people who work there, and advance the story through patrols, investigations, and assignments — gradually uncovering the crisis hidden within.

As events unfold, you'll grow over the course of the summit, encounter feelings you won't forget, and step closer to the untold story behind the Draconite Era.

Rich Environmental Interaction
Every corner of the Institute is worth a second look. Interact with devices, documents, objects, and people to learn how the place runs day to day — and to notice the anomalies buried in the details.

Some interactions turn up extra information, open new conversations, or shape how later events play out. Stay observant, explore on your own initiative, and you may find a crucial lead tucked away somewhere perfectly ordinary.

Exploration, Investigation, and Quest Guidance
As a member of the Joint Security Force, you'll patrol the Institute, speak with its personnel, take on requests, and carry your investigation from one area to the next.
