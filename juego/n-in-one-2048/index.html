De qué trata

You came for a quick break; your brain clocked in for a shift.
N in One: Puzzle Minigame Collection brings twenty minigames together in one cozy little space. Match tiles, merge numbers, push boxes, explore mazes, spot differences—or try keeping up with cake orders and hungry customers.
Pick something relaxing, or give yourself a challenge. Your next game is just a few clicks away.
Twenty ways to play
• Match, clear and merge: Tile Connect, Bubble Pop, Marble Trail, Falling Blocks, Gem Match, Star Groups and 2048.
• Observe and solve: Spot Differences, Colour Code, Pipe Link, Box Push and Cat Trap.
• Plan your route: Maze, Ball Enclosure, Claim the Field, Magic Tower and Room Escape.
• Keep things moving: Cake Workshop, Restaurant and Ten Drops.
Four difficulty levels for every game
Learn the rules at a comfortable pace or take on a tougher challenge. Every minigame includes four difficulty levels and illustrated instructions to help you get started.
Take a break from your break
Your progress saves automatically on your computer. Pause, return to the lobby, or save and quit, then come back to continue later. Play a quick round or settle in with a puzzle that needs a little more thought.
Make yourself comfortable
Choose a color theme, adjust music, sound effects and optional short Chinese voice phrases separately, or turn off gameplay animations.
Offline and ready to play
Enjoy single-player gameplay without an internet connection, in-game ads or additional in-game purchases. The interface supports eight languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish.
Which game should you try first? That puzzle is entirely yours.
