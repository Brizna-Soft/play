Amazing Frog?

Amazing Frog? is an openworld, sandbox, physics game. Grand Frog Auto in Swindon, with farting ragdoll frogs.

De qué trata

The original open-world, ragdoll sandbox physics extravaganza!

Grand Frog Auto, Swindon (with farting ragdoll frogs)

Use bonkers physics to throw ridiculous, farting ragdoll frogs all around town and beyond the city walls.

Its a living world full of wonder

Its a Big Big world out there, with lots to discover, but you don't have to do it alone!

Work together to unlock things faster!

1-4 players Local split-screen co-op
Couch Co-op for days
Play co-op or combat or just do your own thing - its up to you!

Film each other doing crazy stunts

Don’t forget to use a vacuum for faster bulk collection!

Different items unlock different prizes and costumes at different locations
Collect everything from berries, apples, oranges, acorns, zombie flesh, jam jars, ammo, bombs, money, skulls, fish or dead pigeons, live pigeons, live penguins, cockroaches, crabs, jelly fish, dried crispy flies, rabbits, bats, rubber ducks, trophies, cats, sweets...

Collect EVERYTHING!  too many items to list!
You never know what might come in handy.

There are lots of ways to earn cash around Swindon Shire

Work in Gardening, Rubbish Collection, Crazy Taxi Missions, Ice Cream Vendor, Vermin Exterminator, Or Kill the RICH! and take their money.
Earn your cash how you choose! Look out for other Jobs around Swindon.

If it fits, it can flush!
Try Flushing everything to discover the transformative effects of the Magical Mystery Toilet. Repeat flushes can intensify the magic ( just like in real life ).
