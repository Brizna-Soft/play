Labeled as a “troublemaker”, you are transferred to the worst school imaginable.
In a place filled with rumors, pressure, and hidden intentions,
getting close to someone may be the most dangerous choice you can make.

■ Baek Yura
“Not yet… If I make a move now, you might get bored of me too quickly. So I’ll hold myself back a little longer.”
Baek Yura is the school’s dazzling celebrity and a glamorous idol trainee, constantly surrounded by everyone’s attention.
With her perfect smile and effortless social skills, she has always been able to captivate anyone with ease. Yet somehow, you alone fail to notice the provocative sincerity hidden behind her dazzling facade.
“You’re the only one who’s ever treated me this indifferently.”
As she watches you, someone who seems to drift apart from the ordinary world, she carefully maintains a calculated distance—until, little by little, she finds herself becoming captivated by the one exception: you.
“Don’t get the wrong idea. This isn’t just simple curiosity… It’s a perfectly calculated plan to make you completely mine.”
Caught somewhere between possessiveness and fascination, she finally casts aside her sweet disguise and begins moving toward you—and only you—with intentions that are as secretive as they are dangerous.

■ Ha Ian
“I like you. But I’m not asking you to accept my feelings right away.”
At a seaside bus stop in the early hours of dawn, a mysterious girl named Haian offers a gentle warmth amid the sound of crashing waves.
