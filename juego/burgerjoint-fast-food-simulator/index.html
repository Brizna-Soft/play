BurgerJoint: Fast Food Simulator

Juego de PC · Casual, Indie, Simuladores · Indie, Simulación

A fast food simulator game focused on physics-based mechanics and satisfying cooking experience. Flip, stack, grill and assemble burgers however you like, manage your restaurant’s stock, take orders, and enjoy the running of your own virtual kitchen.

De qué trata

🍔 Physics-Based Assembly
Forget "a few clicks to make a burger." In this game, assembly is an interactive process. Experience a tactile approach to the kitchen where you manually grab, stack, and layer every ingredient using physics.
• Build it Your Way: Use physics to manually pick up buns, toppings, and patties to assemble your order.

• Total Freedom: You can stack ingredients with surgical precision or throw them together in a completely sloppy manner.

🍱 Master the Menu
With over 15+ predefined burgers, various drinks and dipping sauces, there’s never a dull moment.
• The Grill Station: Grill your meat to perfection. You can grill from both the top and bottom sides for that even cook but keep a close eye on the grill. There's a fine line between "well-done" and "charcoal."

• Drinks & Dips: Complete the meal with the right beverages and side sauces to keep the orders moving.

💰 Front Desk & Cash Register
Being a chef is only half the battle. You’ll need to manage the Cash Register Flow to keep the business running:
• Take Orders: Use the cash register UI to manually take down what the customer wants.

• Handle Payments: Calculate change for cash transactions or enter card details manually.

🔥 Keep the Kitchen Moving
• Stock Management: Keep an eye on your supplies so you don't run out of ingredients mid-rush.

• Tray Cleaning: Keep the workflow steady by putting dirty trays in the sink and running the faucet to clear them for the next customer.

• Customer Patience: If you take too long or get the orders wrong, customers will get angry and leave. Speed and accuracy are your best friends!
