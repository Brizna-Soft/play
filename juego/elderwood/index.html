Elderwood

Juego de PC · Acción, Casual, Multijugador masivo · Acción, Rol (RPG)

Forge your path in Elderwood, an online fantasy RPG where skills grow through adventure. Master weapons, craft powerful gear, or support allies with bard songs. Conquer clan keeps, brave dangerous PvP regions, and face endgame raids in a world shaped by rivalry, risk, and discovery.

De qué trata

Your first catch. Your first legendary craft. Your first battle alongside a clan.
In Elderwood, small beginnings lead to lasting ambitions. Explore a persistent 3D fantasy world, develop your skills, and find your place as a crafter, fighter, healer, or companion to the dead.

Your equipment opens new possibilities
Equip a sword to fight as a Warrior, take up a bow as a Ranger, or wield a staff to cast Wizard spells. Support allies through Bard songs, restore health and resurrect fallen party members as a Cleric, or command a skeleton through Necromancy.
You aren’t locked into one role. Train different skills, change your equipment, and discover how you prefer to play.

Build your character through what you do
Train skills toward level 100. Cut wood, mine ore, fish, gather herbs, cook meals, and brew potions. Craft equipment with different rarities and random bonuses, then prepare for the challenges beyond town.
Ride into the wilderness, discover new training grounds, and turn the resources you collect into useful supplies and valuable gear.

Make your decisions count
Automatic attacks keep combat moving while you choose your abilities, equipment, food, and potions. Watch your target, manage threat, protect your allies, and use your tools at the right moment.
Organize a party through the group finder, tackle bosses and raids, or bring complementary roles together to face tougher encounters.

Make your clan a force in the world
Form a clan, rally your allies, and battle for capturable keeps. Break through enemy gates, repair defenses with collected materials, and defend the walls with arrows and magic.
Owning a keep provides access to exclusive merchants and benefits—but another clan can take it from you.
